**Key points:** Check immigration space conditions, employment-law dormitory standards, building and fire compliance, and landlord consent separately. A bedroom and living room may have different measurement scopes; 7.5 m² per person alone does not qualify accommodation for both programmes.

For receiving organisations and landlords, this article concerns layouts, floors and contracts. See [the existing dormitory employment-law article](/en/labor/column/gaikokujin-kishukusha-kisoku-todokede-roumu) for rules and plan notifications.

## How should 4.5 m², 7.5 m² and 2.5 m² be read?

| System | Position of the figure | Other checks |
|---|---|---|
| Technical Intern Training guidance | Bedroom: at least 4.5 m² per sleeper | Exclude alcoves and closets as specified; storage, daylight and heating |
| Specified Skilled Worker (i) support | Guidance generally specifies living space of at least 7.5 m² per person | Measurement scope, occupants and current exceptions |
| First-class employer dormitory rules | Bedroom: at least 2.5 m² per person; up to 16 in a room | Applicability of this class and any separate regulation |

Do not simply select the largest figure. Check each measurement scope, occupancy, storage, windows, equipment and safety condition. Plans should distinguish bedrooms and living areas and show dimensions, calculations and occupants.

First-class employer dormitory rules have applicability conditions involving stays of six months or more and related distinctions. Accommodation attached to construction projects with a scheduled completion is subject to separate Construction Industry Employer Dormitory Regulations (建設業附属寄宿舎規程). The table is not universal for construction sites.

For continuing accommodation after Technical Intern Training, the complete current Specified Skilled Worker exception conditions—such as the person's wishes, transition and continued use of the same residence—have not been verified. This article does not say 4.5 m² always suffices. The cited ISA PDF was inaccessible; all current support conditions remain unverified. The immigration professional must also recheck the latest Technical Intern Training PDF edition and calculations.

## How should stairs, floors and fire protection be checked?

Article 11 of the Employer Dormitory Regulations (事業附属寄宿舎規程) distinguishes fewer than 15 from at least 15 workers regularly living in bedrooms on the second floor or above. Do not use only the whole building's capacity. Check evacuation exceptions and the separate Technical Intern Training conditions. Article 9 restricts basement bedrooms and generally bedrooms on the third floor or above, with specified structural exceptions for the latter.

Ask the fire authority about classification as a dormitory or apartment building, area, windowless floors, mixed uses and existing installations. An ordinary apartment listing does not establish suitability as a dormitory. Architects and authorities check zoning, district plans, special-building change-of-use conditions including the relevant area over 200 m², exceptions and requirements even where confirmation is unnecessary.

## Are dormitory rules and installation plans the same notification?

| Procedure | Basis and conditions |
|---|---|
| Dormitory rules | Labour Standards Act (労働基準法), Article 95; Regulations Article 1-2. Consent of a representative of a majority of residents is required for specified matters |
| Installation, relocation or alteration plan | Act Article 96-2; Regulations Article 3-2 and Form 1. Check the covered undertaking and works; notify at least 14 days before starting works |

Consent to specified dormitory rules is different from obtaining opinions on work rules. The 10-worker condition for plans concerns workers employed in the undertaking, not 10 dormitory residents. A social insurance and labour consultant checks dangerous or harmful undertakings and other applicability conditions.

## How should a leased company residence and programme transition be handled?

When the organisation is the tenant, employees' use may be by a loan or sublease arrangement depending on the contract; they are not invariably subtenants. Clarify dormitory use, occupants, necessary consent, reinstatement and move-out settlement, distinguishing agreed clauses from statutory explanations. Consult a labour professional on payroll deductions, labour-management agreements and charges, and a lawyer on disputes.

By October 2026, Employment for Skill Development materials and pre-commencement applications have been published. Plans spanning the new system must check published guidance and transition rules. Do not infer its housing figures from the old system. ## Frequently asked questions

**Q. Does 7.5 m² per person qualify any foreign-worker dormitory?**
A. No. Check each system's measurement scope, occupancy, equipment, safety, support conditions and employment-law standards.

**Q. Is 4.5 m² always enough for continuing company housing?**
A. No universal exception is stated. Check eligible people, their wishes, transition and residence conditions in current official guidance.

**Q. Is Form 1 under Article 96-2 used for dormitory rules?**
A. Article 95 rules and Article 96-2 installation plans are separate. Specified rules also require consent from the residents' majority representative.

**Q. Is every company-housing resident a subtenant?**
A. The loan or sublease relationship depends on the contract. Check use, occupancy and necessary landlord consent.
