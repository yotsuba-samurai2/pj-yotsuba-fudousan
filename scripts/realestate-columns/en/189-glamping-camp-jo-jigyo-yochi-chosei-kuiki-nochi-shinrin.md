**In short:** Land for glamping or a campground can look like the same meadow yet require entirely different checks before purchase, depending on whether it is in an urbanization control area (市街化調整区域), registered as farmland, or privately owned forest under a regional forest plan. Whether you only pitch tents, or build a cabin, manager's hut or trailer house that counts as a building, also moves the answer.

Before securing the land, confirm these three layers — control area, land category, forest. A real estate transaction specialist handles the entry question of whether you can acquire the site; each permit goes to a separate professional. For [commercial property consultations](https://luck428.com/en/toushi), start with how to check a candidate site's zoning, land category and road access.

## Are glamping and campground structures buildings or工作物?

This is the first fork. Article 2(1) of the Building Standards Act defines a building as a structure fixed to land that has a roof together with pillars or walls, among other things. Permanent cabins, manager's huts, cooking shelters and sanitary blocks are usually buildings and may need building confirmation.

A trailer house that, judged by its scale, form and installation, can be **moved freely at any time** is not treated as a building, under national technical guidance (Notice 住指発 No. 419 of 1 December 1987 and Notice 住指発 No. 170 of 31 March 1997). Fixed water and electrical connections, or steps, porches and decks that obstruct movement, can make it a building. For a container fixed to the land, Notice 国住指 No. 2174 of 6 December 2004 is the reference.

Tents and tarps that place no fixed building on the land usually need no building confirmation. But as below, whether you put up a building directly affects whether development permission is required. Whether a trailer house truly "can be moved" depends on how it is installed; confirm the final treatment with the local building authority using drawings and an installation plan.

## Is development permission needed to open in an urbanization control area?

A "development act" under the City Planning Act means **a change to the division or character of land for the purpose of building, or of constructing a specified工作物** (Art. 4(12)). So if you build neither a building nor a specified工作物, changing the land may not amount to a development act by definition.

Among specified工作物, a type-2 specified工作物 is a golf course, or a sports, leisure or similar facility or cemetery of **one hectare or more**, such as a baseball ground, tennis court or amusement park (Art. 4(11); Enforcement Order Art. 1(2)). Some municipal guides treat a campground, as a recreation facility in harmony with natural land use, as not a type-2 specified工作物 unless it involves large-scale earthworks. Treatment varies by municipality, so confirm with the authority, showing the area and the scale of earthworks.

| Form of opening | Main issue in an urbanization control area |
|---|---|
| Tents only, no building or specified工作物 | May not be a development act; confirm individually against the change of division or character |
| A manager's hut, cabins or other buildings | Development act for the purpose of building: development permission (Art. 29) and location tests under Art. 34 |
| Developed as a sports/leisure facility of one hectare or more | May be a type-2 specified工作物 subject to development permission |

To obtain development permission in an urbanization control area, the project must generally fall within one of the items of Article 34. Building anew outside an area with development permission also involves the Article 43 permit. Applying for development permission (Art. 29) is administrative-scrivener work; the real estate specialist does not decide whether it will be granted. The flow is explained in [development permission under City Planning Act Article 29](https://luck428.com/en/legal/column/kaihatsu-kyoka-toshikeikakuho-29jo-nagare).

## If you build on farmland, which farmland-conversion category applies?

If the registered category or actual state is farmland, the Cropland Act applies on top. Converting your own farmland to a campsite yourself engages Article 4; acquiring rights for the purpose of converting engages Article 5 — each generally requires permission from the prefectural governor or an equivalent authority. Moving rights while keeping the land as farmland is Article 3. Certain conversions in an urbanization promotion area are handled by prior notice to the agricultural committee instead (Arts. 4(1)(vii) and 5(1)(vi)).

Glamping and campgrounds can amount to "conversion" of farmland to non-farm use, with or without a building. Farmland in an agricultural-use zone (under the Act on Establishment of Agricultural Promotion Areas) is a higher hurdle, generally needing a prior exclusion procedure. Fitting the right category and preparing the application is administrative-scrivener work; [the distinction between Articles 4 and 5](https://luck428.com/en/legal/column/nochi-tenyo-4jo-5jo-kyoka-nagare) sets out the flow. The final decision on whether farmland can be converted rests with the agricultural committee and the prefecture.

## From what size does forest-land development permission apply when clearing woodland?

If the site is privately owned forest under a regional forest plan, the forest-land development permission under Article 10-2 of the Forest Act is engaged. The size that triggers permission is set by the Forest Act Enforcement Order and is, as a rule, development of **more than one hectare**. For development to install solar power facilities, the threshold was lowered to more than 0.5 hectare from 1 April 2023; that figure is for solar, while clearing for glamping or a campground falls under the one-hectare rule.

Below the permission threshold, some prefectures run a small-scale forest-development notification by ordinance for roughly 0.3 to 1 hectare (0.3 to 0.5 for solar), requiring notice to the governor before work (for example 90 to 30 days ahead). If the land is a protection forest (保安林), separate release or work-permit questions arise. Because the figures and notification rules differ by area at the time of writing, confirm the area, any protection-forest designation and the notification requirement with the prefectural forestry division. Applying for forest-land development permission is also administrative-scrivener work.

## What should you confirm in writing before acquiring the site?

A verbal "it's fine" is not enough. Gather the following in writing before deciding to acquire. They stack, so one missing item can undo the whole plan.

| Item to confirm | Where to check |
|---|---|
| Area division (urbanization control area, etc.) and use district | Municipal city-planning desk; development-permission need at the development guidance section |
| Registered category / whether it is farmland or in an agricultural-use zone | Legal Affairs Bureau (register); agricultural committee or municipal agricultural-promotion desk |
| Whether it is private forest under a regional forest plan / a protection forest | Prefectural forestry division |
| Whether lodging is offered and whether a business permit applies | Public health center (hotel business / simple lodging); food hygiene if meals are served |
| Road access, water, drainage, electricity connections | Road administrator; water and sewerage; power company |

A business that lodges people for a fee is a hotel business, so offering overnight stays at a glampsite raises a simple-lodging permit (Hotel Business Act Arts. 2 and 3). These permits and notifications proceed under separate engagements with independent providers. Applications for development, farmland conversion, forest-land development and the hotel business go to the administrative scrivener (Yotsuba Administrative Scrivener Office); registration of title to a judicial scrivener; business and transfer tax to a tax accountant; and boundary or contract disputes to a lawyer — each contracted directly. There are no referral fees among them. The real estate specialist organizes the entry question of whether the site can be acquired, on the premise that these permits overlap.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q. If it is a tents-only campground, do I need no permit at all in a control area?**
A. If you build no building and no specified工作物, it may not be a development act, but the content of any change to the land, and the Cropland Act or Forest Act if the land is farmland or private forest, apply separately. Confirm the area division, land category and forest-plan status with the authority before deciding.

**Q. I heard that placing a trailer house means no building confirmation is needed.**
A. A unit judged able to be moved freely at any time is not treated as a building, but fixed piping or an attached deck can make it one. Show the installation plan and drawings to the local building authority to confirm.

**Q. Does forest-land development permission apply even to clearing a small part of woodland?**
A. In private forest under a regional forest plan, development of more than one hectare is, as a rule, subject to permission. Below that, some areas have a notification system, and a protection forest needs a separate procedure. Confirm the area and designation status with the prefectural forestry division.

**Q. Who grants the permits, and who can prepare the paperwork?**
A. Development permission is decided by the prefectural governor or equivalent, farmland conversion generally by the prefectural governor or equivalent, forest-land development by the prefectural governor, and the hotel business by the head of the local authority with a public health center. An administrative scrivener can prepare the applications but does not decide whether permission is granted.
