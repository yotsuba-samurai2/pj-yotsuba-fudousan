**Key points:** To list an inherited vacant house on an akiya bank and sell it, first sort out the division of roles between the bank and the brokerage, whether inheritance registration is done, and any farmland or unregistered outbuildings. The earlier you move—before a "poorly managed vacant house" designation—the wider your options; tax changes with whether a capital-gains special exemption applies, so organise it early.

This is for heirs of a rural family home (a vacant house) considering sale or use via an akiya bank. For demolish-or-as-is, see [how to sell a vacant house](https://luck428.com/en/column/souzoku-akiya-kaitai-koyatsuki-dochira); for the inheritance-registration picture, see [registration and sale of an inherited home](https://luck428.com/en/column/souzoku-toki-gimuka-baikyaku).

## How do an akiya bank and a brokerage divide the work?

An akiya bank is a municipal system that collects and publishes vacant-house information. The Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism's "nationwide akiya/vacant-land bank" lets you search across municipalities. The owner applies to register on the municipality's form; the published information centres on the property's location and outline.

| Who | Main role |
|---|---|
| Akiya bank (municipality) | Registering and publishing vacant-house information; the entry point for matching with interested users |
| Real estate broker (us) | Price assessment, statutory explanation, brokering the sale under a brokerage contract |

The bank is a place for publication and matching; statutory explanation and brokerage for a sale are handled by a licensed broker. Some municipalities run a model where a broker affiliated with the bank handles the brokerage, so bank registration and a brokerage contract typically coexist. Registration eligibility and conditions differ by municipality—confirm with the municipality of the address.

## Can a vacant house without completed inheritance registration be registered on the bank?

Inheritance registration (mandatory from April 1, 2024; Real Property Registration Act Article 76-2) must be applied for within three years of learning you acquired the property by inheritance. Property inherited before the start date is also covered, with application sufficient by March 31, 2027; neglect without a valid reason can incur a non-penal fine.

- As a premise for bank registration and sale, basically complete the inheritance registration that moves title to the heir
- If estate division is not settled, an heir-reporting registration can provisionally satisfy the duty, but it is not a transfer of ownership, so a further registration is needed after division is settled
- Registration is a judicial scrivener's work. If multiple heirs dispute shares, consult a lawyer

If title remains in the deceased's name, transfer to the buyer cannot proceed. Check the registration status at the bank-registration stage.

## With farmland or an unregistered outbuilding attached to the main house, what do you settle first?

The home's site may include farmland or an unregistered building (a detached room, a storehouse). Confirm these in order before selling.

| Attached item | What to confirm first |
|---|---|
| Farmland | Whether permission/notification under the Farmland Act (Art. 3 = rights transfer, Art. 4 = self-conversion, Art. 5 = conversion with rights transfer) is needed. Notification may suffice inside an urbanization-promotion area |
| Unregistered building | Whether title-description registration (land and house investigator) is needed, reflecting the building's location, structure and floor area |

Permit applications for farmland sale or conversion are administrative-scrivener work; title-description registration of an unregistered building is a land and house investigator's; ownership registration is a judicial scrivener's. For farmland see [selling or leasing inherited farmland](https://luck428.com/en/column/souzoku-nochi-baikyaku-kashidashi-nagare); for unregistered buildings see [title-description registration and sale of an unregistered building](https://luck428.com/en/column/souzoku-mitouki-tatemono-hyodai-touki-baikyaku). The order of steps changes with what is attached, so inventory it first using the registration certificate, the official map and the current condition.

## To sell before a "poorly managed vacant house" designation, where do you start?

The Act on Special Measures for Promoting Vacant House Measures was amended in 2023 to let municipalities guide and recommend, as a "poorly managed vacant house," a house that if left would likely become a "specified vacant house" (effective December 13, 2023). On recommendation, the land loses the residential-land special measure for fixed-asset tax, and the tax burden can rise (for fiscal years with an assessment date of January 1, 2024 onward).

- The earlier you decide on sale or use before a recommendation, the more options you have
- First advance current-condition checks, assessment, and the title (inheritance registration) status in parallel
- We handle the on-site check, assessment and brokerage preparation. Read this alongside the perspective in [how to sell a vacant house](https://luck428.com/en/column/souzoku-akiya-kaitai-koyatsuki-dochira)

Recommendations are at the municipality's discretion. Confirm the designation status and outlook with the municipality of the address, and move early.

## How does tax change for a sale via an akiya bank?

Sale gains (capital gains) are subject to income and resident tax, and for an inherited vacant house a capital-gains special exemption (the so-called ¥30 million vacant-house special exemption; Act on Special Measures Concerning Taxation, Article 35) may apply under conditions. Application has detailed requirements such as the building's seismic standard or demolition; whether it applies and the tax estimate are a tax accountant's work.

- The capital-gains calculation itself does not change depending on whether it is via the bank
- Whether the exemption's requirements (timing of inheritance, building condition, sale deadline) are met is an individual judgment
- Tax calculation and filing are a tax accountant's; ownership-transfer registration is a judicial scrivener's

Tax is a qualified professional's domain. We handle the sale's arrangements and brokerage; confirm the final tax judgment with a tax accountant.

Price assessment, statutory explanation and brokerage are ours. Inheritance registration and title-description registration of an unregistered building go to a judicial scrivener and a land and house investigator; permit applications for the sale or conversion of farmland on the site to an administrative scrivener; judgment on capital gains and the vacant-house special exemption to a tax accountant; unsettled division among heirs to a lawyer. ## Frequently asked questions

**Q. Can we register on the akiya bank without doing inheritance registration?**
A. Registration itself depends on the municipality's practice, but a sale presumes inheritance registration moving title to the heir. Registration is a judicial scrivener's work.

**Q. If we list on the akiya bank, can we sell without a real estate company?**
A. The bank is an entry point for publication and matching. Statutory explanation and brokerage are handled by a licensed broker, so the sale's practical work is separately needed.

**Q. A small field is attached to the site. Can we sell it as is?**
A. If farmland is included, confirm first whether Farmland Act permission/notification is needed. Permit applications are an administrative scrivener's work; notification may suffice inside an urbanization-promotion area.

**Q. What changes if it is designated a poorly managed vacant house?**
A. On recommendation, the land loses the residential-land special measure for fixed-asset tax, and the burden can rise. Advance assessment and planning before designation.
