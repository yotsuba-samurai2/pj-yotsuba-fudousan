**Key points:** For an authorised nursery with at least 20 places, check age-based space, outdoor play areas and floor-specific evacuation and fire requirements. National rules differ between the second floor and higher floors; Tokyo's current standards and municipal pre-consultation conditions must also be checked before signing.

This article concerns property selection in Bunkyo and nearby areas. It distinguishes [small-scale childcare premises](/en/column/hoiku-shoukibo-bukken-youto-chiiki) and leaves [staffing and employment matters](/en/labor/column/hoikuen-kaisetsu-roumu-haichi-kijun-shogu) to the relevant article.

## How do national space figures relate to Tokyo's rules?

The national Standards for Equipment and Operation of Child Welfare Facilities (児童福祉施設の設備及び運営に関する基準), Article 32, provide the following figures. For children under two, the provision refers to an infant room or crawling room; it is not a simple requirement to provide both.

| Room or area | National minimum per child | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| Infant room | 1.65 m² | Article 32, item 2 |
| Crawling room | 3.3 m² | Item 3 |
| Nursery or play room, age two and over | 1.98 m² | Item 6 |
| Outdoor play area, age two and over | 3.3 m² | Item 6 |

These are national figures. Tokyo's ordinances, establishment standards, additions and calculation methods, and the ward's conditions must be checked. The relevant current Tokyo material has not been fully verified here. Do not set capacity from the 1.65 m² figure alone. Take the age-based capacity and a plan showing room uses to pre-consultation.

## Does small-scale childcare dispense with an outdoor play area?

Not necessarily. Small-scale types A and B and other relevant categories also have outdoor-area requirements depending on the children's ages. This is not an obligation that starts merely at 20 places. Compare the service type, ages and scale.

For authorised nurseries, Article 32, item 5, includes nearby substitute places. A nearby park is not automatically acceptable: check area, safe routes, dependable everyday access and any manager's consent. No nationwide distance is stated here; confirm Tokyo and ward practice.

## What differs between the second floor and higher floors?

| Floor containing childcare rooms | Article 32, item 8 |
|---|---|
| Second floor | Sub-items イ, ロ and ヘ: structure, facilities for ordinary use and evacuation, and fall prevention |
| Third floor and above | All requirements in item 8, including travel distance, kitchen fire protection, interior finish, alarms, reporting and flame resistance |

The structure must be fire-resistant or a specified quasi-fire-resistant type on the second floor, and fire-resistant on higher floors. Some quasi-fire-resistant categories are excluded. Sub-item ロ requires facilities in each of the ordinary-use and evacuation categories, according to the floor. One facility from an undifferentiated list is not enough. Specified indoor stairs can be options; an architect assesses existing stairs.

Do not state sub-item ハ's 30 m travel distance as a universal second-floor requirement under this provision. Distinguish the kitchen's default compartmentation requirement from exclusions involving specified automatic extinguishing arrangements; both are not invariably required together. Separately check additional building, fire and local rules.

## How should property, authorisation and staffing be coordinated?

An allowed zoning use does not remove district-plan, ordinance or existing-building constraints. Check the conditions for confirmation of conversion to a special building, including the relevant area over 200 m² and similar-use exceptions. Building requirements remain even without a confirmation application.

Plan premises, staffing, operations, finances and employment arrangements in parallel before applying. Consult the municipality first. Under the Child Welfare Act (児童福祉法), Article 35(4), authorisation of private nurseries is generally by the prefectural governor, with exceptions for competent cities and other authorities. Check the Tokyo/ward division for Bunkyo.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q. Is an outdoor play area unnecessary for small-scale childcare?**
A. Not universally. Check the service category and ages, and each applicable outdoor or substitute-area requirement.

**Q. Can capacity in Tokyo be calculated using 1.65 m² per infant?**
A. Not from the national figure alone. Check current Tokyo space and calculation standards and the ward's conditions.

**Q. Does every second-floor facility need the 30 m and kitchen rules under item 8?**
A. Item 8 applies イ, ロ and ヘ on the second floor and all its requirements on higher floors. Check additional local and other legal requirements separately.

**Q. Who examines authorisation and changes descriptive building registration?**
A. The competent authority examines the application, supported by an administrative scrivener. Land and house investigators handle descriptive registration; judicial scriveners handle rights registration.
