**Before signing:** Put the use of every floor, the proposed inpatient beds and the equipment for each clinic on a set of plans. A clinic with no inpatient beds is not exempt from accessibility or fire safety rules. Nor do two clinics automatically make a building mixed-use. Show the same plans to the building authority, local fire department and public health centre before committing to the premises.

## What should the owner and the tenant establish first?

Under the national zoning table in Japan’s Building Standards Act (建築基準法), clinics are in principle permitted in all 13 types of use district. This does not automatically cover an independently operated pharmacy, café or office in the same building. Ask for the building approval and completion inspection records, plus drawings that show the existing and proposed use on each floor. Have an architect and the local authority check local district rules and whether the change of use requires an application, even if the last tenant was a clinic.

Under Japan’s Medical Care Act (医療法), an establishment with no inpatient facilities or room for no more than 19 inpatients is a clinic; one equipped for 20 or more is a hospital. Do not simply add up the beds of separate operators on the same site or assume the entire property is one healthcare institution. Give the public health centre a plan for each establishment.

## When does an outpatient clinic have to meet accessibility standards?

Article 5(ii) of the order under Japan’s Barrier-Free Act (バリアフリー法) lists hospitals **and clinics**, without limiting clinics to those with beds. For new construction and certain other work, the national accessibility standards generally become mandatory at 2,000 m² of relevant floor area (Act, Art. 14; Order, Art. 9). For an extension, rebuilding or a change of use, the calculation concerns the part being extended, rebuilt or converted. Neither the leased suite alone nor the gross area of the entire building necessarily gives the answer.

Tokyo sets lower thresholds for work covered by its ordinance: hospitals and inpatient clinics qualify regardless of size, while outpatient clinics qualify from 500 m² of relevant floor area. Below 500 m², separate metropolitan or municipal standards or notification requirements may still apply. Mark the step-free entrance, lift, corridors and accessible toilet on the plan and check which common areas count.

## Which fire classification applies?

Under the classifications in effect since 1 April 2016 in Appended Table 1 to Japan’s Fire Service Act Enforcement Order (消防法施行令), an outpatient clinic or a midwifery facility without accommodation is in **item (6)イ(4)**. An inpatient clinic is in (6)イ(2) if it both offers a specified specialty and has facilities for at least four inpatients; other inpatient clinics are in (6)イ(3). Hospitals are in (6)イ(1) or (3) depending on their characteristics. Midwifery facilities with accommodation are in (6)イ(3). A treatment couch alone does not establish that a facility takes inpatients.

Mixed use under item (16) depends on the actual uses, how they are managed and whether a secondary use is subordinate. Several clinic tenants alone do not establish mixed use. Ask the fire department which equipment serves the whole building and which requirements apply to individual premises, taking account of floor, area, structure and compartmentation. Agree in the lease who installs, pays for and maintains each item.

## What must each operator file?

An individual doctor or dentist who meets the applicable clinical training requirements normally files a notice within 10 days **after** opening. A medical corporation or other operator requiring permission must obtain it **before** opening and still file a notice within 10 days **after** opening. Beds may need separate authorisation, notification or permission to use. The manager reports installation of a covered diagnostic X-ray unit within 10 days. One filing by the landlord does not cover all the operators.

The Medical Care Act amendment covering these areas took effect on 1 April 2026; when a local designation applies is a separate question. If a prefecture has formally designated an area with an excess of outpatient physicians, a new outpatient medical clinic generally needs an additional filing at least six months before opening unless unavoidable circumstances apply. This advance notice does not apply to dental clinics. Check the prefecture’s published areas and effective dates rather than assuming a proposed designation is in force.

## What should each party bring to the meeting?

- **Owner or manager:** floor-by-floor use and dimensions, building records, fire equipment inspection records, electrical and drainage capacity, and who can approve alterations to common areas.
- **Tenant:** specialties, planned inpatient capacity, equipment dimensions, weight and power, X-ray room and plumbing requirements. Draw the boundary between exclusive and shared reception or waiting areas instead of assuming sharing will be permitted.
- **Both parties:** clarify fit-out costs, landlord works, deadlines and what happens if the premises do not qualify. A narrow lift or inadequate supply capacity cannot necessarily be fixed inside the suite.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q. May we sign as soon as we confirm that the zoning permits a clinic?**
A. Not on zoning alone. Check the other uses, existing approvals, fire equipment and any change-of-use procedure. Ask the owner for plans showing the use of the entire building, not just the available suite.

**Q. In Tokyo, is an outpatient clinic under 500 m² automatically exempt?**
A. No. Confirm which areas count. Even below the metropolitan building accessibility threshold, other metropolitan or municipal requirements and filings may apply.

**Q. Do two outpatient clinics in the same building automatically make it mixed-use for fire purposes?**
A. No. An outpatient clinic itself falls under (6)イ(4). Ask the local fire department to assess other independent uses, common areas, floors and compartments before deciding which equipment is required.

**Q. Can a branch of a medical corporation stop once it obtains permission to open?**
A. No. After obtaining prior permission, it must also notify the authority within 10 days of opening. Check any bed or X-ray filing and, for outpatient medical clinics, any separate advance notice required in a designated area.
