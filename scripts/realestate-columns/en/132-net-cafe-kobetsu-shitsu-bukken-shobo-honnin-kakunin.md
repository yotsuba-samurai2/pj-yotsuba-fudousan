## How far are internet-café properties restricted by zoning and floor area?

Under the Building Standards Act, an internet café or manga café is generally treated like a "store (a store for a service business)." That said, a format where booths let customers use amusement facilities may be seen as close to an "amusement facility" or "place of public entertainment," and which use category applies depends on the building's make-up and the designated administrative agency's judgment. Rather than assuming "this district can take it," check the zoning and use classification for each property.

| To check | Points to look at on the property side |
| --- | --- |
| Zoning | Whether store/amusement-type uses can be built in the district; residence-only districts such as Category I low-rise residential are heavily restricted |
| Existing use | Whether a use-change confirmation application is needed from the former use (office, retail, etc.)—the need turns on changes to a special building over 200 m² |
| Floor area | Whether the number of booths, aisles, reception and a kitchen area fit |
| Floor/position | A basement, a windowless floor or an upper floor makes escape and fire conditions heavier |

Whether a use-change confirmation application is required is governed by Article 87 of the Building Standards Act and the rules on changes to special buildings (such as over 200 m²). Judgments on zoning and building feasibility are the field of the architect and the designated administrative agency. Through lease brokerage and the important-matters explanation, we organise the property's zoning, existing use and area before contracting.

## What fire-prevention and escape conditions apply to the private-booth areas?

The heart of a complex café is the booth areas. Item (2)-ni of Appended Table 1 of the Fire Service Act Enforcement Order covers "stores engaged in the business of letting customers use, in private rooms (including similar facilities), karaoke boxes or other facilities or articles for amusement." Internet cafés and manga cafés are placed under this item by the enforcement regulation. Because customers stay in enclosed booths, a delay in escape during a fire is treated as serious.

| To check | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Automatic fire-alarm system | Item (2)-ni requires it regardless of total floor area (the area threshold was removed by the amendment in force from 1 October 2008) |
| Guide lights / escape routes | Whether the booth layout keeps aisle width and distance to exits |
| Interior-finish restriction | Whether the booth walls and ceilings meet the interior-finish restriction |
| Notification of start of use of a fire-prevention object | Before use, notify the fire department under each municipality's fire-prevention ordinance (Tokyo: by 7 days before use) |

For item (2)-ni facilities, the need for an automatic fire-alarm system even at a small scale differs greatly from an ordinary retail store or office. Where booths are retrofitted into an existing building, whether the alarm system, guide lights and compartments can be added afterwards becomes the dividing line in property selection. The design and installation of fire equipment and the notification to the fire department are the field of the fire-equipment engineer and the designated administrative agency. We confirm the property's current state (existing alarm system, floor, whether windowless) before contracting.

## Who files the Tokyo Internet Terminal Use Business (identity-verification) notification, and when?

In Tokyo, the Ordinance on the Regulation of Internet Terminal Use Business (in force from 1 July 2010) regulates businesses that set up private rooms and let customers use the internet on communication terminals. A complex café that provides even some private rooms falls under this "Internet Terminal Use Business."

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Timing | Notify, per store, by 10 days before opening (ordinance art. 3) |
| Where | Via the police station (community safety section) with jurisdiction over the store, to the Tokyo Metropolitan Public Safety Commission |
| Identity verification | A duty to ask customers to present identity documents and verify their identity particulars (ordinance art. 4) |
| Record retention | A duty to create and keep the identity-verification record and the terminal-identification record (for 3 years from the last day of service) |
| Change notification | When a notified item changes, within 10 days of the change |

Identity verification and record retention are the ordinance's central duties, aimed at preventing crimes committed under anonymity. The notification form (Appended Form 1) states the business hours and the method of creating and keeping the identity-verification record and terminal-identification record, with a resident record attached for a sole proprietor, or the articles of incorporation and a certificate of registered matters for a company.

Preparing these notification documents and organising the attachments is the work of the administrative scrivener. ## Where is the line on whether late-night operation or the booth specification touches the Entertainment Business Act / "specified entertainment"?

Complex cafés often operate late into the night, so people worry "won't this need an Entertainment Business Act permit." An ordinary internet café or manga café generally does not fall under "specified entertainment restaurant business," which serves food while providing amusement, or under "entertainment business" involving hospitality, and the Internet Terminal Use Business notification is often enough.

The line, however, shifts with what is provided in the booths. If sexual services are provided in a booth, the problem of store-type sex-related special businesses arises; if alcohol is served and amusement provided late at night, the permit for a specified entertainment restaurant business (Entertainment Business Act) becomes a point. The final legal judgment on which business category applies is the field of the lawyer and the police (public safety commission). Neither we nor an administrative scrivener can fix the business category.

What the property side can do is settle the intended format first (late-night operation or not, serving food/alcohol, booth specification) and check whether it is consistent with the building's use, fire-prevention and the ordinance. On property requirements for amusement-related uses, the points in [Confirming use and area for a game-arcade property (Entertainment Business Act category 5) (Japanese)](https://luck428.com/column/game-center-fuei-5go-bukken-youto-menseki) are a useful reference for the relationship between compartments and permits.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q. Can I turn a vacant office floor straight into an internet café?**
A. First check whether a use-change confirmation application is needed (such as a change to a special building over 200 m²) and whether an automatic fire-alarm system and escape routes can be fitted to the booth areas. An office as-is may not meet the fire or escape conditions, so fitting out the interior and equipment is a precondition. The feasibility of the use change is the field of the architect and designated administrative agency; the fire equipment, the fire-equipment engineer.

**Q. When, and by whom, is the Tokyo Internet Terminal Use Business notification filed?**
A. In Tokyo, notify per store by 10 days before opening, via the police station (community safety section) with jurisdiction over the location, to the Tokyo Metropolitan Public Safety Commission (ordinance art. 3). Preparing the notification documents is the administrative scrivener's work; we do not act as agent. On the real-estate side we organise whether the property can hold the "private rooms etc." under the ordinance.

**Q. If there are private rooms, is an automatic fire-alarm system always required?**
A. A store falling under item (2)-ni of Appended Table 1 of the Fire Service Act Enforcement Order (a private-booth type such as a karaoke box or internet café) requires an automatic fire-alarm system regardless of total floor area (the area threshold was removed by the amendment in force from 1 October 2008). Where booths are retrofitted into an existing building, whether the equipment can be installed becomes the dividing line. The design and installation are the field of the fire-equipment engineer.

**Q. Does operating late at night require an Entertainment Business Act permit?**
A. An ordinary internet café or manga café generally does not fall under specified entertainment restaurant business or entertainment business, and the Internet Terminal Use Business notification is often enough. However, serving alcohol and providing amusement late at night, or providing sexual services in a booth, raise other regulations as points. The final judgment on the business category is the field of the lawyer and the public safety commission.
