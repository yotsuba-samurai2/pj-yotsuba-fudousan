## What is seikatsu kaigo, and how does its property differ from other disability welfare services?

Seikatsu kaigo is a disability welfare service defined in Article 5(7) of the Comprehensive Support for Persons with Disabilities Act (the Act on Comprehensive Support for the Daily and Social Life of Persons with Disabilities). It is defined as providing, to persons needing constant care as specified by competent ministerial ordinance, mainly in the daytime and at a facility for persons with disabilities or the like, care such as bathing, toileting and meals, the provision of opportunities for creative or productive activity, and other conveniences. Grasping it as a "daytime-activity" service where a person with severe disabilities spends the day and gains, together with care, opportunities for activity, makes its difference from other services clear.

Even within disability welfare services, the idea of which rooms and floor area a property must have differs by service type. Seikatsu kaigo centres on care and daytime activity, so a room for work/activity, a consultation room and sanitary facilities are the basis. The governing provision and the facility-standard article differ from after-school day service, employment-continuation support and disability group homes.

| Service type | Core content | Property-side focus |
|---|---|---|
| Seikatsu kaigo (Act art. 5(7)) | Daytime care + creative/productive activity | Training/work & consultation rooms, sanitary facilities, zoning, fire class |
| After-school day service | After-school support for schoolchildren | Floor area of the guidance/training room, zoning, floor |
| Employment-continuation support A/B | Work opportunities and training | Workspace, zoning, fire code |
| Disability group home | Communal-living residence | Private/shared living facilities, zoning, fire code |

In considering a seikatsu kaigo property, applying floor area and rooms with the same feel as a child-oriented or residence-type service leads to a wrong judgment. The property requirements of after-school day service are organised in [how to check zoning and the first-floor requirement for an after-school day service property](https://luck428.com/en/column/houkago-day-bukken-youto-chiiki-1kai), employment-continuation support type A in [the property and zoning of employment-continuation support type A](https://luck428.com/en/column/shuro-keizoku-shien-a-bukken-youto-chiiki), and group homes in [the property, use and fire code of a disability group home](https://luck428.com/en/column/shogaisha-grouphome-kyodoseikatsu-enjo-bukken-youto-shobo). ## How far are the facility standards (training/work room, consultation room, washroom, toilet, multipurpose room) required?

The facility standard for seikatsu kaigo is set by Article 81 of the "Standards for Personnel, Facilities and Operation of Designated Disability Welfare Service Businesses" (2006 MHLW Ordinance No. 171). Paragraph 1 requires a designated seikatsu kaigo office to have a training/work room, a consultation room, a washroom, a toilet and a multipurpose room, and other facilities necessary for operation.

Paragraph 2 sets the specific standard for each facility as follows. The training/work room must have ample space for training or work and be equipped with the machinery necessary for training or work. The consultation room must have a partition or the like to prevent conversation within the room from leaking. The washroom and toilet must be suited to the users' characteristics. Paragraph 3 then provides that the consultation room and the multipurpose room may be shared where there is no hindrance to supporting users, and Paragraph 4 provides that these facilities are, in principle, exclusive to the office (with an exception where there is no hindrance to supporting users).

| Facility (art. 81(1)) | Standard (art. 81(2) etc.) |
|---|---|
| Training/work room | Ample space for training/work; equipped with the necessary machinery |
| Consultation room | A partition etc. to prevent conversation leaking |
| Washroom/toilet | Suited to users' characteristics |
| Multipurpose room | May be shared with the consultation room (where no hindrance) |

A frequent misunderstanding in property consideration is the treatment of a rest room and a bath. By definition seikatsu kaigo can include bathing care, but the facilities Article 81 necessarily requires are those in the table above; a rest room and a bath are not included in that mandatory list. An office that provides bathing care will, in practice, prepare bathing facilities, but that is a matter of practical provision distinct from whether it is on the mandatory list. Also, the specific square metres of floor area and its relation to capacity, and special provisions for multi-function or small-scale types, are supplemented by the designating authority's (prefecture, designated/core city) designation guidance and interpretation, so confirm the numbers with the latest guidance of the designating authority with jurisdiction over the property (we do not state unverified square-metre figures here). We do not conclusively judge facility conformity; we help, as general information, by cross-reading the facility standard and arranging confirmation with the competent authority.

## How do you check zoning and the building use (welfare facility) under the Building Standards Act?

Even if the facility rooms seem to fit, whether you may build (use) a welfare facility there is a separate matter. Here zoning and the treatment of use under the Building Standards Act take effect.

A seikatsu kaigo office counts as a "child welfare facility, etc." under Article 19(1) of the Enforcement Order of the Building Standards Act. That paragraph treats, as child welfare facilities etc., facilities used for a disability welfare service business (limited to a business providing seikatsu kaigo, independence training, employment-transition support or employment-continuation support) and the like. Whether building is allowed by zoning is checked in Appended Table 2 of the Building Standards Act. In general, child welfare facilities etc. can be built in many residential and commercial zones, but there are zones — such as an exclusive industrial zone — where the treatment of a welfare facility with living rooms becomes strict.

| Check item | Content | Where to check |
|---|---|---|
| Classification of use | A seikatsu kaigo office is a child welfare facility etc. (Enforcement Order art. 19(1)) | Specified administrative agency / building official |
| Zoning allowance | Building allowance by zone in Appended Table 2; watch exclusive industrial zones etc. | Specified administrative agency / city-planning desk |
| Use-change confirmation application | When converting an existing building to welfare use above a certain scale | Architect / specified administrative agency |

Note that the zoning determination is not decided formally by the facility's name but is judged from the substance of its main function and form. When converting an existing building, if the floor area exceeds a certain scale, a building confirmation (confirmation application) accompanying the use change may be required, and whether it is required and the necessary work are an architect's field. We carry out the property-side checks — zoning, the front road, the building's general condition — and organise the flow of confirming with an architect and the specified administrative agency whether a use change or confirmation application is required. ## Which fire-code class is it, and what equipment is required?

A welfare facility is positioned in item (6) of the fire-object classification of Appended Table 1 of the Fire Service Act Enforcement Order, and this determines the required fire-fighting equipment. Where seikatsu kaigo falls within this item (6) is important in considering the property's fire-code conformity.

Item (6) of Appended Table 1 is organised so that a facility that mainly admits persons for whom evacuation is difficult is item (6)-ro, and other welfare facilities are item (6)-ha. A disability welfare service facility falls into item (6)-ro or item (6)-ha depending on the users' condition (whether self-evacuation is difficult) and on whether it is an admission (residential) or a commuting (day) type. Seikatsu kaigo centres on daytime activity (commuting), but the judgment of the class may change depending on the proportion of users with severe disabilities and the form of the facility, so confirmation with the fire department with jurisdiction is indispensable.

| Class | Main target | Points that tend to become heavier property-side |
|---|---|---|
| Item (6)-ro | Facility mainly admitting persons for whom evacuation is difficult | Stricter standards for automatic fire alarms, sprinklers, etc. |
| Item (6)-ha | Welfare facilities other than the above (commuting type, etc.) | Fire equipment determined by total floor area and occupancy |

Fire-fighting equipment (extinguishers, automatic fire alarms, guide lights, sprinklers, etc.) differs in whether it is required depending on the class and the total floor area and occupancy. In particular, where it falls into item (6)-ro, standards such as for sprinkler installation tend to become strict, and converting an existing building can carry a heavy burden of additional work. Which class it falls into, which fire equipment is required, and how the appointment of a fire manager and the inspection/notification of fire equipment are required — confirm these with the fire department with jurisdiction over the property, noting the reference date. We do not conclusively judge the fire class or whether equipment is required.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q. Does a seikatsu kaigo property necessarily need a rest room or a bath?**
A. The facilities Article 81 of the "Standards for Personnel, Facilities and Operation of Designated Disability Welfare Service Businesses" (2006 MHLW Ordinance No. 171) necessarily requires are a training/work room, a consultation room, a washroom, a toilet, a multipurpose room and other facilities necessary for operation. A rest room and a bath are not included in that mandatory list. Seikatsu kaigo can include bathing care, so an office that provides bathing will, in practice, prepare bathing facilities, but that is separate from the mandatory list. The square metres of floor area, the relation to capacity and special provisions differ by the designating authority's guidance, so confirm with the designating authority with jurisdiction over the property, noting the reference date.

**Q. Can an existing office or shop be converted to seikatsu kaigo?**
A. It may be possible, but you must confirm whether the rooms of the facility standard (art. 81) fit, whether it can be used as a welfare facility (a child welfare facility etc. = Enforcement Order art. 19(1)) under zoning and the building use of the Building Standards Act, and the outlook for the fire class (Appended Table 1 item (6)) and fire equipment. A use change above a certain scale may require a confirmation application, and whether it is required and the necessary work are an architect's field. The fire class and whether equipment is required are confirmed with the fire department. We carry out the property-side checks — zoning, floor area, the front road, the outlook for the fire class — and organise the referrals to the architect, the fire department and the administrative scrivener.

**Q. Which fire-code class does seikatsu kaigo fall into?**
A. As a welfare facility it is classified in item (6) of Appended Table 1 of the Fire Service Act Enforcement Order. Within item (6), a facility that mainly admits persons for whom evacuation is difficult is item (6)-ro, and others are item (6)-ha, and a disability welfare service facility is divided by the users' condition and by the admission/commuting distinction. Seikatsu kaigo centres on commuting, but the judgment may change with the proportion of severe users and the form of the facility. The required fire equipment is determined by the class and the total floor area and occupancy, so confirm with the fire department with jurisdiction over the property. We do not conclusively state the class.
