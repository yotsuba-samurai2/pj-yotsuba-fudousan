## What should you obtain and mark on the plans before a viewing?

| Stage and material | Record or check | With whom |
|---|---|---|

This is a working checklist, not a statutory application-document list. Missing records or unresolved alterations must remain open, not be marked compliant. The following sections explain what to check against the plans and site.

## How do you compare the room layout with facility standards?

The facility standard for seikatsu kaigo is set by Article 81 of the "Standards for Personnel, Facilities and Operation of Designated Disability Welfare Service Businesses" (2006 MHLW Ordinance No. 171). Paragraph 1 requires a designated seikatsu kaigo office to have a training/work room, a consultation room, a washroom, a toilet and a multipurpose room, and other facilities necessary for operation.

Paragraph 2 sets the specific standard for each facility as follows. The training/work room must have ample space for training or work and be equipped with the machinery necessary for training or work. The consultation room must have a partition or the like to prevent conversation within the room from leaking. The washroom and toilet must be suited to the users' characteristics. Paragraph 3 then provides that the consultation room and the multipurpose room may be shared where there is no hindrance to supporting users, and Paragraph 4 provides that these facilities are, in principle, exclusive to the office (with an exception where there is no hindrance to supporting users).

| Facility (art. 81(1)) | Standard (art. 81(2) etc.) |
|---|---|
| Training/work room | Ample space for training/work; equipped with the necessary machinery |
| Consultation room | A partition etc. to prevent conversation leaking |
| Washroom/toilet | Suited to users' characteristics |
| Multipurpose room | May be shared with the consultation room (where no hindrance) |

A frequent misunderstanding in property consideration is the treatment of a rest room and a bath. By definition seikatsu kaigo can include bathing care, but the facilities Article 81 necessarily requires are those in the table above; a rest room and a bath are not included in that mandatory list. An office that provides bathing care will, in practice, prepare bathing facilities, but that is a matter of practical provision distinct from whether it is on the mandatory list. Also, the specific square metres of floor area and its relation to capacity, and special provisions for multi-function or small-scale types, are supplemented by the designating authority's (prefecture, designated/core city) designation guidance and interpretation, so confirm the numbers with the latest guidance of the designating authority with jurisdiction over the property (we do not state unverified square-metre figures here). We do not conclusively judge facility conformity; we help, as general information, by cross-reading the facility standard and arranging confirmation with the competent authority.

## Who checks zoning and existing use against the property particulars?

Even if the facility rooms seem to fit, whether you may build (use) a welfare facility there is a separate matter. Here zoning and the treatment of use under the Building Standards Act take effect.

A seikatsu kaigo office counts as a "child welfare facility, etc." under Article 19(1) of the Enforcement Order of the Building Standards Act. That paragraph treats, as child welfare facilities etc., facilities used for a disability welfare service business (limited to a business providing seikatsu kaigo, independence training, employment-transition support or employment-continuation support) and the like. Whether building is allowed by zoning is checked in Appended Table 2 of the Building Standards Act. In general, child welfare facilities etc. can be built in many residential and commercial zones, but there are zones — such as an exclusive industrial zone — where the treatment of a welfare facility with living rooms becomes strict.

| Check item | Content | Where to check |
|---|---|---|
| Classification of use | A seikatsu kaigo office is a child welfare facility etc. (Enforcement Order art. 19(1)) | Specified administrative agency / building official |
| Zoning allowance | Building allowance by zone in Appended Table 2; watch exclusive industrial zones etc. | Specified administrative agency / city-planning desk |
| Use-change confirmation application | When converting an existing building to welfare use above a certain scale | Architect / specified administrative agency |

Note that the zoning determination is not decided formally by the facility's name but is judged from the substance of its main function and form. When converting an existing building, if the floor area exceeds a certain scale, a building confirmation (confirmation application) accompanying the use change may be required, and whether it is required and the necessary work are an architect's field. We carry out the property-side checks — zoning, the front road, the building's general condition — and organise the flow of confirming with an architect and the specified administrative agency whether a use change or confirmation application is required. The point of converting a building without an inspection certificate to welfare use is also touched on in [can a building without an inspection certificate be changed to welfare use?](/en/column/kensazumisho-nashi-bukken-fukushi-youto-henko).

## How do you check existing fire equipment and combined services with the fire station?

A facility providing daytime-only seikatsu kaigo is listed under item (6)-ha(5) of Appended Table 1 of the Fire Service Act Enforcement Order. The proportion of users with severe disabilities alone does not automatically turn a day facility into item (6)-ro. Where residential support, short stays or other uses are combined, confirm the classification with the local fire department based on the services, overnight use and the building as a whole.

Required fire equipment also depends on floor area, storey, structure and overnight accommodation. Before signing for a property, show the fire department the service plan and floor plans to check the classification, required works, fire management and inspection/notification duties. We do not make the final conformity determination.

[Ken-o regional fire-equipment guidance, item (6)-ha(5)](https://www.nagasaki-kenoukumiai.jp/wp/wp/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/2-1seireibeppyou.pdf) (checked 18 November 2026).

## When converting an existing building, what should the property side settle before designation?

Before proceeding to the designation application (an administrative scrivener's field), there are points that, if settled property-side, reduce rework. Whether designation is granted is decided by the two wheels of property requirements and personnel/operation requirements, so if the property premise collapses, the later process has to be redone.

First, confirm — in parallel with considering lease or purchase — whether the rooms of the facility standard (art. 81) physically fit, whether it can be used as a welfare facility under zoning and the building use of the Building Standards Act, and the outlook for the fire class and fire equipment. For a lease, whether the lessor consents to use for a welfare business and to use-change/fire work, the scope of restoration, and whether the contract term matches the continuation of the designation are also check points. These are matters to settle before the property is decided; stumbling on facilities, use or fire code after filing the designation application costs extra time and money.

| Point to settle | Content | Main referral |
|---|---|---|
| Facilities & area | Whether art. 81 rooms fit; relation to capacity | Real estate (us) + designating authority's guidance |
| Zoning / use change | Appended Table 2, Enforcement Order art. 19, need for a confirmation application | Architect / specified administrative agency |
| Fire code | Item (6)-ro/ha class, fire equipment | Fire department with jurisdiction |
| Lease conditions | Consent to use/work, term, restoration | Real estate (us) |

At this stage we handle the property-side checks — zoning, floor area, the front road, the outlook for the fire class — and the organisation of lease conditions. Rather than judging facility conformity or whether designation is granted, we provide, as general information, the traffic control that connects you to the administrative scrivener handling the designation application, the architect handling the use change / confirmation application, and the fire department with jurisdiction.

Opening a seikatsu kaigo involves real estate, licensing, building, labor and tax, so it is realistic to separate the property issues from the non-property issues and assign each to the respective expert. ## Frequently asked questions

**Q. What should I take to a viewing?**
A. Take floor plans, property particulars and notes on expected users and care activities. Mark room layouts and routes, and record photos and dimensions with the owner’s permission. Plans alone do not establish compliance: compare them with the site and consult the designation authority, architect and fire station.

**Q. Is a landlord’s agreement to welfare use enough to sign?**
A. It does not establish designation, building or fire compliance. Clarify permitted use and alterations, costs, reinstatement and outstanding authority checks. Review the particular pre-designation contract conditions; legal contract advice or disputes belong with a lawyer.

**Q. Which fire-code class does seikatsu kaigo fall into?**
A. A facility providing daytime-only seikatsu kaigo is listed under item (6)-ha(5) of Appended Table 1 of the Fire Service Act Enforcement Order. The proportion of users with severe disabilities alone does not automatically turn a day facility into item (6)-ro. Where residential support, short stays or other uses are combined, confirm the classification with the local fire department based on the services, overnight use and the building as a whole.

**Q. Can I ask a real-estate company to do the designation application and staffing together?**
A. No. What we (real estate) handle is up to checking the property's zoning, floor area and fire-code conformity and brokerage. The designation application is an administrative scrivener's field, use change / confirmation application an architect's, personnel/labor a labor and social security attorney's, and tax a tax accountant's; these cannot be bundled into one contract, and we connect you in a form where each is an independent business contracted with separately (no referral fees are exchanged).
