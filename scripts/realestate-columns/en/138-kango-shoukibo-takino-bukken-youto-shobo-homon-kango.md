**The key point**: Premises that can house an integrated community care service (kango-shokibo-takino, "kango-takino") are first narrowed by four points: whether the building's use and zoning allow it (because it has an overnight function, it cannot be placed in an exclusive industrial zone, and the use classification must be checked); whether the floor area can meet the scale of registered capacity of 29 or fewer, day use up to 18, overnight up to 9; whether overnight rooms (one person per room, floor area at least 7.43 sqm) can be secured for the overnight capacity; and whether the fire equipment (sprinklers, automatic fire alarm) for residents who cannot evacuate unaided can be installed. Because it involves home-visit nursing and adds nursing staff, its starting point differs from ordinary small-scale multifunctional care (shokibo-takino). ## How does kango-takino (the combined service) differ from shokibo-takino?

Home-visit nursing is built in, and nursing-staff placement is added to the staffing standard. This splits the starting point from small-scale multifunctional care (shokibo-takino) on both the premises and operations sides.

Kango-shokibo-takino is a type of "combined service" defined in article 8, paragraph 23 of the Long-Term Care Insurance Act (Act No. 123 of 1997), combining home-visit nursing and small-scale multifunctional care. The amendment in force on 1 April 2024 (Reiwa 6) positioned it clearly in the Act as "kango-shokibo-takino," content that had previously been in the enforcement regulations. Like shokibo-takino, it is a community-based service (art. 8, para. 14); in principle residents of the municipality where the office is located use it, and the mayor designates it (art. 78-2, para. 1).

The differences on the premises and staffing sides are as follows.

| Point | Shokibo-takino | Kango-takino |
|---|---|---|
| Combination | Day visit, home visit (care), overnight | Day visit, home visit (care and nursing), overnight |
| Nursing staff | Not required | Of the staff, 2.5 or more (full-time equivalent) must be nursing staff (public-health nurse / nurse / assistant nurse) |
| Assumed users | Elderly needing care generally | Home-dwelling elderly with high medical needs (just after discharge, end-of-life, etc.) |
| Basis | Act art. 8, para. 19 | Act art. 8, para. 23 (combined service) |

The staffing standard (the Standards concerning Personnel, Equipment and Operation for Designated Community-Based Service Businesses = Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Ordinance No. 34 of 2006, art. 171) sets staff numbers for each of the day/home-visit/overnight time bands, and requires that, of the office's staff, 2.5 or more (full-time equivalent) be nursing staff and that at least one be a full-time public-health nurse or nurse. Because the designation of home-visit nursing (including deemed designation) and securing nursing staff are premised, the design of the system—not just the premises—is heavier. The overnight premises conditions specific to shokibo-takino are in [Property requirements for small-scale multifunctional care (Japanese)](https://luck428.com/en/column/shoukibo-takino-kaigo-bukken-youken); the premises conditions for a stand-alone home-visit nursing station are in [Property conditions for a home-visit nursing station (Japanese)](https://luck428.com/en/column/homon-kango-station-bukken-youken). This article focuses on what is specific to a kango-takino that covers both under one roof.

## What zoning and building use allow opening?

Because it has an overnight function, the use classification is a little more complex than for day-only services. This is a point to confirm with the designated administrative agency and an architect.

Because a kango-takino has an overnight (stay) function, under the Building Standards Act (Act No. 201 of 1950) it may be classified as a "dormitory" or a "child welfare facility, etc. (treated as including welfare facilities for the elderly)." Which it is changes the zones it can be built in and the measures required. What can be said in common is that **in an exclusive industrial zone, dwellings, dormitories and child welfare facilities, etc. cannot be built, so a kango-takino office cannot be placed there either**. Whether it can be used in other zones depends on how the building is classified, so before deciding on premises, confirm with the municipality's city-planning and building-guidance sections and an architect.

When converting an existing building, check whether a use-change confirmation application is needed. Building Standards Act art. 87 (1) applies the confirmation rules when a building's use is changed into a special building under art. 6 (1) (i), which is "a special building for a use listed in column (i) of Appended Table 1 where the total floor area for that use exceeds 200 square metres."

| Floor area used for that purpose | Use-change confirmation application |
|---|---|
| 200 sqm or less | In principle not needed |
| Over 200 sqm | Needed (Building Standards Act art. 87 (1), art. 6 (1) (i)) |

But not needing a confirmation application and not needing to comply with the Building Standards Act are different things. Art. 87 (2) applies art. 48 (zoning restrictions) and the evacuation/fire rules to a use change, so substantive rules—lighting, smoke exhaust, evacuation, interior-finish restrictions—apply regardless of whether a confirmation application is needed. Judging the use classification and designing the use change are the field of an architect and a designated confirmation and inspection body; we handle obtaining and handing over documents such as the premises' registration and drawings.

## What are the overnight capacity and the area standards for overnight rooms and living rooms?

The number of overnight rooms and the size of the living/dining room set the ceiling on how many people can be accepted. The kango-takino capacity framework is common with shokibo-takino.

Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Ordinance No. 34 of 2006 sets the kango-takino registered capacity (the upper limit of users who can register) at 29 or fewer (satellite type: 18 or fewer), and then sets the daily "day" and "overnight" use capacities within the following ranges.

| Category | Daily use capacity |
|---|---|
| Registered capacity | 29 or fewer (satellite type: 18 or fewer) |
| Day service | Half of registered capacity to 15; up to 18 depending on registered capacity |
| Overnight service | One-third of the day-service use capacity to 9 |

An overnight room has a capacity of one person (two if deemed necessary for the user's care) and a floor area of at least 7.43 sqm (at least 6.4 sqm if part of a hospital or clinic is used as the overnight room). The living room and dining room are "an appropriate size to fully perform their function," judged not by a specific number but by "whether day users can spend time there."

Read from the premises side—**if you want to accept up to 9 overnight users, you need the space to secure 9 overnight rooms of at least 7.43 sqm each (on the premise of no shared rooms)**. In addition, a kango-takino tends to need flow lines and equipment for medical procedures and infection control (hand-washing, waste disposal, storage). The thinking on day-centred home care, with a training space for the "day" function, is also useful in [Property requirements for day service (Japanese)](https://luck428.com/en/column/tsusho-kaigo-dayservice-bukken-kinou-kunren-shitsu). In a second-hand detached house or a tenancy, whether the layout can be renovated to fit the number of overnight rooms and the size of the living/dining room determines the ceiling on how many can be accepted. Note that each municipality has its own standards ordinance (added or additional requirements), so the application of area and equipment rules differs by region.

## What is the Fire Service Act classification (Appended Table 1, item (6) ro / ha) and the fire equipment required?

Because there is an "overnight" function, the fire-equipment burden can be heavier than for day-only services. This ties directly to renovation cost.

A kango-takino is classified under item (6) ro or item (6) ha of Appended Table 1 of the Fire Service Act Enforcement Order (Cabinet Order No. 37 of 1961). Which applies depends on how many people needing evacuation assistance there are (whether it mainly admits/stays persons who cannot evacuate unaided), judged by the competent fire department. A kango-takino that stays users with high medical needs is more likely to fall under (6) ro—social welfare facilities, etc. admitting persons who find self-evacuation difficult—than a day-centred facility.

| Classification | Sprinkler guide |
|---|---|
| (6) ro | The amendment in force on 1 April 2015 removed the "275 sqm or more" area requirement, making installation in principle required regardless of total floor area. An exemption remains where under 275 sqm and certain fire-spread-suppression-structure requirements are met |
| (6) ha | Needed where total floor area is 275 sqm or more, etc. (varies by classification and area) |

Besides this, (6) ro also requires an automatic fire alarm, a fire alarm reporting to the fire authority, and fire extinguishers, depending on area, etc. **When converting a second-hand detached house or small tenancy, this sprinkler cost can be a large part of the opening cost**, so before deciding on premises, consult the competent fire department in advance and confirm what is required and to what extent for that building. Judging the classification and designing the equipment are the field of the fire department and a fire-equipment engineer; we do not make that judgment.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q. How do the premises conditions differ between kango-takino and small-scale multifunctional care (shokibo-takino)?**
A. The capacity framework (registered capacity 29 or fewer, day use up to 18, overnight up to 9) and the 7.43 sqm overnight-room standard are common. The difference is that a kango-takino builds in home-visit nursing and is subject to a staffing standard requiring 2.5 or more (full-time equivalent) of its staff to be nursing staff (Ordinance No. 34 of 2006, art. 171), and that, assuming users with high medical needs, it tends to need flow lines and equipment for medical procedures and infection control. The premises' size, use and fire are confirmed in common with shokibo-takino.

**Q. Can a kango-takino be opened in a detached house in a residential area?**
A. It depends on the zoning and the building classification. Because a kango-takino has an overnight function, under the Building Standards Act it may be treated as a "dormitory" or a "child welfare facility, etc. (treated as including welfare facilities for the elderly)." It cannot be built in an exclusive industrial zone; whether it can be used in other zones depends on the use classification. On converting an existing building, if the area used for that purpose exceeds 200 sqm, a use-change confirmation application is needed (art. 87 (1)). Confirm the use classification and design with an architect and the designated administrative agency.

**Q. With an overnight function, is a sprinkler always required?**
A. A kango-takino is classified under Appended Table 1 item (6) ro or (6) ha, and the competent fire department judges the classification from the situation of those needing evacuation assistance. For (6) ro, the amendment in force on 1 April 2015 made a sprinkler in principle required regardless of total floor area (an exemption remains if under 275 sqm and fire-spread-suppression-structure requirements are met). A kango-takino staying users with high medical needs is more likely to fall under (6) ro. Because it differs by building, consult the competent fire department in advance.

**Q. If opening as a medical corporation, does the real-estate company handle the incorporation and designation application together?**
A. No. We handle only the premises survey/brokerage and the lease/sale contract. The kango-takino designation application, checking add-on requirements, and the incorporation/articles changes for opening as a medical corporation are handled by an administrative scrivener, an independent business, contracted with separately. The use-change confirmation is an architect's field, fire equipment the fire department's and a fire-equipment engineer's, registration a judicial scrivener's, and care remuneration and tax a tax accountant's. We do not exchange referral fees.
