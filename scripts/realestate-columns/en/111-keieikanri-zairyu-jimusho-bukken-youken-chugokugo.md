**Key points:** When a new corporation rents premises for Business Manager status, verify a separate business presence, the right to business use and continuing occupation before signing. Premises alone do not secure immigration approval; drafting a business plan and the required expert assessment are separate tasks.

This article concerns forming a corporation and renting its office, for Chinese-speaking founders. Do not automatically apply the same contract arrangements to owned premises. See [the existing Business Manager explanation](/en/legal/column/keieikanri-zairyu-chuka-kigyousha-kaisha-setsuritsu) for immigration requirements and [non-resident incorporation](/en/legal/column/hikyojusha-kabushiki-setsuritsu-shihonkin-kouza) for capital payments and banking.

## What evidence supports the office's business presence?

| Item | Property-side evidence |
|---|---|
| Separation | Dedicated-area plan, photographs, access and relationship to residential or shared space |
| Rights to use | Business-use contract, landlord consent and agreed pre/post-incorporation names |
| Continuity and scale | Term of use, space and equipment for operations, staff and stock |
| Appearance and activity | Signage consent, actual activities, mail arrangements and business equipment |

An address-only virtual office or freely shared workspace makes business presence difficult to establish. A dedicated rental office is not a guarantee either. The amendment guidance generally excludes home/office combinations. No universal minimum area is stated here: check the actual operations, employment and stock.

## How do the 2025 amendment and property conditions differ?

The guidance for the amendment effective October 16, 2025 identifies capital/contributions of at least JPY 30 million, at least one full-time employee and requirements concerning Japanese ability and experience or education. Employee eligibility, combinations of conditions, application type/date and transitional treatment cannot be decided from a property contract.

On October 10, 2026, the Immigration Services Agency (出入国在留管理庁, ISA) page could not be retrieved in full. All current conditions, exceptions and transitional treatment remain unverified here. This article does not describe existing holders as automatically exempt for three years or imply that renting a private room obtains approval. A human immigration professional with access must check the official ministerial ordinance and guidance before setting contract conditions and application timing.

## Who changes a pre-incorporation personal lease to the company?

For this corporate-rental scenario, agree the original contracting party and the method and timing of a later company contract with the landlord. A change of name, succession or new lease depends on the contract. Corporate registration is a judicial scrivener's task; amending a lease is not itself that registration. The landlord, tenant and broker check the terms.

Agree business use, signs, works and reinstatement as contract clauses, distinguishing them from statutory items under the Real Estate Brokerage Act (宅地建物取引業法), Articles 35 and 37. Investors pay in capital, financial institutions handle transfers and accounts, and tax accountants undertake accounting and tax work.

## What is needed for viewings and explanations from overseas?

An online viewing is different from an IT important-matters explanation. Arrange advance delivery and receipt of the explanation document, readable video and audio, and presentation and checking of the real estate transaction specialist's card. Electronic documents require separate advance consent and related procedures. Set arrangements for connection failures, identity checks and remittance arrival. See [IT explanations for overseas buyers](/en/column/kaigai-buyer-it-jusetsu-online-yoken).

## How are business-plan and professional roles separated?

We investigate property, organise contract conditions, give statutory explanations and broker transactions. Immigration application assistance and intermediary services belong to administrative scriveners or others with the applicable authority. Plan drafting assistance is separate from assessment required by the amended criteria. The official ISA index identifies small and medium enterprise management consultants, certified public accountants and tax accountants as assessors. The complete eligibility, relationship and assessment-method conditions remain unverified; the administrative-scrivener qualification alone is not treated as sufficient for that assessment. Immigration authorities examine and decide applications.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q. Is a dedicated rental office guaranteed to qualify?**
A. No. Check separation, rights to use, scale, continuity and actual activities. The immigration professional and authority assess them.

**Q. Does every application require a company-name lease?**
A. This article concerns a newly formed company renting premises. Owned premises and names before and after formation require their own checks.

**Q. Is changing a lease name part of judicial-scrivener registration?**
A. It is separate from corporate registration. The landlord and tenant agree the method, and the broker checks contract conditions.

**Q. Does drafting by an administrative scrivener complete expert assessment?**
A. Drafting assistance and assessment under the amended criteria are separate. Check the required assessor and conditions in official material and engage each professional for the relevant work.
