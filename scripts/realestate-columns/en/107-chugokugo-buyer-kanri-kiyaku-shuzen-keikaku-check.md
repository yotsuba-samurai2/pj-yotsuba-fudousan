**Key points:** Read the management rules, repair plan, accounts and arrears before contracting for a resale condominium. Check voting requirements following the April 2026 amendment. Overseas owners should distinguish a domestic condominium manager from a tax payment administrator.

For Chinese-speaking buyers and agents, this article identifies documents to request from the management association. It complements [general important-matters explanations](/en/column/chugokugo-buyer-juyojiko-check-toushi-bukken) and [foreign-exchange reporting for non-residents](/en/column/hikyojusha-fudosan-shutoku-gaitameho-houkoku).

## What documents should be requested?

| Document | What to read | Relationship to statutory explanations |
|---|---|---|
| Current management rules and use rules | Short-term rentals, pets, permitted use, amendment date and voting rules | Use restrictions and related matters are relevant |
| Management company's investigation report | Fees, repair contributions, arrears and management arrangements | Evidence for statutory items |
| Long-term repair plan and accounts | Updates, estimates, funding gaps, loans and special contributions | Detailed documents for additional due diligence |
| Meeting minutes and repair records | Proposed increases, use restrictions and major works | Additional evidence of future risks |

Request them from the seller, management company or association. Record unavailable documents and unresolved risks when considering price and contract terms. Model management rules do not replace the actual condominium's rules.

## Are low repair contributions an advantage?

Not by themselves. Compare planned costs, accumulated funds, proposed increases, special contributions, loans, works timing and past implementation. Look at when the plan was revised and how it relates to current estimates. We do not guarantee investment quality from these documents.

## How should use restrictions and amendments be read?

Administrative permission or notification does not necessarily allow an activity prohibited by management rules. Read the current restrictions and meeting discussions about amendments.

Following the amendment effective April 1, 2026, establishment, amendment or abolition of rules under the Act on Building Unit Ownership, etc. (区分所有法), Article 31(1), generally requires at least three-quarters of both the unit owners attending and their voting rights. For a special-resolution meeting, the quorum is a majority of all unit owners and a majority of all voting rights. Qualified professionals must check written voting and proxies, consent by owners particularly affected, the particular rules' amendment status and application depending on the convening date. The law does not automatically rewrite every building's rules.

## How do arrears and overseas management differ?

Article 8 allows relevant claims to be asserted against a specific successor such as the buyer. The association may claim unpaid fees from the buyer even if the sale contract allocates them to the seller. This does not automatically extinguish the seller's debt. Confirm arrears and possible late-payment charges, then consider settlement at completion. See [selling with unpaid management fees](/en/column/kanrihi-tainou-mama-ureru).

The amended law also provides a domestic-manager system for unit owners living overseas, covering meeting notices, voting and payment of management fees. It is not described here as mandatory for every buyer; check the building's procedure. This differs from a tax payment administrator. Tax advice, tax-document preparation and representation belong to tax accountants; an owner's own appointment or notification and local requirements must be distinguished.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q. Is having a long-term repair plan enough?**
A. Check its revision date, estimates, funds, planned increases, special contributions, loans and completed works. Existence alone does not establish good management.

**Q. Will rules allowing short-term rentals remain unchanged?**
A. They can change. Check the amended voting basis and quorum effective April 2026, the actual rules and meeting discussions.

**Q. Can the association claim arrears from the buyer despite a seller-payment clause?**
A. It may. Distinguish the parties' allocation from the association's rights, confirm arrears and consider settlement at completion.

**Q. Is a domestic manager the same as a tax payment administrator?**
A. No. One concerns condominium management and the other tax procedures. Engage a tax accountant for professional tax work and check the owner's appointment and notification requirements separately.
