**Key points:** For private-booth internet and manga cafés, check zoning and floor area, booth plans and operations, fire protection (automatic fire alarms) and identity-verification facilities before signing. Not providing bedding does not by itself settle whether lodging-business rules apply from plans alone, and food service with booth lighting and sightlines can raise separate public-morals questions.

This article covers issues specific to private booths, reclining seating and internet-terminal services for operators and property owners. For businesses involving lodging see [lodging and hotel property requirements](https://luck428.com/en/column/ryokangyo-hotel-eigyo-bukken-youken); for private lodging see [minpaku property conditions](https://luck428.com/en/column/minpaku-bukken-joken-kakunin) and [special-zone minpaku certification](https://luck428.com/en/column/tokku-minpaku-bukken-youken-nintei).

## Which zones can accommodate the property?

Internet and manga cafés are generally treated under Building Standards Act use restrictions as "shops." Unlike clinics, which are permitted in all zones, viability depends on the zone and relevant-use floor area at the address.

| Zone | What to check before signing |
|---|---|
| Category I/II low-rise residential, etc. | Confirm whether the tight shop-use restriction makes it difficult in principle |
| Residential system (Category I/II residential, quasi-residential) | Confirm conditional viability, such as shop floor-area limits |
| Neighbourhood commercial, commercial, quasi-industrial | Check district plans, special-use districts and local rules alongside the national use table |
| Industrial, exclusively industrial | Confirm shop viability and floor-area conditions |

This summarises general use restrictions, not an individual compliance decision. Whether a café is treated as an "amusement facility" or a "service shop" varies by building and operation; the architect and building authority confirm the use category. Zones are not a simple scale from looser to stricter.

## Do reclining booths fall under the lodging business (simple lodging)?

The Hotel Business Act (旅館業法, Act No. 138 of 1948), Article 2, defines the lodging business as providing lodging for a fee; simple-lodging business is one type. Internet and manga cafés are often not treated as requiring a lodging permit, but as booths, naps and long stays increase, municipalities may make a comprehensive judgment.

Health and Labour Ministry materials also discuss facilities not covered by the Act yet capable of lodging in practice. Public-health offices look not only at whether bedding is provided but at the booth structure and actual use. Check:

- Whether operations provide bedding (futons or beds)
- Whether booths lock and are set up for long stays or naps
- Whether pricing and membership terms could be read as "lodging"

"No bedding, no lodging business" cannot be stated as a blanket rule. Confirm classification with the local public-health office, showing booth plans and operations.

## What booth layout keeps it clear of the public-morals categories (low-lit / partitioned seats)?

The Act on Control and Improvement of Amusement Business, etc. (風俗営業等の規制及び業務の適正化等に関する法律, Act No. 122 of 1948), Article 2(1), makes certain food/drink businesses subject to permission. Booth-type premises serving food may formally resemble these, so check them separately.

| Category (Article 2(1)) | Element to examine |
|---|---|
| Low-lit food/drink premises (item 2) | Serving food/drink with seat illumination at 10 lux or less |
| Partitioned-seat premises (item 3) | Seats hard to see from elsewhere and no more than 5 m², used to serve food/drink |

Because café booths are small, they can formally meet the partitioned-seat area element. The dividing point is often "hard to see from elsewhere," making booth sightlines (a door window, open ceiling) and illumination the issue. Confirm classification with the police and public safety commission using booth plans and the food-service method. Not serving late-night alcohol does not by itself establish that no permission is needed.

## What does the internet-terminal business ordinance require of the property?

Tokyo has the Ordinance on Regulation of Internet-Terminal Use Businesses (インターネット端末利用営業の規制に関する条例, Tokyo Ordinance No. 64 of 2010, effective July 1, 2010), covering premises that let customers use internet terminals. Property selection and opening involve the notification and identity-verification practice.

| Item | What to check before signing/opening |
|---|---|
| Notification | File per store, before opening, with the Tokyo Public Safety Commission via the police station covering the address |
| Attachments | A plan showing booth and PC layout is required |
| Identity verification | Verify and record the user's name, address and date of birth from identity documents |
| Record retention | Keep records for the period the ordinance sets (a set number of years from the last service date) |

Confirm the layout can accommodate a reception flow and facilities for identity verification and record-keeping. Confirm the current forms, deadlines and retention years directly with the police before applying; outside Tokyo, check each prefecture's ordinance. Ordinances differ by locality.

## What fire equipment and interior-finish limits should you check when selecting a property?

Premises providing internet use or manga reading in booths are treated as booth-type premises under the Fire Service Act Enforcement Order (消防法施行令), Appended Table 1, category (2)ニ, and automatic fire alarms are required regardless of floor area. The Yokohama City Fire Bureau guidance notes that enclosed spaces partitioned only by screens or curtains may also qualify as booth-type premises.

- Whether automatic fire alarms are installed, or the building allows installation
- Whether escape routes, guidance lights and extinguishing equipment work with the booth layout (including windowless-floor and mixed-use checks)
- Whether alarm communication can be designed so sound or insulation does not block alarms
- Whether interior-finish restrictions (wall and ceiling finishes) are met

These vary by building with use classification, existing equipment, floors and area. Confirm fire-equipment requirements and technical standards with the fire department and a qualified contractor, and interior limits and any change-of-use confirmation application with an architect.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q. If we provide no bedding, is a lodging permit unnecessary?**
A. Not as a blanket rule. Confirm with the local public-health office, including booth structure, actual stays and pricing, showing plans and operations.

**Q. If we serve no late-night alcohol, is a public-morals permit unnecessary?**
A. Alcohol alone does not decide it. Confirm with the police whether booth illumination, sightlines and size fall under low-lit or partitioned-seat premises.

**Q. Can the internet-terminal notification be filed before the property is fixed?**
A. It needs booth and PC layout plans, so prepare it after the property and layout are set. Design the identity-verification reception flow first.

**Q. Can automatic fire alarms be added later?**
A. Booth-type premises require them regardless of area. For a property without them, confirm before signing that the building allows installation, alongside escape and interior limits.
