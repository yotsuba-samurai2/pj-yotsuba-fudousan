**In short:** a property usable for special-zone minpaku (the national strategic special zone foreign-visitor accommodation business) is largely decided by three points — (1) whether it is in a zone and land-use district the municipality allows, (2) whether a room of roughly 25㎡ or more is fitted with a kitchen, bath, toilet and washbasin, and (3) whether you can put in place neighbour notification and complaint handling. The stay is, as a rule, at least two nights and three days.

For operators and investors looking for an accommodation-use property in a national strategic special zone such as Ota Ward (Tokyo) or Osaka City, this organises the property side of special-zone minpaku — only "what you can check about the property": land-use district, room area and equipment. The certification application under Article 13 of the National Strategic Special Zones Act, and the licensing judgment of which system to choose among minpaku, lodging business and special-zone minpaku, go to an administrative scrivener; the need for fire-safety equipment to the fire station with jurisdiction; tax to a licensed tax accountant — each routed separately. How to view a minpaku property under the Housing Accommodation Business Act is in [conditions for a property usable as minpaku](https://luck428.com/column/minpaku-bukken-joken-kakunin). ## How does special-zone minpaku differ from minpaku and the lodging business?

Special-zone minpaku is a separate system allowed only in national strategic special zones. You receive municipal certification and may run an accommodation business without being subject to the Hotel Business Act. A one-night stay is not allowed; the stay is, as a rule, at least two nights and three days.

Housing accommodation (minpaku), the lodging business (simple lodging, etc.) and special-zone minpaku differ in their governing law, procedure and minimum stay. Special-zone minpaku is the foreign-visitor accommodation business under Article 13 of the National Strategic Special Zones Act (Act No. 107 of 2013), and can only be carried out in an area designated as a national strategic special zone.

| System | Governing law | Procedure | Minimum stay / annual cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Special-zone minpaku | National Strategic Special Zones Act Art. 13 | Municipal certification (specific certification) | As a rule, at least 2 nights/3 days. No annual operating-days cap |
| Minpaku | Housing Accommodation Business Act | Notification to the prefectural governor, etc. | No minimum stay. Annual provision capped at 180 days |
| Lodging business (simple lodging, etc.) | Hotel Business Act | Permit from the prefectural governor, etc. | No stay or operating-days limit |

Once certified, special-zone minpaku is exempt from the Hotel Business Act, with no annual operating-days cap. On the other hand, the areas where it can be carried out are limited to national strategic special zones, and the stay has a floor of, as a rule, two nights and three days. Note that in Osaka City, the acceptance of new certification applications and change applications such as adding rooms ended on 29 May 2026 (Reiwa 8). Which system to choose varies with the property's location and the business plan, and the licensing judgment of system choice is the province of an administrative scrivener.

## In which zone and land-use district can a property be used for special-zone minpaku?

First, it must be within the area of a municipality that carries out special-zone minpaku as a national strategic special zone. On top of that, the land-use district is limited by the municipality's ordinance and guidelines, and residential-only districts generally cannot be used.

Special-zone minpaku is carried out in areas designated as national strategic special zones, such as Ota Ward in Tokyo and Osaka Prefecture / Osaka City (new acceptance ended). The treatment of land-use districts is set by each municipality's ordinance and guidelines. Ota Ward, for instance, limits its operating area — from the viewpoint of preserving the existing living environment — to land-use districts where a hotel or inn can be built under Article 48 of the Building Standards Act.

| Ota Ward example (land-use districts of the operating area) | Whether included |
|---|---|
| Category II residential, quasi-residential, neighbourhood commercial, commercial, quasi-industrial | Included in the operating area |
| Category I residential | Only buildings of 3,000㎡ or less are included |
| Category I/II low-rise residential exclusive, rural-residential, etc. (residential-exclusive system) | Not included in the operating area |

Because the zone/land-use lines differ by municipality, confirm before viewing — against the municipality's latest guidelines and the city-planning map — whether the property's location is within the operating area and whether the land-use district is one where a hotel or inn can be built. For a leased property, also check that the lease and the management rules do not prohibit an accommodation business.

## What room area and equipment are required?

A room of roughly 25㎡ or more, fitted with a kitchen, bath, toilet and washbasin; locks on the entrance and windows; appropriate ventilation, lighting and illumination; bedding and furniture provided and cleanliness maintained. Foreign-language guidance is also a requirement.

The facility standards for special-zone minpaku are set by the National Strategic Special Zones Act Enforcement Order (Cabinet Order No. 99 of 2014) and, under it, the MHLW ordinance and each municipality's ordinance. The main points that bite on the property side are as follows.

| Check item | Guide / content |
|---|---|
| Area of one room | Roughly 25㎡ or more (confirm against the municipality's ordinance/guidelines) |
| Water facilities | A kitchen, bath (shower acceptable), toilet and washbasin |
| Security / partitioning | Each room's entrance and windows can be locked |
| Hygiene / habitability | Appropriate ventilation, lighting, illumination; bedding and furniture provided; cleanliness maintained |
| Guidance for guests | How to use the facility and the surrounding area provided also in a foreign language |
| Fire | Per use/scale, automatic fire-alarm equipment, fire extinguishers, etc. Confirm with the fire station |

"25㎡ or more per room" is common to many operating municipalities, but the way area is reckoned by number of rooms and the fine detail differ by municipality, so confirm against the ordinance and guidelines. Fire-safety equipment varies with the building's use and scale; automatic fire-alarm equipment and fire extinguishers are required depending on the total floor area and number of storeys. **Be sure to confirm the need for, and specifications of, fire-safety equipment with the fire station that has jurisdiction.**

## For neighbour notification and complaint handling, what is required of the property?

Before opening, you must notify neighbouring residents of the business and set up a point of contact that responds appropriately and promptly to complaints and enquiries. The property side needs a place to post the contact details and the capacity to maintain a complaint-handling setup.

For special-zone minpaku, explanation to neighbouring residents and a complaint/enquiry-handling setup are among the certification requirements. Osaka City requires setting up a complaint-handling counter and posting a sign showing the facility name, the complaint recipient and a phone number; Ota Ward and others require holding an explanatory session for neighbours and keeping a record of it. In practice, notifying residents of buildings within a set range of the facility's boundary (e.g. roughly 10 m) by leafleting or visits is the operating approach.

| What the property equips | Content |
|---|---|
| Notification | Inform neighbours of the business before opening (explanatory session, leaflet, visit, etc.; keep a record) |
| Complaint counter | Set up a contact setup that handles complaints/enquiries appropriately and promptly |
| Posting a sign | Secure a place to post the facility name, complaint recipient and contact details |

Where a unit in a multi-unit building is used, the relationship with the other residents of the same building is especially likely to become an issue, so checking the management rules and careful notification of neighbours matter from the property-selection stage. The concrete method of notification and complaint handling is set by the municipality's ordinance and guidelines.

## What should be prepared on the property side before the certification application?

Assemble: whether the location fits the operating area/land-use district, the floor plan, room area and equipment drawings, a prior consultation with the fire station, confirmation of the lease/management rules, and the neighbour-notification plan. These are things you can check and prepare on the property.

The certification application itself is the province of an administrative scrivener, but there are things to get in order on the property side as a premise of the application. Fixing the property's conformity first makes system choice and the application smoother.

| Property-side material / check to prepare | Where to confirm |
|---|---|
| Whether the location is in the operating area, and whether the land-use district allows a hotel/inn | The municipality's city-planning map / guidelines |
| Current state of the floor plan, room area, equipment (kitchen, bath, toilet, washbasin) | The property drawings / site |
| Current state and need of fire-safety equipment | Prior consultation with the fire station |
| Whether the lease / management rules prohibit an accommodation business | The lessor / management association |
| Plan for neighbour notification and complaint handling | The municipality's guidelines |

Confirm the property's conformity and the current state of fire and equipment by document and on site, and hand system choice and the certification application to an administrative scrivener. ## Frequently asked questions

**Q. Does special-zone minpaku have an annual operating-days cap?**
A. No. Whereas minpaku under the Housing Accommodation Business Act is capped at 180 days of provision a year, special-zone minpaku is certified by the municipality and exempt from the Hotel Business Act, so there is no annual operating-days cap. A one-night stay is not allowed; the stay is, as a rule, at least two nights and three days. Which system fits the property and business plan is the province of an administrative scrivener.

**Q. Can special-zone minpaku be done in a property in a residential-only district?**
A. Generally no. In the Ota Ward example, the operating area is limited to land-use districts where a hotel or inn can be built under Article 48 of the Building Standards Act (Category II residential, quasi-residential, neighbourhood commercial, commercial, quasi-industrial, and Category I residential at 3,000㎡ or less), and residential-exclusive districts are not included. Because the zone/land-use lines differ by municipality, confirm against the municipality's guidelines and city-planning map.

**Q. How many square metres does a room need?**
A. In many operating municipalities, roughly 25㎡ or more per room is the guide. But the way area is reckoned by number of rooms and the fine detail differ by municipality's ordinance and guidelines. Besides area, a kitchen, bath, toilet and washbasin, locks on entrances and windows, ventilation, lighting and illumination, bedding and furniture, and maintaining cleanliness are requirements. Confirm the latest standards against the municipality's guidelines.

**Q. Can I still apply for special-zone minpaku certification in Osaka City now?**
A. In Osaka City, the acceptance of new certification applications and change applications such as adding rooms ended on 29 May 2026 (Reiwa 8). You can no longer newly start in Osaka City. Because the areas carried out and the acceptance status vary by municipality, confirm the latest guidance of the municipality where the property is located, and consult an administrative scrivener on system choice.
