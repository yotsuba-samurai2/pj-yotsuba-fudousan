## What changed in the safety rules for light cargo from April 2025?

Light-cargo delivery (light motor-truck transport), whose vehicle numbers have surged as a backbone of home delivery and last-mile work, had its safety measures tightened against a background of rising accidents. The amending act is Act No. 23 of 2024 (promulgated on 15 May 2024), and the related ordinances (the amendment of the Safety of Transport Regulations for the Motor Truck Transport Business, and the ordinance partially amending the Motor Vehicle Accident Report Regulations, etc.) were promulgated on 1 October 2024. Enforcement is phased: the part on the registration of training providers took effect on 1 November 2024, and the part regulating light motor-truck transport operators (the appointment of a safety manager, operating records, and so on) took effect on 1 April 2025.

The main new duties for operators are as follows. Because the ordinances set the fine detail, confirm the forms and place of retention in official materials immediately before filing.

| New duty | Content | Example excluded from scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Appointing a light motor-truck safety manager | Appoint one per business office and have them complete the training (Article 36-2 of the Motor Truck Transport Business Act) | An operator transporting only with two-wheel vehicles (so-called motorcycle courier) |
| Preparing and retaining operating records | Record each day's start/end points and the distance driven, and retain for one year | A two-wheel-only operator |
| Preparing and retaining accident records | Record the outline, cause, and recurrence-prevention measures of accidents, and retain | — |
| Accident reporting | Report accidents of a certain scale or larger to the Minister through the Transport Branch Office | — |
| Guidance and aptitude tests for drivers | Special guidance and aptitude tests for accident-causing, newly hired, and elderly drivers, and keeping a driver ledger | A two-wheel-only operator |

## Whom do you appoint as the light motor-truck safety manager, and by when must you notify?

Article 36-2 of the Motor Truck Transport Business Act requires a light motor-truck transport operator carrying cargo with a four-wheel or larger light vehicle to appoint one light motor-truck safety manager per business office promptly after making the business notification. Even an operator working alone must appoint someone who meets the requirements — namely that person themselves (a two-wheel-only motorcycle courier is outside the scope).

Who may be appointed is one of the following.

| Who may be appointed | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| A training graduate | A person who completed the light motor-truck safety manager training run by a registered training provider within two years before the appointment date |
| A person who also completed the periodic training | A person who completed the above training and, in addition, the light motor-truck safety manager periodic training within two years before the appointment date |
| An operation manager | Where the operator also runs a general or specified motor-truck transport business, a person appointed as the operation manager at that business office |

When you appoint (and when you dismiss), you notify the Minister of the safety manager's name and so on without delay, as prescribed by ministerial ordinance. In practice the window is the competent Transport Branch Office, and the appointment notification is accompanied by a certificate of completion of training completed within two years before the appointment date. After appointment, except for a person who doubles as the operation manager, you must have them take periodic training every two years. Confirm the fees, payment deadlines, and training schedule in the official guidance of the training provider and the Transport Branch Office.

## What records and reports become newly necessary?

Along with appointing a safety manager, records and reports on daily operations and accidents became duties. Because ordinances such as the Safety of Transport Regulations for the Motor Truck Transport Business set the fine detail, confirm the forms and retention methods in official materials.

- Operating records: record each day's start point, end point, and the distance driven, and retain them for one year.
- Accident records: record the outline, cause, and recurrence-prevention measures of an accident, and retain them (the retention period follows the ordinance; the Ministry's guidance states three years for accident records).
- Accident reporting: report accidents of a certain scale or larger, such as those causing casualties, to the Minister through the Transport Branch Office.
- Guidance and aptitude tests for drivers: provide special guidance and aptitude tests for accident-causing, newly hired, and elderly drivers, and prepare a light motor-truck driver ledger kept at the business office.

Records are not "make them and be done"; the aim of the system is that the safety manager uses them to secure the safety of operations. Operating records relate to the management of working hours as well, but the preparation and retention of records is a duty on the safety-rules side of the transport business, while the management of working hours themselves is, as below, a matter of the labor framework.

## What should an operator already in business do?

For an operator already running light cargo, there is a transitional measure (grace period) for appointing a safety manager. An operator that had made the business notification for light motor-truck transport by the end of March 2025 is given a grace of two years after enforcement and need only appoint a safety manager by March 2027. An operator that makes the business notification on or after April 2025 has no grace and appoints one promptly before starting the business. For guidance and aptitude tests for drivers and for maintaining the driver ledger, a grace of three years after enforcement is provided.

| Category of operator | Deadline to appoint a safety manager |
| --- | --- |
| An existing operator that notified by the end of March 2025 | Appoint by March 2027 |
| An operator that notifies on or after April 2025 | Appoint promptly by the start of business |

Even with a grace period, since completion of training must be within two years before the appointment date, putting training off can leave you unable to appoint in time if it coincides with a period when bookings are hard to get. An existing operator, too, is safer to secure a training date early and to prepare the form of operating records in advance. ## FAQ

**Q. Even if I run light cargo alone, must I appoint a safety manager?**
A. Yes. An operator carrying cargo with a four-wheel or larger light vehicle must, even when working alone, have that person themselves complete the training and be appointed as the safety manager per business office. What is outside the scope is an operator transporting only with two-wheel vehicles (a so-called motorcycle courier). Confirm the detail in the Transport Branch Office's guidance immediately before filing.

**Q. By when must I appoint a safety manager?**
A. An existing operator that had made the business notification by the end of March 2025 has a two-year grace after enforcement and need only appoint one by March 2027. An operator that notifies on or after April 2025 has no grace and appoints one promptly by the start of business. Because completion of training must be within two years before the appointment date, we recommend taking the training early.

**Q. How long must operating records and accident records be kept?**
A. Operating records of each day's start/end points and the distance driven are retained for one year. Accident records of the outline, cause, and recurrence-prevention measures of an accident are retained (the Ministry's guidance states three years). The forms and retention methods are set by ordinances such as the Safety of Transport Regulations for the Motor Truck Transport Business, so confirm them in official materials.

**Q. Can I ask one office to handle everything from the notification to the management of working hours?**
A. The business notification and the notification of the safety manager's appointment are for an administrative scrivener (us), while the management of working hours and the duty roster, work rules, and social-insurance procedures in line with the Improvement Standards Notice are for a labor and social security attorney — each contracted separately as an independent business.
