**In short:** Put the Certificate of Eligibility (COE) refusal notice beside a copy of the application. Match each stated reason to the evidence already submitted. Filing a new application is different from asking a court to set aside the refusal. If you are considering court action, consult a lawyer promptly.

## Was the COE refused, or was a visa refused?

Start with the issuing authority and the document it examined. A COE under Article 7-2(1) of the Immigration Control and Refugee Recognition Act (出入国管理及び難民認定法) provides advance evidence that the proposed activity or status meets the landing condition in Article 7(1), item 2. What must be proved depends on the status of residence. A COE may also be withheld if it is clear that another condition listed in items 1, 3 or 4 is not met (Enforcement Regulation, Article 6-2(5), proviso).

A COE is neither a visa issued by a Japanese diplomatic mission nor permission to land. For example, the general six-month restriction described by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs concerns a new **visa** application for the same purpose following a visa refusal. It is not an instruction to wait six months after a COE refusal.

## What should the applicant take from the notice, and whom should they contact?

Keep the date of receipt, reviewing office, application number, unmet condition and stated reasons, along with copies of the application and its attachments. Separate what the notice actually says from what the applicant or host can document and what remains unknown. That gives you specific questions to put to the office.

In a response published by the Cabinet Office in 2018, the Ministry of Justice explained that reasons are normally explained by the office that examined the application; not every branch can give that explanation. That response does not establish the number of interviews available today or a uniform booking procedure. Follow the notice and check with the reviewing office about who may ask, whether an appointment is needed, and what identification or documents to bring. Record what the officer said separately from your own interpretation.

## How should we organize records from China or Taiwan?

Nationality alone does not determine the document list. The table illustrates ways to cross-check the previous filing, not documents every applicant must submit.

| Check | Example |
|---|---|
| Names and dates | Align the passport spelling, Chinese characters in simplified or traditional script, names on certificates, and date systems. If a name or spelling changed, explain the difference with supporting records. |
| Proposed activity | Compare the job description and start date in the application with the host's contract and supporting papers. Show when and why a plan changed. |
| Translations and originals | Attach Japanese translations to foreign-language supporting documents (Enforcement Regulation, Article 66). Make it easy to match each translation to its source document and page. |

If the issue is what a job involves, replacing one job title with another tells the examiner little. Distinguish the facts at the time of the first filing from the present plan, then show who will perform which tasks, where, and what records support the explanation. Do not fill gaps by guessing. ## How do a fresh application, an administrative appeal and a court action differ?

A fresh filing asks for a COE again; it does not overturn the earlier decision. Article 7(1), item 10 of the Administrative Complaint Review Act (行政不服審査法) excludes decisions on foreigners' entry and departure from the administrative review procedure under that Act. That exclusion is not a bar to seeking judicial review.

In a COE case decided on 10 July 2014 (Tokyo District Court, 2013 (Gyo-U) No. 235), the court examined and rejected the claim to revoke the refusal. It dismissed as inadmissible the separate claim for an order compelling issuance. The result does not predict another applicant's prospects. If you are considering a challenge, take the notice, its date of receipt and the filed papers to a lawyer without waiting for a fresh COE decision. Ask which deadlines apply to your case.

## FAQ

**Q. Can someone in China or Taiwan apply simply by hiring an administrative scrivener in Japan?**
A. An eligible representative is not the same as a professional who submits documents. If the applicant is abroad, check whether a person in Japan, such as an employee of the receiving organization, qualifies as the representative for that status. Submission by a duly notified lawyer or administrative scrivener is subject to its own conditions (Act, Article 7-2(2); Enforcement Regulation, Article 6-2(3) and (4)).

**Q. Is everyone entitled to exactly one meeting about the refusal?**
A. Do not assume a uniform entitlement or number of interviews. Read the notice, list what needs clarification, and ask the reviewing office how to make the inquiry.

**Q. What if we omitted an explanation the first time?**
A. Identify the previous statement it relates to, whether this was true at the time or is a later change, and what evidence supports it. More documents alone do not guarantee issuance.
