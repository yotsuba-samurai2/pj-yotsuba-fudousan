**In short:** Employment Selection Support (shuro sentaku shien) is a welfare service for persons with disabilities created on 1 October 2025. Together with a person who wishes to work or to continue working, it assesses aptitude, knowledge, and ability for employment through short-term productive and other activities, and organizes the person's wishes and the accommodations they will need — an "employment assessment" (Comprehensive Support for Persons with Disabilities Act Article 5(13); newly created by the amending Act = 2022 Act No. 104, in force 1 October 2025). Whereas employment transition support and continuous employment support Type A/B provide a "place to work," Employment Selection Support is an entry-point support that sizes up which way of working or which service best suits the person; the purpose differs. To be designated you obtain designation as a designated welfare-service provider from the prefectural governor (for designated and core cities, the mayor) under Act Article 36, and must meet the personnel, equipment, and operation standards set in the ministerial ordinance (2006 MHLW Ordinance No. 171), Chapter 10-2 (Articles 173-2 to 173-9). For personnel, employment selection support workers are placed at a full-time-equivalent of at least the number of users divided by 15 (Ordinance Article 173-3), and the implementing body is limited to a designated provider of transition or continuous employment support that the governor recognizes as having, within the past three years, had three or more of its users newly employed by an ordinary workplace or an equivalent track record (Ordinance Article 173-6). The employment selection support worker is in principle a person who has completed the employment-selection-support-worker training course, with a transitional measure until the end of FY2027 treating completers of the basic training and the like as equivalent (2025 MHLW Public Notice No. 89). ## How does Employment Selection Support differ from existing continuous and transition employment support?

Employment Selection Support is a new welfare service created on 1 October 2025 (Act Article 5(13)). The provision defines it as, for a person who wishes to work or to continue working, assessing aptitude, knowledge, and ability for employment through short-term productive and other activities, organizing the person's wishes and needed accommodations, and providing the necessary benefits (Ordinance Article 173-2). In short, using the method of "employment assessment" (grasping employment needs, evaluating ability and aptitude, and organizing accommodations to be made after employment starts), it helps size up, together with the person, the way of working or the service that suits them — an entry-point support.

By contrast, employment transition support is training toward ordinary employment, and continuous employment support Type A/B is the provision of a place to work itself. Employment Selection Support is weighted toward "assessment for choosing," not "a place to work"; the purpose differs. From October 2025, where a person newly uses continuous employment support Type B, they are in principle to use Employment Selection Support first; a similar treatment is planned from April 2027 for Type A and for transition support beyond the standard period (施行 notice; confirm the timing in the competent local government's latest guidance).

| Axis | Employment Selection Support | Transition support | Continuous support Type A/B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Employment assessment (aptitude and wishes) | Training toward ordinary employment | Provision of a place to work |
| Position | Entry point for choosing a service | Transition to ordinary employment | Ongoing work/productive activity |
| Basis | Act Article 5(13) | Act Article 5(14) | Act Article 5(15) |

The designation requirements for existing Type A/B are organized in [Requirements for designation of continuous employment support Type A/B](https://luck428.com/en/legal/column/shuro-keizoku-shien-ab-shitei-shinsei-youken), and the designation of independence training in [Designation application for independence training](https://luck428.com/en/legal/column/jiritsu-kunren-seikatsu-kinou-shitei-shinsei-youken). The content and judgments of the assessment are borne by qualified professionals and the operator as an independent business under a separate contract.

## What are the requirements for the personnel (the employment selection support worker) needed to be designated?

The personnel to place at a designated office are a manager and employment selection support workers. Under Ordinance Article 173-3(1), the number of employment selection support workers is, by the full-time-equivalent method, at least the number of users divided by 15, and at least one is placed. The number of users is the previous year's average (for a new designation, an estimate) (Article 173-3(2)). The worker is in principle engaged exclusively in the office's duties, except where this does not hinder support of users (Article 173-3(3)). The chapter on Employment Selection Support contains no provision that directly requires placing a service-management supervisor as continuous employment support does.

The employment selection support worker is a person designated by the Minister as one who provides the designated service, in principle a completer of the employment-selection-support-worker training course (2025 MHLW Public Notice No. 89). However, until the end of FY2027 there is a transitional measure treating as a course-completer a person who has completed the basic training conducted by the National Institute of Vocational Rehabilitation and the like, or training equivalent or above (such as the employment-support basic training [for employment-support-worker type], the visiting-type job coach training, the service-management-supervisor specialist-course training [employment-support course], and the counselling-support-worker specialist-course training [employment-support course]).

| Job type | Placement | Main qualification | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Manager | 1 (concurrent duty allowed if no hindrance) | Social welfare officer qualification or 2+ years in social welfare work, etc. | Ordinance applied provisions |
| Employment selection support worker | FTE ≥ users ÷ 15 (at least 1) | Completion of the support-worker training (basic training etc. deemed equivalent until end of FY2027) | Ordinance Article 173-3; Public Notice No. 89 |

## Where do you look for the equipment and operation standards and the conditions required of the premises?

The equipment and operation standards follow Chapter 10-2 of the Ordinance (Articles 173-2 to 173-9) and its applied provisions (Articles 173-4, 173-5, 173-9). Many local governments require a training/work room, a consultation room, an office, a washroom, a toilet, and a multipurpose room, and show area guidelines in their guidance (for example, a certain area per user for the training/work room, and a width considering privacy for the consultation room). Because the specific dimensions, whether sharing is allowed, and the treatment when operated integrally with daily-life care, independence training, transition, or continuous support differ by local government, always confirm in the competent local government's latest guidance (this article does not assert a specific area; accessed 2026-11-27).

On the premises side, the key points are zoning, whether a change of use under the Building Standards Act is needed, and fire-service conformity. These are the domain of a licensed real estate broker and an architect, to be settled before the lease. On the operation side, the implementation of the evaluation and organization (Ordinance Article 173-7) and coordination with related institutions (Ordinance Article 173-8) are required, so a system to share assessment results with the person and related institutions is essential.

| Zoning / change of use | Use under the Building Standards Act; fire conformity | Licensed real estate broker / architect (separate contract) |
| Operation system | Implementation of evaluation and coordination with institutions | Ordinance Articles 173-7, 173-8 |

## What is the application flow, and where do people stumble at the pre-consultation?

Designation is obtained from the prefectural governor (for designated and core cities, the mayor) (Act Article 36). In many local governments, you work back from the designation date (often the first of the month) to meet the application deadline of the previous month and the pre-consultation. The order is generally: confirm the implementing-body requirement, fix the premises and equipment, secure personnel (employment selection support workers), pre-consult, make the main application, and be designated. Because the deadline and standard processing period are set by each local government, confirm them in its guidance (this article does not assert a specific number of days).

Where people stumble is the implementing-body requirement (below) and overlooking the worker's qualification requirement. In particular, whether a completed course falls under the training deemed equivalent until the end of FY2027 must be checked by matching the training name against the public notice one by one. Skipping the pre-consultation risks being told of shortfalls in personnel or equipment at the main application and missing the designation date.

| Step | Content | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Confirm the implementing-body requirement | Designation for transition/continuous support and a track record of ordinary employment | Ordinance Article 173-6 |
| Secure personnel | Confirm the worker's training completion | Public Notice No. 89; labour consultant (labour) |
| Pre-consultation | Confirm conformity via drawings and personnel plan | Local-government window |
| Main application | Prepare and submit the application | Application by scrivener |

## What transitional measures and co-location points apply when an existing employment-service office newly takes this on?

The implementing body of Employment Selection Support is limited to a designated welfare-service provider of transition or continuous employment support that the governor recognizes as having, within the past three years, had three or more of its users newly employed by an ordinary workplace, or as having an equivalent track record and experience in employment support (Ordinance Article 173-6). Thus a brand-new juridical person is not expected to start Employment Selection Support alone from the outset; it is a design in which an existing employment-service office takes it on against a track record. Some local governments indicate operating with a minimum capacity of 10 or more (local guidance; accessed 2026-11-27).

When co-located and operated integrally with daily-life care, independence training, transition, or continuous support, some local governments allow sharing existing equipment other than the training/work room, but operation that keeps the neutrality of the assessment (the stance of supporting the person's best choice) is required. Take care that it is not seen as steering users to the office's own services; attend to the independence of the assessment. The content and judgment of the assessment are the decision domain of qualified professionals and the operator; the administrative scrivener is limited to supporting the preparation of application documents.

| Issue | Content | Referral |
|---|---|---|
| Implementing-body requirement | Designation for employment services + 3+ ordinary-employment moves in the past 3 years | Ordinance Article 173-6 |
| Transitional measure | Worker: basic training etc. deemed equivalent until end of FY2027 | Public Notice No. 89 |
| Co-location / sharing | Whether equipment other than the training/work room may be shared | Local guidance |
| Neutrality | Operation not seen as steering to one's own services | Operator / qualified professionals |

## FAQ

**Q. Can a brand-new juridical person be designated for Employment Selection Support?**
A. In principle this is thought difficult. The implementing body is limited to a designated welfare-service provider of transition or continuous employment support that the governor recognizes as having, within the past three years, had three or more of its users newly employed by an ordinary workplace, or an equivalent track record (Ordinance Article 173-6). It is a design in which an existing employment-service office takes it on against a track record. The specific possibility rests with the competent local government's review.

**Q. What training must one complete to become an employment selection support worker?**
A. In principle, completion of the employment-selection-support-worker training course (2025 MHLW Public Notice No. 89). However, until the end of FY2027 there is a transitional measure treating as a completer a person who has completed the basic training or training equivalent or above (the employment-support basic training [for employment-support-worker type], the visiting-type job coach training, and the service-management-supervisor / counselling-support-worker specialist-course training [employment-support course], etc.). Check whether the training applies by matching its name against the public notice.

**Q. How many employment selection support workers must be placed?**
A. Under Ordinance Article 173-3, by the full-time-equivalent method, at least the number of users divided by 15, with at least one placed. The number of users is the previous year's average (for a new designation, an estimate). How FTE is counted, the treatment of part-time staff, and the social-insurance enrolment line are labour design, the domain of a social insurance labour consultant.

**Q. Must a person use Employment Selection Support before using continuous employment support Type B?**
A. Where a person newly uses continuous employment support Type B from October 2025, they are in principle to use Employment Selection Support first (施行 notice). A similar treatment is planned from April 2027 for Type A and for transition support beyond the standard period. Confirm the timing and exceptions in the competent local government's latest guidance.
