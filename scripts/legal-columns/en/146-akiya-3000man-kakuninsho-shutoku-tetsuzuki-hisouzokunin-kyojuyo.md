**In short:** to sell an inherited family home (a vacant house) and use the "special deduction of up to 30 million yen on the transfer of a vacant house" (Article 35, paragraph 3 of the Act on Special Measures Concerning Taxation), you must attach a "Confirmation of the Decedent's Residential House, etc." to your final tax return. This confirmation is issued by the mayor of the municipality with jurisdiction over the house and land you sold (guidance of the National Tax Agency and the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism, accessed 2026-10-31). The application for issuance is accompanied by the decedent's removed residence certificate, documents showing the date electricity, gas, or water was stopped, a copy of the sale contract, and the like. This article is general information within the scope of preparing documents submitted to a public office; it does not make the tax judgement of whether the special measure applies, nor calculate or file the tax.

## Why is the "Confirmation of the Decedent's Residential House" needed, and where do you apply?

The special deduction of up to 30 million yen on the transfer of a vacant house allows, under certain requirements, a deduction of up to 30 million yen from the transfer income when you sell a decedent's residential house (or its site) that you inherited (Article 35, paragraph 3 of the Act on Special Measures Concerning Taxation; Article 20-3 of its Enforcement Order; Article 18-2 of its Enforcement Regulation). To receive it, you are required to attach a "Confirmation of the Decedent's Residential House, etc." to your final tax return (National Tax Agency "No. 3306," accessed 2026-10-31).

The confirmation is issued not by the tax office but by the mayor of the municipality with jurisdiction over the house and land you sold. In other words, the tax filing goes to the tax office, while the confirmation that proves the facts underlying the special measure goes to the municipality. The confirmation shows that the municipality has verified, by documents, that the house was the "decedent's residential house" and that it was vacant from the time of inheritance until the time of transfer.

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Basis | Article 35, paragraph 3 of the Act on Special Measures Concerning Taxation (Enforcement Order Article 20-3; Enforcement Regulation Article 18-2) |
| Issuer of the confirmation | The mayor of the municipality with jurisdiction over the asset sold |
| Submission / use | Attached to the final tax return and submitted to the tax office (one of the requirements for the special measure) |
| Deduction | Up to 30 million yen (for transfers on or after 1 January 2024, where three or more heirs acquired the property, up to 20 million yen) |

The judgement of whether the special measure actually applies and the calculation and filing of the tax are the domain of a tax accountant. Our office (administrative scrivener) assists within the scope of preparing and submitting the documents to apply for issuance of the confirmation.

## How are the requirements for the house (year of construction, not condominium, vacant) organised?

The confirmation is issued on the premise that the house you sold meets the facts of the "decedent's residential house" that is the object of the special measure. The main requirements shown by the National Tax Agency are as follows (No. 3306, accessed 2026-10-31).

| Requirement | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Time of construction | Built on or before 31 May 1981 |
| Not a condominium | Not a building registered as a condominium (sectional ownership) |
| Residence just before inheritance | No person other than the decedent was living there immediately before the inheritance began (limited to one building mainly used by the decedent as a residence) |
| Continued vacancy | Not used for business, leasing, or residence from the time of inheritance until the time of transfer |
| Applicable period | Transfer between 1 April 2016 and 31 December 2027 |
| Transfer price | The transfer price is 100 million yen or less (judged by adding transfers by other heirs, etc.) |

Even where the decedent was admitted to a nursing home or the like immediately before the inheritance began, the case may still qualify if certain requirements are met, such as having been certified as requiring long-term care. In that case, documents showing the admission and the care certification are additionally needed. Confirm the final application of which requirement fits and the tax judgement with a tax accountant.

## What documents are attached to the application?

When you apply to the municipality for issuance of the confirmation, you attach documents such as the following to the application (the issuance guidelines of each municipality, accessed 2026-10-31; names and copies differ by municipality).

| Category | Main documents |
| --- | --- |
| Confirming inheritance and residence | The decedent's removed residence certificate; the heir's residence certificate |
| Confirming it was vacant | Documents confirming the date electricity, gas, or water was stopped, such as a stop certificate or a notice slip of discontinuance. Note that the stop date must be on or after the date the inheritance began |
| Confirming the transfer | A copy of the sale contract |
| If admitted to a nursing home | Documents showing the care certification and the fact of admission to the facility |

If the stop date of electricity, gas, or water is before the date the inheritance began, you cannot show that the house was vacant at the time of inheritance, and the confirmation may not be granted. It is safer to check the record of the stop date early. Because the required documents differ by municipality and additions may be requested, confirm with the latest guidance of the municipality where the property is located before you apply.

## How do the documents differ between demolishing before selling and seismically retrofitting before selling?

This special measure is broadly divided into (1) the type where you seismically retrofit the house (sell it as a house with earthquake resistance) and (2) the type where you demolish the house and sell only the land, and the form of the confirmation also differs by the mode of transfer.

- Seismically retrofitting before selling: it is a requirement that the house conforms to certain seismic standards at the time of transfer. For transfers on or after 1 January 2024, the case also qualifies if it comes to meet the standard by 15 February of the year following the year of transfer. A certificate of conformity to seismic standards and documents relating to the work become necessary.
- Demolishing and selling the land: the requirements must be met from the time of inheritance until the time of demolition, and from the time of demolition until the time of transfer. Documents showing the demolition of the building (such as a closed-register certificate) become necessary. For transfers on or after 1 January 2024, the case also qualifies if the demolition, etc. is carried out by 15 February of the year following the year of transfer.

| Mode of transfer | Key requirements | Main additional documents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Retrofit and sell | Conformity to seismic standards at transfer (for transfers on/after 2024, conformity by 15 February of the following year also allowed) | Certificate of conformity to seismic standards, work-related documents |
| Demolish and sell the land | Requirements from inheritance to demolition and from demolition to transfer (for transfers on/after 2024, demolition by 15 February of the following year also allowed) | Documents showing demolition of the building (such as a closed-register certificate) |

Which form applies and the sufficiency of the documents are confirmed with the municipality and a tax accountant. This article stays with information on the framework and does not apply any specific case.

## FAQ

**Q. Where can I obtain the Confirmation of the Decedent's Residential House?**
A. It is issued by the mayor of the municipality with jurisdiction over the house and land you sold (National Tax Agency "No. 3306," accessed 2026-10-31). It is not the tax office. You attach the issued confirmation to your final tax return to receive the special measure (Article 35, paragraph 3 of the Act on Special Measures Concerning Taxation). Because the counter and form differ by municipality, confirm with the latest guidance of the municipality where the property is located.

**Q. Why is the stop date of electricity or gas important?**
A. Because the special measure presupposes that the house was vacant from the time of inheritance until the time of transfer, the municipality confirms the fact that it was vacant by documents showing the date electricity, gas, or water was stopped. The stop date must be on or after the date the inheritance began; if the stop is before that date, the confirmation may not be granted. It is safer to check the record of the stop date early.

**Q. Do the documents differ between demolishing before selling and selling without demolishing?**
A. Yes. When you demolish and sell the land, documents showing the demolition of the building (such as a closed-register certificate) are needed; when you seismically retrofit and sell, a certificate of conformity to seismic standards is needed; and the form of the confirmation also differs. For transfers on or after 1 January 2024, the case also qualifies if the demolition, etc. or the conformity to seismic standards is done by 15 February of the year following the year of transfer. Confirm which applies with the municipality and a tax accountant.

**Q. Does an administrative scrivener also calculate the tax?**
