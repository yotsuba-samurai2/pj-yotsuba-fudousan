**In short:** septic-tank "installation work" and "cleaning" are separate systems, filed with separate authorities. A person who intends to run a septic-tank construction business must obtain the **registration of the prefectural governor** for the area in which the business is to be carried out (Septic Tank Act Article 21(1); the registration has a term of five years and is renewable). On the other hand, a person who intends to run a septic-tank cleaning business must obtain the **licence of the mayor** for the area (Article 35(1)). In other words, construction is "registration (prefecture)" and cleaning is "licence (municipality)": the governing articles and the windows both differ. A construction business must place a **septic-tank construction engineer** at each office (Article 29(1)). A construction business operator who holds a licence for civil-engineering works, building works or pipework works under the Construction Business Act is exempt from the registration provisions and, on starting a construction business, **notifies** the prefectural governor without delay (Article 33). ## How do the "registration" for septic-tank construction and the "licence" for septic-tank cleaning differ?

Even for the same "septic tank," installation work and cleaning are separate systems. A person who intends to run a septic-tank construction business must obtain the registration of the prefectural governor for the area in which the business is to be carried out (Septic Tank Act Article 21(1)). By contrast, a person who intends to run a septic-tank cleaning business must obtain the licence of the mayor for the area in which the business is to be carried out (Article 35(1)). Registration and a licence differ in legal nature, and the windows also split into "prefecture" and "municipality."

| Item | Septic-tank construction business | Septic-tank cleaning business |
|---|---|---|
| System | Registration (Septic Tank Act Article 21) | Licence (Septic Tank Act Article 35) |
| Filed with | Prefectural governor for the area | Mayor for the area |
| Term | Five years (renewable; Article 21(2),(3)) | Conditions/time limit may be attached (Article 35(2)) |
| Core personnel requirement | A construction engineer at each office (Article 29) | Facility and ability meeting the technical standards set by the Ministry of the Environment ordinance (Article 36) |
| If a construction-business licence is held | Special provision of Article 33 (notification suffices) | Not covered by the special provision |

The difference in nature between a "registration" and a "licence" is sorted out from the same angle in [How the demolition-work registration differs from a construction-business licence](https://luck428.com/en/legal/column/kaitai-koji-gyo-touroku-kensetsu-kyoka-chigai). Of the installation, maintenance and cleaning of a septic tank, what this article covers is the two of installation work and cleaning.

## What requirements, such as a septic-tank construction engineer, does construction-business registration need?

A registered septic-tank construction business must place a septic-tank construction engineer at each office (Septic Tank Act Article 29(1)). If an office comes to lack one, measures to comply must be taken within two weeks (Article 29(2)). And when carrying out septic-tank work, it must have a construction engineer supervise it on site, or a qualified construction-business operator must supervise it on site themselves (Article 29(3)). The construction engineer is the central qualification the law places to secure the quality of the work and its conformity with the structural standards.

A construction-engineer licence is issued by the Minister of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism to (i) a person who has passed the construction-engineer examination, and (ii) a person who, after passing the pipework-施工-management technical examination (second-stage examination) under Construction Business Act Article 27, has completed the course of a designated training body (Article 42(1)). The application for registration states, for each office, the name and licence number of the construction engineer and is submitted to the prefectural governor (Article 22). The governor registers it without delay, except where a ground for refusal applies (Article 23). A ground for disqualification (Article 24) results in refusal. Because the concrete attachments and forms depend on each prefecture's application guidance, confirm them directly on that prefecture's official page just before writing.

## What are the facility and personnel standards for a cleaning-business licence?

The septic-tank cleaning-business licence is granted by the mayor (Septic Tank Act Article 35(1)). The mayor must not grant the licence unless they find that the application meets both of: (i) the **facility for the business and the applicant's ability conform to the technical standards set by the Ministry of the Environment ordinance**, and (ii) the applicant does not fall under a ground for disqualification (Article 36). Cleaning requires equipment for drawing out the sludge and scum arising in a septic tank and carrying it on to transport and disposal (such as suction and transport equipment), and the ability to do this properly.

The substance of the technical standards is delegated to the Ministry of the Environment ordinance (the Septic Tank Act Enforcement Regulations), and the details of personnel and facilities and the operation of screening differ by municipality. The licence may have a time limit or conditions necessary for preserving the living environment and for public hygiene attached (Article 35(2)). Therefore, which equipment and how many units to prepare, and which qualified persons to assign, must be settled by directly confirming the screening standards of the municipality where the business is planned. As an environmental licence, as with [How to obtain an industrial-waste collection-and-transport licence](https://luck428.com/en/legal/column/sanpai-shushu-unpan-kyoka-torikata), showing in writing that the facility and ability meet the standards is the heart of the application.

## How are those with a construction-business licence treated under the "special provision"?

There is an important exception for construction work. For a construction business operator under Construction Business Act Article 2(3) who holds a licence for any of **civil-engineering works, building works or pipework works** in Appended Table 1 of that Act, Septic Tank Act Articles 21 to 28 (the registration provisions) and the like do not apply (Article 33(1)). When such a person runs a septic-tank construction business, they are deemed a construction business registered under Article 21(1) (Article 33(2)). This is the so-called "special septic-tank construction operator," and there is no need to obtain a fresh registration.

This does not mean no procedure at all: on starting the septic-tank construction business, they must **notify** the prefectural governor of that without delay (Article 33(3)). The same applies when there is a change in the notified matters or when the business is abolished. Conversely, when a registered septic-tank construction operator becomes a construction business operator covered by this special provision, that person's Article 21 registration loses its effect (Article 33(4)). So if your company holds a construction-business licence (any of civil-engineering, building or pipework), the entry point is "notification"; if not, it is "registration." The requirements for a construction-business licence itself are sorted out in [Construction-business licence requirements, management experience and the full-time qualified engineer](https://luck428.com/en/legal/column/kensetsugyo-kyoka-yoken-keikan-sengi-zaisan).

## FAQ

**Q. Can I file for the construction and the cleaning at the same authority?**
A. No. A septic-tank construction business is the "registration" of the prefectural governor for the area (Septic Tank Act Article 21(1)), and a septic-tank cleaning business is the "licence" of the mayor for the area (Article 35(1)): the governing articles and the filing windows differ. To do both, you must obtain registration from the prefecture and a licence from the municipality separately.

**Q. How long is a construction-business registration valid?**
A. The registration has a term of five years (Septic Tank Act Article 21(2)). To keep running the business, renewal registration is required (Article 21(3)). If the disposition on a renewal application is not made by the expiry date, the former registration remains effective until the disposition (Article 21(4)). A cleaning-business licence may have a time limit or conditions attached (Article 35(2)).

**Q. If I hold a construction-business licence, do I not need the construction registration?**
A. A construction business operator holding a licence for any of civil-engineering, building or pipework works is exempt from the registration provisions and is deemed registered (Septic Tank Act Article 33(1),(2)). However, on starting the construction business, notification to the prefectural governor is required without delay (Article 33(3)). There is no such special provision for the cleaning-business licence.

**Q. Must a septic-tank construction engineer always be placed?**
A. A septic-tank construction business must place a construction engineer at each office (Septic Tank Act Article 29(1)). When carrying out work, it must have the construction engineer supervise it on site, or a qualified construction operator supervises it themselves (Article 29(3)). The licence is issued by the Minister of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism to examination passers and the like (Article 42(1)).
