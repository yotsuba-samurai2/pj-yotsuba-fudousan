**Key point:** a venue providing customer amusement and alcohol late at night needs a specified amusement restaurant licence if it does not fall within another regulated amusement-business category. Before renting, check the permitted area, operating hours and structural standards, including at least 33 m² per guest room.

**Last updated: 2026-10-10**

This article organises business categories and property checks for clubs and restaurants with live performances or dancing. Confirm individual classification and compliance with a qualified professional and the local police; the prefectural public safety commission decides the licence.

## Which businesses require this licence?

Article 2(11) of the Act on Control and Improvement of Amusement Business (風俗営業等の規制及び業務の適正化等に関する法律; the Entertainment Business Act) covers premises equipped to provide customer amusement and food or drink, with alcohol, operating in that form during late-night hours. Businesses falling within the Act’s other amusement-business categories are excluded. Each premises requires permission under Article 31-22.

| Element | Actual operation to check |
|---|---|
| Late-night operation | Whether amusement and alcohol service are combined between midnight and 6 a.m. |
| Amusement, or yūkyō | Whether the operator actively prompts customers to enjoy amusement |
| Food/drink and alcohol | Whether customers eat or drink and receive alcohol |
| Other regulated categories | Whether customer entertainment, low lighting or other features place the venue in another category |

A venue providing amusement only by day, then stopping it and serving alcohol at night, is not classified solely because it also stays open at night. Identify separately the hours of amusement and alcohol service.

The National Police Agency’s 2025 interpretation standards explain amusement. Examples include shows or live music for unspecified customers, dancing, encouraging customers to sing karaoke, and games. Simply providing equipment customers use on their own differs from actively encouraging them. Assess the conduct, equipment and operation rather than the event name or the label “bar”.

## How does it differ from customer entertainment and late-night alcohol notification?

| Actual business | Main procedure under the Act | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Entertaining customers with personalised service while providing food/drink | Type 1 amusement-business licence | Articles 2(1)(i), 3(1) |
| Late-night amusement combined with alcohol, without that customer entertainment | Specified amusement restaurant licence | Articles 2(11), 31-22 |
| Alcohol-focused late-night service without entertainment or amusement | Within the late-night alcohol-serving restaurant notification category | Article 33(1); businesses routinely serving ordinary staple meals and other statutory exceptions are excluded |

This table addresses categories under the Entertainment Business Act. Notification does not settle Food Sanitation Act restaurant permission, fire safety, building rules or other requirements. See [Premises for a type 1 customer-entertainment restaurant](/en/legal/column/fuzoku-eigyo-1go-settai-inshoku-kyoka-bukken-kyori) and [Late-night alcohol notification](/en/legal/column/shinya-shurui-teikyo-todokede-yoken).

Low-illumination restaurants fall under Article 2(1)(ii). Item (iii) concerns partitioned-seat restaurants; these are different categories. Apply the prescribed measurement method and operating conditions when assessing low illumination. Specified amusement premises generally need to maintain lighting above 10 lux, and dimmed periods must also be discussed with the local police.

## How should operating areas and hours be checked?

The premises generally must be in an area designated by prefectural ordinance. A qualifying premises in a hotel or similar facility may have an exception, but being inside a hotel alone is insufficient.

| Item | Check before signing a lease |
|---|---|
| Area | Whether the exact address and block fall within the ordinance and designation notice |
| Protected facilities | Local facility categories, distances and measurement method, including schools and hospitals |
| Hours | Hours prohibited by the prefectural ordinance |
| Hotel exception | Whether the specific National Public Safety Commission rule conditions are met |

The Enforcement Order (風俗営業等の規制及び業務の適正化等に関する法律施行令) provides a framework for ordinance-designated zones extending up to roughly 100 metres around protected-facility sites. This does not impose a uniform nationwide 100 m separation requirement. There is no single nationwide distance or closing-time figure to apply. Kyoto Police’s notice updated on 28 September 2026 gives a 5–6 a.m. prohibition and a 70 m protected-facility distance for the relevant category. These illustrate local differences and must not be transferred to another location. The target property’s ordinance, designation and actual surroundings remain unverified. Give the local police community safety section the proposed operation and precise address. Restrictions on minors and alcohol service require separate checks.

## What are the 33 m² room and other structural standards?

The main checks under Article 75 of the Enforcement Regulation (風俗営業等の規制及び業務の適正化等に関する法律施行規則) are below. Total premises area and guest-room area are different measurements.

| Item | Basic requirement or check |
|---|---|
| Guest-room area | At least 33 m² for each guest room |
| Lighting | Structure or equipment that maintains lighting inside the premises above 10 lux |
| Visibility | No equipment obstructing sight lines inside guest rooms |
| Door locks | No locking equipment on guest-room entrances; entrances leading directly outside the premises are excepted |
| Other equipment | Check prohibitions on equipment harmful to the public-morals environment, and noise/vibration standards |

During late-night operation, lighting generally must remain above 10 lux. The NPA standards also contain a time-condition provision for rooms where amusement occurs only at the seats: periods at 10 lux or below must be less than half of each late-night operating period. Distinguish the structural standard from operating rules and do not apply the provision on your own. Confirm guest-room boundaries, area calculation, light-measurement positions, operational settings and lock exceptions against current rules with the local police. Match plans to actual conditions before leasing or building interiors. Area and lighting alone do not guarantee a licence.

## What documents and application steps are involved?

Discuss the operation, location and structure in advance, prepare documents and apply to the commission through the local police. Document examination and site checks precede the decision. The full attachments, processing period and specific disqualification conditions following the 2025 amendments must be checked for the location and applicant; all of these have not been verified here.

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Operation | Application form and descriptions of the business and amusement methods |
| Premises | Floor plan, area-calculation drawing, lighting/sound plans and evidence of the right to use the premises |
| Applicant | Resident record, declarations and, for a company, articles and registration certificate |

## FAQ

**Q. What if amusement is provided by day but only alcohol is served at night?**
A. Remaining open late does not alone establish this classification. Check whether late-night operation combines amusement with alcohol and whether another regulated category applies. Without late-night amusement, assess late-night alcohol notification or another category according to the actual operation.

**Q. Is simply providing karaoke equipment amusement?**
A. Providing equipment customers use on their own differs from actively encouraging them to sing. Confirm staff conduct and event operation against the Agency’s interpretation standards with local police.

**Q. Can I open with only a late-night alcohol notification?**
A. That notification concerns a category under the Entertainment Business Act. Customer entertainment or late-night amusement may require a different licence. Restaurant permission under the Food Sanitation Act, fire and building requirements also need separate confirmation.

**Q. Are 33 m² rooms and compliant lighting sufficient anywhere?**
A. No. In addition to at least 33 m² per guest room and the general requirement to maintain lighting above 10 lux, check the local permitted area, distances and prohibited hours, other equipment standards and disqualification grounds. Area and lighting compliance alone do not guarantee permission.
