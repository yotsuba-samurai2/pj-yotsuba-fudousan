**In short:** with the shift from Technical Intern Training to Employment for Skill Development, an existing "supervising organisation" does not automatically slide over into a "supervising-and-support organisation"; it must newly obtain permission as a supervising-and-support organisation (Immigration Services Agency, "Employment for Skill Development Q&A," Q33). The system's enforcement date is 1 April 2027 (Reiwa 9), based on the amending Act relating to Employment for Skill Development (Act No. 60 of 2024). So that procedures can proceed without waiting for enforcement, pre-enforcement applications for supervising-and-support organisation permission have been accepted from 15 April 2026 (Reiwa 8), and pre-enforcement applications for certification of the (supervision-type) Employment for Skill Development plan from 1 September 2026 (the application point being the Head Office Examination Division annex of the Organization for Technical Intern Training). A holder of supervising-and-support organisation permission is stated to be deemed to hold general supervising business permission under the Technical Intern Training system (same Q&A, Q39). ## When shifting to Employment for Skill Development, how does a Technical Intern Training supervising organisation's position change?

Employment for Skill Development is an acceptance framework newly created in place of Technical Intern Training, with the aims of securing and developing human resources. Its basis is the amending Act relating to Employment for Skill Development (Act No. 60 of 2024, promulgated 21 June 2024), which amends the Act on Proper Technical Intern Training and Protection of Technical Intern Trainees and others. The residence status for acceptance is placed within Appended Table I of the Immigration Control and Refugee Recognition Act.

The role corresponding to the "supervising organisation" that supervised accepting bodies under Technical Intern Training is, under Employment for Skill Development, carried by the "supervising-and-support organisation." It is not merely a change of name; the permission framework itself switches to that of the new system. The Immigration Services Agency's guidance gives the start of the system's operation (enforcement date) as 1 April 2027 (Reiwa 9). The starting point, therefore, is that a Technical Intern Training supervising organisation cannot simply continue to operate as a supervising-and-support organisation after the enforcement date. For how this differs from a registered support organisation (the outsourced-support body under Specified Skilled Worker), see [Registration of a registered support organisation and the support plan](https://luck428.com/legal/column/toroku-shien-kikan-touroku-shien-keikaku).

## Must you newly obtain supervising-and-support organisation permission, and are there transitional measures?

Yes, you must newly obtain permission. The Immigration Services Agency's "Employment for Skill Development Q&A," Q33, states that, to perform work relating to Employment for Skill Development, a supervising organisation must newly obtain supervising-and-support organisation permission. The qualification is not carried over automatically.

At the same time, a treatment to avoid a gap across enforcement is shown. Q39 of the same Q&A states that a person who has obtained supervising-and-support organisation permission is deemed to hold general supervising business permission under the Technical Intern Training system, so no separate renewal is required for the supervising organisation's permission period.

| Item | Content (Immigration Services Agency guidance) |
| --- | --- |
| Enforcement date | 1 April 2027 (Reiwa 9) |
| Pre-enforcement application for supervising-and-support organisation permission | Accepted from 15 April 2026 (application point: Head Office Examination Division annex, Organization for Technical Intern Training) |
| Pre-enforcement application for certification of the (supervision-type) plan | Accepted from 1 September 2026 |
| Treatment of an existing supervising organisation | No automatic shift; new supervising-and-support organisation permission required (Q33) |
| If permission is obtained | Deemed to hold general supervising business permission under Technical Intern Training (Q39) |

The acceptance period for pre-enforcement applications, and the rough time needed from application to the start of operations, may change with operational updates. When considering an application, be sure to confirm the latest acceptance status on the official pages noted below.

## What does certification of the Employment for Skill Development (acceptance) plan examine?

Under Employment for Skill Development, a mechanism is set up whereby the Employment for Skill Development plan prepared by the accepting body (such as a company) is certified. This corresponds to the certification of the technical intern training plan under Technical Intern Training. Certification of the plan is expected to examine the premises for proper acceptance — such as the field and work covered by the development, the development period and goals, the acceptance structure, and remuneration. It is also stated that obtaining certification of a supervision-type plan presupposes supervising-and-support organisation permission (including a pre-enforcement application).

That said, the detailed examination items, criteria and forms are in part to be made concrete in the operational guidelines towards enforcement, and must be confirmed within the range that has been settled and published. This article shows, as what could be confirmed from primary information, the broad outline of the examination (that a plan-certification system is established and is linked with supervising-and-support organisation permission), and leaves the individual criteria values and the details of forms to the latest published materials. ## FAQ

**Q. Can the current supervising organisation become a supervising-and-support organisation as it is?**
A. It cannot shift as it is. The Immigration Services Agency's "Employment for Skill Development Q&A," Q33, states that performing work relating to Employment for Skill Development requires newly obtaining supervising-and-support organisation permission. However, once permission is obtained, the holder is deemed to hold general supervising business permission under Technical Intern Training, and no separate renewal is required for the supervising organisation's permission (Q39).

**Q. When is the enforcement date, and can you apply before enforcement?**
A. The system's enforcement date is 1 April 2027 (Reiwa 9). So that things can proceed without waiting for enforcement, pre-enforcement applications for supervising-and-support organisation permission have been accepted from 15 April 2026 (Reiwa 8), and pre-enforcement applications for certification of the (supervision-type) Employment for Skill Development plan from 1 September 2026 (the application point being the Head Office Examination Division annex of the Organization for Technical Intern Training). Because the acceptance period may change, confirm the latest status in the Organization's guidance before applying.

**Q. Can an administrative scrivener handle the permission application or plan certification?**
A. Supporting the drafting of application documents and immigration filing-agency for residence status are within the scope of an administrative scrivener who has filed the filing-agent notification. On the other hand, labour design such as work rules, wages and social insurance is the work of a labour and social security attorney, and representation in a labour dispute is the work of a lawyer. You contract with each as an independent business separately.

**Q. Can you judge whether permission or certification will be granted for an individual acceptance?**
A. This article is general information and does not determine or guarantee whether an individual permission or certification will be granted. Examination criteria and forms are in part to be made concrete in the operational guidelines; please base your judgment on the latest settled and published materials and, where needed, on confirmation by a qualified professional.
