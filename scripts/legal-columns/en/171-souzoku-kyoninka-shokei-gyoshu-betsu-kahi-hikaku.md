**In short:** after a sole proprietor dies, gather the permits and build a deadline checklist using the date of death. Separate construction applications within 30 days, hotel and regulated-entertainment applications within 60 days, and prompt food-business notifications.

## What should the family put on the handover checklist?

This page is for relatives managing the immediate aftermath of the death of an individually licensed business owner. For the general distinction between succession and obtaining a new permit, read the [business-licence succession overview](/en/legal/column/souzoku-jigyo-shokei-kyoninka-shoukei-saishutoku).

Collect permits, renewal notices and previous applications. Check whether the holder is the deceased individual or a company: a company representative's death is not the same procedure. Record the permit name and number, holder, authority, death date, application or notification, deadline, documents being prepared, and receipt record.

## Which deadlines run from the death?

| Business | Procedure and deadline | Initial contact |
|---|---|---|
| Construction, continuing the whole business | Apply within 30 days after death (建設業法, Construction Business Act, Article 17-3) | Licensing authority |
| Hotels and inns | Apply for approval within 60 days after death (旅館業法, Article 3-4) | Local public-health authority |
| Regulated entertainment business | Apply for approval within 60 days after death (風営法, Article 7) | Police station responsible for the premises |
| Food business | Notify succession without delay (食品衛生法, Article 56) | Public health centre |

These are application deadlines, not deadlines for completion of approval. Do not substitute the funeral date or the date heirs agree on estate division for the death date. Confirm the actual last day, holiday rules, required attachments and submission method with each authority. Keep evidence of receipt; a consultation alone is not a completed application. “Without delay” does not give a food business a 30- or 60-day grace period.

## Which documents should relatives gather?

- Permits, renewal notices and prior applications to identify the holder and authority.
- Evidence of the death date and family relationships; ask which family-register records or alternatives the particular procedure accepts.
- The proposed successor and the status of agreement among heirs; obtain the prescribed consent form where required.
- Current staffing, qualifications and premises information, so the authority can identify any permit requirement affected by the death.

This is a preparation checklist, not a universal statutory attachment list. Bunkyo City's food-business guidance lists family-register evidence or a certified statutory inheritance information chart, and consent where there are multiple heirs. Do not assume those documents suffice for other industries; check each current application form.

## What if documents are missing or the family wants to keep trading?

Do not wait for a final successor decision before checking deadlines. Tell the authority what is missing. A telephone enquiry does not itself extend the deadline, and filing does not guarantee approval. Construction, hotel and regulated-entertainment laws provide deemed-permit arrangements following an application, but the actual application and requirements need individual checking. Other businesses may need a fresh permit. Possessing the deceased's permit is not enough to decide that trading can continue.

## Who do you hand succession applications, registration and tax to (separate engagement)?

Inheritance succession of a licence overlaps, besides the procedure with the licensing authority, with the inheritance procedures themselves (registration, tax, disputes). The roles divide as follows:

- Preparing and acting for the approval/consent-approval/notification applications for each industry's succession, fresh-licence applications for industries that cannot be succeeded, confirming the heirs and preparing the estate-division agreement → 四葉行政書士事務所 (administrative scrivener)
- Inheritance registration, company officer-change registration and the like → a judicial scrivener
- Inheritance-tax filing, and tax on business assets and unlisted shares → a tax accountant
- Disputes over estate division among heirs, and objections to adverse dispositions such as non-approval → an attorney
- Confirming title and the post-inheritance lease or sale of business real estate (shops, garages, materials yards, etc.) → 四葉不動産株式会社 (a separate business)

What an administrative scrivener handles is the "application to the authority" part: approval, consent-approval, notification and the fresh licence. Registration is by a judicial scrivener, tax by a tax accountant, and disputes and objections by an attorney, each as an independent business under a separate contract. 四葉行政書士事務所 and 四葉不動産株式会社 are separate businesses. ## FAQ

**Q. What date starts the checklist?**
A. The construction, hotel and regulated-entertainment application periods above run after the owner’s death. Do not wait for the funeral or estate division; confirm the exact last day and submission method with the authority.

**Q. Does a food business have 30 days to notify?**
A. No. Article 56 requires notification without delay, not a 30-day grace period. Confirm the form and attachments with the public health centre.

**Q. Are family-register records and consent forms the same for every industry?**
A. Not necessarily. Do not reuse food-business guidance as the requirements for construction or regulated entertainment. Check each current form and distinguish preparation materials from mandatory attachments.

**Q. Can I ask for the succession application, inheritance registration and inheritance tax together?**
A. You can consult at one window, but the contracts are separate. The licence succession and fresh application are by an administrative scrivener, registration of real estate and the company by a judicial scrivener, inheritance tax by a tax accountant, and disputes among heirs by an attorney, each as an independent business under a separate contract. 四葉行政書士事務所 and 四葉不動産株式会社 are separate businesses, and our office receives no referral fee.
