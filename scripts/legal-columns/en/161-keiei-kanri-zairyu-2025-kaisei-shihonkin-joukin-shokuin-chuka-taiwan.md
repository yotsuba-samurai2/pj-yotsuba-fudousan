**In short:** the "Business Manager" status of residence had its criteria raised by the reform of the landing-criteria ministerial ordinance effective 16 October 2025 (Reiwa 7). The main changes are capital or total investment of 30 million yen or more, the employment of one or more full-time employees, Japanese ability of the applicant or a full-time employee (a level at which one can understand and use Japanese highly independently = CEFR B2 level or above), three or more years of management experience or a degree in a related field, and expert confirmation of the business plan by a small-business consultant, certified public accountant, tax accountant and the like. For those already residing under "Business Manager," there is a transitional measure until 16 October 2028 (Reiwa 10), three years after the effective date, and a renewal is not refused solely because the new criteria are not met. The basis is Appended Table I-2 "Business Manager" of the Immigration Control and Refugee Recognition Act and the Ministerial Ordinance setting the criteria under Article 7, paragraph 1, item 2 of that Act. ## What changed for the Business Manager visa in October 2025?

The "Business Manager" status of residence corresponds to the activity in Appended Table I-2 of the Immigration Control and Refugee Recognition Act, "to operate a trade or other business, or to engage in the management of that business, in Japan." The criteria required for this status are set in the Ministerial Ordinance setting the criteria under Article 7, paragraph 1, item 2 of that Act (the landing-criteria ordinance), and the reform ordinance effective 16 October 2025 (Reiwa 7) raised their content. The Immigration Services Agency also issued, on the same date, guidance covering the online-application input method and the treatment of affiliated-organization categories.

Previously the level was capital/total investment of 5 million yen or more (or two or more full-time employees); after the reform the amount rose substantially, and qualitative requirements such as Japanese ability, management experience and expert confirmation of the business plan were added. Those who newly start a business and apply have the post-reform criteria applied directly. The full picture of a new application, including company establishment and securing an office, is organized in [how a Chinese or Taiwanese entrepreneur obtains the "Business Manager" status of residence](https://luck428.com/legal/column/keieikanri-zairyu-chuka-kigyousha-kaisha-setsuritsu), and the continuity of the business asked at renewal is organized in [proving the "continuity and substance of the business" at the renewal of a Business Manager visa](https://luck428.com/legal/column/keieikanri-koshin-jigyo-keizokusei-rissho-chuka-taiwan). This article focuses on the time-dependent issues of the new criteria and the transitional measure of the October 2025 reform.

| Item | Pre-reform guide | Post-reform (effective 16 October 2025) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Capital/total investment | 5 million yen or more (or two or more full-time employees) | 30 million yen or more |
| Full-time employees | Alternative option of two or more | Employment of one or more required |
| Japanese ability | No explicit criterion | Applicant or a full-time employee at CEFR B2 level or above |
| Management experience/degree | No explicit criterion | Three or more years of management experience or a degree in a related field |
| Business plan | Submission | Confirmation by a small-business consultant, CPA, tax accountant, etc. |

## How are the 30-million-yen capital requirement and the full-time employee requirement judged?

Capital/total investment is confirmed by the company registration and the substance of the investment. The premise is that it can be explained as property devoted to the business, not window-dressing money, and you may be asked for materials showing the source of the funds. A form that merely dresses up the face amount while lacking substance is viewed negatively, also from the purpose of the reform. The payment of capital and the registration of a capital increase are the field of the judicial scrivener, and the tax treatment is the field of the tax accountant.

A full-time employee was positioned as a requirement of "employment of one or more" by the reform. The scope of who can be employed, the judgment of "full-time," the prescribed working hours and the application of social insurance need to be confirmed from both the Immigration Services Agency's guideline and the practice of labor and social insurance. The procedures for the social and employment insurance of full-time employees are handled by a labor and social security attorney. For the details — how exactly the number is counted, and which status-of-residence holders may be counted as full-time employees — be sure to confirm the guideline text. This article does not go into individual countability that cannot be verified.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Capital/total investment of 30 million yen or more | Substance of the investment/payment, source of funds, allocation to the business | Judicial scrivener (registration), tax accountant (tax) |
| One or more full-time employees | Real existence of employment, full-time nature, application of social insurance | Labor and social security attorney (application procedures) |
| Securing an office | Real existence of an independent office, no sharing with the residence | Real estate (a separate business) |

## Who is the Japanese-ability and the expert confirmation of the business plan required of?

For Japanese ability, either the applicant themselves or a full-time employee is required to meet a "level at which one can understand and use Japanese highly independently" (CEFR B2 level) or above. A field to enter whether there is a manager or full-time employee at this level has also been added to the Immigration Services Agency's online-application form. It is designed so that either the person meets it or a full-time employee meets it, but how exactly it is proved (the treatment of tests such as the Japanese-Language Proficiency Test) is shown in the guideline, so confirm it in the latest guidance before submission.

The business plan must not only be submitted but also receive the confirmation of an expert. Those said to be able to handle the confirmation are a small-business consultant, a certified public accountant, a tax accountant and the like. It is a mechanism in which a third-party expert looks at the feasibility of the business plan, and the appropriateness of the figures and premises of the plan is examined. The administrative scrivener handles the preparation of the application documents and the filing for the status of residence, but the expert confirmation of the business plan and the determination of the settlement and tax amount are each the field of an expert who is an independent business. ## What about the transitional measure (until October 2028) for those already residing?

Those already residing under "Business Manager" have a transitional measure. According to the Immigration Services Agency's guidance, during the period until the day on which three years pass from the effective date of 16 October 2025 — that is, until 16 October 2028 (Reiwa 10) — a renewal of the period of stay is not refused solely because the post-reform new criteria (capital/total investment of 30 million yen or more, management experience/degree, employment of full-time employees, and the like) are not met. It is a cushioning measure so that the impact of the reform is not felt all at once.

However, the understanding "you need do nothing until 2028" is not accurate. The transitional measure defers the application of the new criteria; the points confirmed at renewal as before — that there is substance to the management, that public dues (corporation tax, consumption tax, inhabitant tax, etc.) and social insurance are fulfilled, and that an independent office is secured — are still looked at, separately from the transitional measure. The thinking on the continuity of the business at renewal is treated in detail in the aforementioned [proving the "continuity and substance of the business" at the renewal of a Business Manager visa](https://luck428.com/legal/column/keieikanri-koshin-jigyo-keizokusei-rissho-chuka-taiwan).

| Category | Treatment during the transitional period (until 16 October 2028) |
| --- | --- |
| New application (certification/change) | The post-reform new criteria apply |
| Renewal for those residing (formal requirements of the new criteria) | Not refused solely because the new criteria are not met |
| Management substance/public dues/social insurance/office | Confirmed as before, separately from the transitional measure |

## FAQ

**Q. I currently reside under "Business Manager" with capital of 5 million yen. Must I raise it to 30 million yen right away, or I cannot renew?**
A. For the renewal of those already residing under "Business Manager," there is a transitional measure until 16 October 2028 (Reiwa 10). During this period, a renewal is not refused solely because the new criteria such as capital/total investment of 30 million yen or more are not met. However, the substance of management, the fulfillment of public dues and social insurance, and securing an independent office are confirmed as before, separately from the transitional measure. Confirm the scope and details of the transitional measure in the guideline.

**Q. Does the president themselves have to meet the Japanese-ability requirement?**
A. It is designed so that either the applicant themselves or a full-time employee meets CEFR B2 level or above (a level at which one can understand and use Japanese highly independently). Even if the person is unsure about Japanese, there may be room to handle it if there is a full-time employee who meets the requirement. The specifics of which test and score prove it are shown in the guideline, so confirm the latest guidance before submission.

**Q. May I prepare the business plan myself?**
A. After the reform, the business plan is required to receive the confirmation of an expert such as a small-business consultant, CPA or tax accountant. It is a mechanism in which the figures and premises of the plan are looked at by a third party's eyes. The administrative scrivener handles the preparation and filing of the status-of-residence application, but the expert confirmation of the business plan is performed by an expert who is an independent business, contracted separately. They receive no referral fee from one another.

**Q. May the full-time employee be a foreigner?**
A. The reform made the employment of one or more full-time employees a requirement, but the details — the scope of who can be employed, how "full-time" is counted, and the kinds of status of residence that can be counted — are set in the Immigration Services Agency's guideline. This article does not assert individual countability that cannot be verified. The application procedures for full-time employees' social and employment insurance are handled by a labor and social security attorney, and the final eligibility is by the Immigration examination.
