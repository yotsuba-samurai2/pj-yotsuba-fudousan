**In short:** for holders admitted under the pre-reform Business Manager criteria, the transitional period to 16 October 2028 does not guarantee renewal. Prepare evidence of current operations separately from plans to meet the revised criteria.

This article concerns existing residents preparing renewal evidence in response to the October 2025 reform. For incorporation and initial applications, see the [Business Manager application overview](/en/legal/column/keieikanri-zairyu-chuka-kigyousha-kaisha-setsuritsu). For ordinary evidence of business continuity, see the [renewal evidence guide](/en/legal/column/keieikanri-koshin-jigyo-keizokusei-rissho-chuka-taiwan). It does not determine individual eligibility or guarantee permission.

## What does the transitional period to 16 October 2028 mean for existing residents?

Those already residing under "Business Manager" have a transitional measure. According to the Immigration Services Agency's guidance, during the period until the day on which three years pass from the effective date of 16 October 2025 — that is, until 16 October 2028 (Reiwa 10) — renewal is assessed in light of the business situation and the prospect of meeting the new criteria, even if those criteria are not yet met. It is a cushioning measure so that the impact of the reform is not felt all at once.

However, the understanding "you need do nothing until 2028" is not accurate. The transitional measure does not guarantee renewal and the prospect of meeting the new criteria is also examined; the points confirmed at renewal as before — that there is substance to the management, that public dues (corporation tax, consumption tax, inhabitant tax, etc.) and social insurance are fulfilled, and that an independent office is secured — are still looked at, separately from the transitional measure. The thinking on the continuity of the business at renewal is treated in detail in the aforementioned [proving the "continuity and substance of the business" at the renewal of a Business Manager visa](/en/legal/column/keieikanri-koshin-jigyo-keizokusei-rissho-chuka-taiwan).

| Category | Treatment during the transitional period (until 16 October 2028) |
| --- | --- |
| New application (certification/change) | The revised criteria generally apply; check the pre-reform application and start-up certificate exceptions |
| Renewal for those residing (formal requirements of the new criteria) | Assessed using the business situation and the prospect of meeting the new criteria |
| Management substance/public dues/social insurance/office | Confirmed as before, separately from the transitional measure |

## How should renewal evidence distinguish current results from plans to meet the new criteria?

Start with the residence expiry date, the previous application and the affiliated-organization category, then consult the official renewal checklist. The following are preparation examples, not documents required in every case. Match changes since the previous application to their dates, reasons and evidence.

| Topic | Examples of current evidence | Preparation for revised criteria |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business performance | Latest annual financial statements; explanation of management activities during the current residence period | Reconcile changes since the previous application and figures in the plan |
| Capital | Registry certificate and records supporting payment or investment | Identify the shortfall, funding, timing and progress; do not present a planned increase as completed |
| Staffing and Japanese ability | Employment and attendance records, status evidence and Japanese-ability evidence | Assess staffing and language separately; distinguish planned hires from actual employment |
| Taxes, insurance and permits | Evidence of tax payments, insurance enrollment and payment, and required business permits | Document missing records or unfulfilled duties and actual corrective steps |
| Qualifications and business plan | Relevant degree or experience evidence; business plan and expert confirmation | Distinguish applicable criteria from outstanding preparation and its timing |

A timetable alone does not ensure renewal. Check consistency among the explanation, accounts, registry, employment and tax records. Follow current immigration guidance on category-specific omissions and additional evidence.

## How do renewal preparations differ from the criteria for new applications?

New applications generally undergo assessment under the revised criteria. The transition for existing residents is not a deferral of capital or staffing requirements for new applicants. This comparison identifies gaps for renewal planning rather than providing another initial-application guide.

| Item | Pre-reform guide | Post-reform (effective 16 October 2025) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Capital/total investment | 5 million yen or more (or two or more full-time employees) | 30 million yen or more |
| Full-time employees | Alternative option of two or more | Employment of one or more required |
| Japanese ability | No explicit criterion | Applicant or a full-time employee at B2 under Japan’s Reference Framework for Japanese-Language Education level or above |
| Management experience/degree | Management activities already required at least three years of relevant experience, etc. | Three or more years of management experience or a doctoral, master’s or professional degree in management or a field relevant to the business |
| Business plan | Submission | Confirmation by a Japanese-qualified SME management consultant, CPA or tax accountant |

The 30-million-yen criterion is not annual turnover. For a company, it means paid-in capital of a stock company or total contributions of a membership company; operating expenses cannot simply be added to its capital. For a sole proprietor, it is the total invested in the business, including premises, one year of employee salaries and equipment. Both the financial criterion and employment of at least one eligible full-time employee are required.

The required full-time employee must reside in Japan, be someone other than the person operating or managing the business, and be a Japanese national, Special Permanent Resident, or holder of an Appended Table II status: Permanent Resident, Spouse or Child of Japanese National, Spouse or Child of Permanent Resident, or Long-Term Resident. Employing only holders of Appended Table I work or other statuses does not satisfy this staffing requirement. Full-time status and actual employment require individual verification; a labor and social security attorney handles insurance procedures.

For the separate Japanese-language criterion, a full-time employee may also hold an Appended Table I status. Either the applicant or a full-time employee can satisfy the criterion; B2 is not required of everyone.

The reviewers must hold Japanese qualifications as a Small and Medium Enterprise Management Consultant, certified public accountant or tax accountant. They check specificity, reasonableness and feasibility. Officers or employees of the applicant’s company cannot act as reviewers, but external advisory CPAs or tax accountants can.

## FAQ

**Q. Can I wait until October 2028 to prepare?**
A. No. The transition is neither guaranteed renewal nor exemption from applying. Prepare to explain current operations and prospects of meeting the revised criteria at each renewal.

**Q. How should I describe a planned capital increase or hire?**
A. Separate completed actions from plans, with supporting information on timing, funding and progress. Do not describe future plans as requirements already satisfied.

**Q. Do taxes and insurance still matter during the transition?**
A. The transition does not waive public obligations. Tax, insurance, labor-law compliance and required permits remain relevant. Check the current category-specific document guidance.

**Q. Will submitting everything in this table secure renewal?**
A. No. These are preparation examples. Required and additional documents depend on the application and organization category, and immigration authorities decide the outcome.
