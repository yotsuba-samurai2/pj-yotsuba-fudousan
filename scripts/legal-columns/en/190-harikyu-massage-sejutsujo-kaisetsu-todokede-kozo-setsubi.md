**In short:** When you open an acupuncture, moxibustion or anma-massage-shiatsu facility, file a notification within 10 days of opening, stating the location and the names of the practitioners working there, to the prefectural governor for the area where the facility is located (the mayor or ward head in cities and special wards with a public health center), under Article 9-2(1) of the Act on Practitioners of Anma, Massage and Shiatsu, Judo Therapy, etc. (the acupuncture/moxibustion law). The facility must meet the standards in the Enforcement Regulation (Article 25 requires a dedicated treatment room of at least 6.6 m²). A judo-therapy (sekkotsuin) clinic is a separate notification under a separate law.

## When and where do you file the opening notification?

The notification is filed after opening, not before. Article 9-2(1) of the Act requires a person who has opened a facility to notify, within 10 days of opening, the location, the names of the practitioners working there and other matters prescribed by Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare ordinance, to the prefectural governor for the area where the facility is located. In a city or special ward that maintains a public health center, the notification goes to the mayor or ward head (parenthetical in Article 8(1)).

A change to the notified matters must likewise be notified (Article 9-2(1), latter part). When the facility is suspended or closed, notify within 10 days of that day; the same applies when a suspended facility reopens (Article 9-2(2)). This is a post-opening notification, not a prior license. The counter, the form and the attachments (a copy of the practitioner's license, a floor plan, and so on) vary by municipality, so confirm with the public health center with jurisdiction right before you file.

| Occasion | Deadline | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Opening the facility | Within 10 days of opening | Act, Art. 9-2(1) |
| Change to notified matters | Within 10 days of the change | Act, Art. 9-2(1), latter part |
| Suspension, closure, reopening | Within 10 days of that day | Act, Art. 9-2(2) |
| Start of travel-only practice | On starting | Act, Art. 9-3 |

A clinic opened by a physician follows a different law. For the idea of a medical clinic's opening notification, see [the clinic opening notification (Medical Care Act, Article 8)](https://luck428.com/en/legal/column/shinryojo-kaisetsu-todokede-iryoho-8jo).

## What must the facility (floor area, ventilation, disinfection, partitioning) meet?

The structure and equipment of a facility must conform to the standards prescribed by ordinance (Act, Article 9-5(1)). Those standards are set out in Article 25 of the Enforcement Regulation: (1) a dedicated treatment room of at least 6.6 m²; (2) a waiting room of at least 3.3 m²; (3) the treatment room must be able to open at least one-seventh of its floor area to the outside air (unless there is an adequate ventilation device in its place); and (4) a facility to disinfect the instruments and the hands used in treatment.

The person opening the facility must also take the sanitary measures prescribed by ordinance (Act, Article 9-5(2)). Those measures appear in Article 26 of the Enforcement Regulation: (1) keep the facility clean at all times; and (2) provide adequate daylight, lighting and ventilation.

| Standard | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Dedicated treatment room | At least 6.6 m² | Enf. Reg. Art. 25(i) |
| Waiting room | At least 3.3 m² | Enf. Reg. Art. 25(ii) |
| Ventilation | One-seventh of floor area opens to outside air (or an equivalent device) | Enf. Reg. Art. 25(iii) |
| Disinfection | Disinfection facility for instruments and hands | Enf. Reg. Art. 25(iv) |
| Sanitary measures | Keep clean at all times; adequate daylight, lighting, ventilation | Enf. Reg. Art. 26 |

The prefectural governor may require reports and have officials inspect the premises (Act, Article 10). Where the structure does not meet the standards, the governor may, for a fixed period, restrict or prohibit use of all or part of the facility or order improvements (Act, Article 11(2)). The ordinance figures are nationwide minimums, but matters such as fixed-wall partitioning and independence from a residence or shop are often made concrete in municipal guidance, so bring a floor plan and consult the public health center in advance. The approach for barber and beauty shops is also instructive: see [opening notification and facility standards for barber and beauty shops](https://luck428.com/en/legal/column/biyoushojo-kaisetsu-todokede-kozo-setsubi-kijun).

## How far are travel-only practice and advertising allowed?

A practitioner who works solely by making house calls does not file a facility opening notification; instead, on starting the work, the practitioner notifies the prefectural governor for the area of residence (Act, Article 9-3). Suspension, closure and reopening follow the same rule.

Advertising is tightly restricted. Article 7(1) of the Act prohibits advertising, for anma, massage, shiatsu, acupuncture or moxibustion businesses or their facilities, anything other than: (1) the fact that one is a practitioner and the practitioner's name and address; (2) the type of business under Article 1; (3) the facility's name, telephone number and location; (4) the days or hours of treatment; and (5) other matters designated by the Minister. Even when advertising these items, the content must not extend to the practitioner's skill, methods or career (Article 7(2)). Phrases such as "effective for..." or "master physician" can run into this restriction.

A person without a license under Article 1 is, in principle, prohibited from engaging in acts resembling medical practice as a business (Act, Article 12). Advertising and client-gathering by unlicensed persons raises issues under this provision. Whether a given expression is permissible depends on the wording and the medium, so check specific cases with a licensed person and the public health center with jurisdiction.

## How does the notification differ from a judo-therapy (sekkotsuin) clinic?

A sekkotsuin or honetsugi clinic (judo therapy) is a different national qualification governed by a different law, the Judo Therapists Act. Its facility opening notification is set out in Article 19(1) of that Act: within 10 days of opening, notify the location, the names of the judo therapists working there and other matters to the prefectural governor. The structure is governed by Article 20 and advertising by Article 24 — a separate scheme running parallel to the acupuncture law.

The scope of work also differs. A judo therapist must not, except with a physician's consent, treat the affected area of a dislocation or fracture (Judo Therapists Act, Article 17; first aid is excepted). So if one shop offers both acupuncture/moxibustion and judo therapy, each may need its own notification — under Article 9-2 of the acupuncture law and Article 19 of the Judo Therapists Act. How many notifications are required depends on who performs which treatment under which license, so settle the license types and the content of treatment before deciding the notification structure.

| Item | Acupuncture, moxibustion, anma-massage-shiatsu | Sekkotsuin (judo therapy) |
|---|---|---|
| Governing law | Act on Practitioners of Anma, Massage and Shiatsu, etc. | Judo Therapists Act |
| Opening notification | Art. 9-2 (within 10 days) | Art. 19 (within 10 days) |
| Facility standards | Art. 9-5 / Enf. Reg. Art. 25 | Art. 20 |
| Advertising restriction | Art. 7 | Art. 24 |

## FAQ

**Q. Can I file the opening notification before I lease the premises?**
A. The opening notification is filed by "a person who has opened a facility" within 10 days of opening (Act, Article 9-2(1)). In practice, interior work that meets the facility standards (Enforcement Regulation, Article 25) comes first, so it is common to bring a floor plan to the public health center at the contract and fit-out stage, confirm the requirements, then proceed.

**Q. Can I use a room in my home as a treatment room?**
A. The ordinance requires a dedicated treatment room of at least 6.6 m², a waiting room of at least 3.3 m², ventilation and a disinfection facility (Enforcement Regulation, Article 25). How to partition from the residence, and the handling of fixed walls and entrances, are often made concrete in municipal guidance, so bring a floor plan and confirm with the public health center.

**Q. Can I put "effective for stiff shoulders and back pain" on my sign?**
A. Article 7 of the Act limits what may be advertised and prohibits content extending to skill, methods or career. Expressions claiming efficacy can run into this restriction. Whether a specific expression is permissible varies by medium, so confirm with a licensed person and the public health center with jurisdiction.
