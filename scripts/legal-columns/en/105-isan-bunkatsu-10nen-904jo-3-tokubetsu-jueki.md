**In short:** inheritances that began before the reform have a transition lasting at least through 31 March 2028. Estate division is still possible after ten years. Check the date of death, any court petition and any agreement among all heirs before deciding how the rule applies.

**Information checked: 10 October 2026.** This article focuses on the transition for estates that remained undivided when the reform took effect in April 2023. For special benefits and contributions generally, see the [existing explanation](/en/legal/column/souzoku-tokubetsu-jueki-kiyobun); for other deadlines, see the [inheritance deadline guide](/en/legal/column/souzoku-kigen-matome).

## Does the ten-year rule also apply to older inheritances?

Civil Code Article 904-3 provides that estate division after ten years from the opening of the inheritance generally uses statutory or designated shares rather than adjusted shares reflecting special benefits and contributions. Special benefits are addressed in Article 903 and contributions in Article 904-2. The ten-year rule does not make estate division itself impossible.

The provision was introduced by Act No. 24 of 2021 and took effect on 1 April 2023. It also applies to earlier inheritances, subject to the transition in Article 3 of the amending Act's supplementary provisions.

## How do you compare ten years from death with 31 March 2028?

An inheritance normally opens when the decedent dies. For an inheritance that opened before the reform took effect, the general restriction is deferred until the later of the ten-year period from death and the five-year transition from the effective date. That transition runs through 31 March 2028.

| When the inheritance opened | Period to check before the general restriction applies |
| --- | --- |
| Before 1 April 2023, with ten years ending by the end of March 2028 | A transition applies at least through 31 March 2028 |
| Before 1 April 2023, with ten years ending after the end of March 2028 | The ten-year period from the opening of the inheritance is the reference |
| On or after 1 April 2023 | The ten-year period from the opening of the inheritance is the reference |

Gather the date of death, details of undivided assets and any family-court petition. Neither “an old inheritance is already too late” nor “every estate must be divided by March 2028” is a reliable blanket conclusion. Ask a lawyer to check the exact end date and any exception against the records.

## Can adjusted shares still be considered after the period?

The proviso to Civil Code Article 904-3 contains two exceptions.

| Exception | Outline of the statutory conditions |
| --- | --- |
| Item 1 | An heir requested estate division from the family court before the ten-year period elapsed |
| Item 2 | An heir was unable to make that request for an unavoidable reason during the final six months, and requested division before six months elapsed after that reason ceased |

A timely request can bring the later division within an exception. This is not a guarantee that “the clock stops” or that the claimed adjustment will be accepted. The interaction with the transition, whether and how to petition, and what constitutes an unavoidable reason require assessment of the individual case. See the [mediation and adjudication guide](/en/legal/column/souzoku-isanbunkatsu-chotei-shinpan), and consult a lawyer promptly if the relevant date is near.

All heirs may also agree to a division reflecting special benefits and contributions after the period. A unanimous agreement is different from asking a court to accept a claim disputed by another heir.

## FAQ

**Q. Does estate division become impossible after ten years?**
A. No. Division remains possible. The general basis for the shares changes, while exceptions involving a court request and the possibility of unanimous agreement must be considered separately.

**Q. If my parent died before the reform, is March 2028 always the deadline?**
A. No. The transition lasts at least through 31 March 2028. If the ten-year period from death ends later, that later period is the reference. The exact end date must be checked against the records.

**Q. Does a timely court request guarantee recognition of a special benefit or contribution?**
A. No. A timely request relates to an exception under Article 904-3. Whether a special benefit or contribution exists, and its amount, are separate questions. Ask a lawyer about the necessary petition and application of the exception.

**Q. Can an administrative scrivener draft the estate division agreement?**
A. We assist with the agreement where all heirs agree, and with investigating the inheritance relationships. Negotiations and disputes go to a lawyer, registration to a judicial scrivener, and inheritance-tax filing to a tax accountant, under separate contracts.
