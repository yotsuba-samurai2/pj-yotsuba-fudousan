**In short:** to sell LP gas (liquefied petroleum gas) as a business, you need registration of a sales business under the Act on Securing the Safety and Optimizing Transactions of Liquefied Petroleum Gas (the LP Gas Act), Article 3. Where the sales offices are only within one prefecture, the prefectural governor with jurisdiction over their location registers; where they span two or more prefectures, the Minister of Economy, Trade and Industry registers. A registered operator appoints a chief of operations for each sales office (Article 19 of the LP Gas Act), bears the duty to perform safety services (Article 27), and may entrust them to an accredited safety organisation (Articles 28 and 29). When a sales contract is concluded with a general consumer, a document stating the prescribed matters must be delivered without delay (Article 14 of the LP Gas Act, the so-called Article 14 document). This article is general information organising the framework; it does not make specific legal judgements such as whether a particular registration will be granted.

## What is the "registration" needed to start selling LP gas, and how does it differ from a licence?

Anyone who intends to conduct the business of selling LP gas (liquefied petroleum gas) to general consumers must obtain registration of a sales business (Article 3, paragraph 1 of the Act on Securing the Safety and Optimizing Transactions of Liquefied Petroleum Gas = the LP Gas Act). The application states the name and address, the location of the sales offices, and the type and capacity of the storage facilities and supply equipment (paragraph 2), with documents on safety services and the like attached (paragraph 4).

"Registration" differs in structure from a "licence". Registration is granted by the registering administrative agency when the application conforms to the statutory requirements (Article 3-2, "implementation of registration"), and refusal is limited to cases falling under the grounds for refusal in Article 4 (having had a registration revoked without a set period having passed, no prospect of appointing a chief of operations, storage facilities or supply equipment not conforming to the technical standards, and the like). This differs in character from a licence, where the agency judges grant or refusal with broad discretion.

| Item | Organisation in the provisions |
| --- | --- |
| System | Registration of a sales business (Article 3 of the LP Gas Act) |
| Implementation of registration | Registered if the application conforms to the requirements (Article 3-2) |
| Refusal of registration | Limited to cases falling under the grounds for refusal (Article 4) |
| Change / lapse | A change of sales office is notified (Article 8); on certain grounds the registration lapses (Article 24) |

When registered matters change, notification is required (Article 8 of the LP Gas Act), and the registration lapses on certain grounds such as discontinuance of business (Article 24). The final application of which requirement fits is judged by the registering administrative agency after its review.

## Does the registration go to the prefectural governor or the Minister of Economy, Trade and Industry?

The destination of registration is decided by the range in which you place the sales offices. Article 3, paragraph 1 of the LP Gas Act provides that where the sales offices are only within one prefecture, the prefectural governor with jurisdiction over their location registers, and where the sales offices span the areas of two or more prefectures, the Minister of Economy, Trade and Industry registers.

| Range of the sales offices | Registering administrative agency |
| --- | --- |
| Only within one prefecture | The prefectural governor with jurisdiction over the location |
| Spanning two or more prefectures | The Minister of Economy, Trade and Industry |

If you later set up a sales office in another prefecture so that the range changes, the registering administrative agency may change, and the notification and the like in that case are provided in Article 6 of the LP Gas Act. Because which counter applies, and the handling and forms, differ by prefecture and regional bureau, confirm with the prefecture (fire-and-safety division) or the regional Bureau of Economy, Trade and Industry with jurisdiction once the layout of the sales offices is decided (guidance of the High Pressure Gas Safety Institute of Japan and the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry, accessed 2026-10-31).

## How are the appointment of the chief of operations and safety services (entrustment to a safety organisation) organised?

A registered operator must, for each sales office, appoint a chief of operations from among those who hold the prescribed qualification (such as a Class 2 sales chief certificate) and have practical experience in sales (Article 19 of the LP Gas Act). The chief of operations performs duties such as supervising safety regarding the conformity of supply equipment and consumption equipment to the technical standards and the preparation of the Article 14 document (Article 20). On appointment or dismissal, the registering administrative agency is notified without delay.

In addition, a registered operator bears the duty to perform "safety services" such as inspection and investigation at the start of supply, and periodic supply-equipment inspection and periodic consumption-equipment investigation (Article 27 of the LP Gas Act). These safety services may be entrusted to an accredited safety organisation (Article 28), and a person who intends to become a safety organisation obtains accreditation from the Minister of Economy, Trade and Industry or the prefectural governor (Article 29). In practice, the accreditation application for the safety organisation and the arrangement of the entrustment contract are often advanced together with the registration application, and any change of the entrustee must be notified.

| Role | Basis | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Appointment of the chief of operations | Articles 19 and 20 of the LP Gas Act | Appointed for each sales office; supervises safety. Appointment and dismissal are notified |
| Duty to perform safety services | Article 27 of the LP Gas Act | Inspection and investigation at the start of supply, periodic inspection and investigation |
| Entrustment to a safety organisation | Articles 28 and 29 of the LP Gas Act | May be entrusted to an accredited safety organisation; a change of entrustee is notified |

The types of certificate, the years of practical experience, and the standards for each type of safety service are detailed and are amended. Confirm the specific application with the latest guidance of the prefecture and regional bureau with jurisdiction and of the High Pressure Gas Safety Institute of Japan.

## What is required for the document given to consumers (the Article 14 document) and for price transparency?

When a registered operator concludes a contract to sell LP gas with a general consumer, it must deliver without delay a document stating the type of LP gas, the method of delivery, the price, and other prescribed matters (Article 14 of the LP Gas Act, the so-called Article 14 document). With the counterparty's consent, provision by electromagnetic means is also permitted (the same Article).

In recent years, the system has been reviewed from the viewpoint of optimising LP gas transactions and making prices transparent, and the LP Gas Act has been amended by Act No. 67 of 2024 (promulgated 2024-06-26, in force 2025-12-25, accessed 2026-10-31). Confirm the handling relating to clarifying the breakdown of equipment costs and prices with the latest guidance of the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (Agency for Natural Resources and Energy) immediately before you act. Because exactly which entries are required changes with the form of the contract, this article stays with information on the framework and does not judge the propriety of the entries of a specific document.

## FAQ

**Q. Do I need a licence or a registration to start selling LP gas?**
A. To sell LP gas as a business, you need registration of a sales business under Article 3 of the LP Gas Act. Registration is granted when the application conforms to the statutory requirements (Article 3-2), and refusal is limited to cases falling under the grounds for refusal (Article 4). This differs in character from a licence, where the agency judges with broad discretion. Whether a specific registration is granted is judged by the prefecture or regional bureau with jurisdiction after its review.

**Q. Is the counter for registration the prefecture or the national government?**
A. Where the sales offices are only within one prefecture, the prefectural governor with jurisdiction over their location registers; where they span two or more prefectures, the Minister of Economy, Trade and Industry registers (Article 3, paragraph 1 of the LP Gas Act). If the range later changes, the registering administrative agency may change, and the notification and the like in that case are provided in Article 6 of the same Act.

**Q. Must I always appoint a chief of operations?**
A. A registered operator must appoint a chief of operations for each sales office (Article 19 of the LP Gas Act). The chief of operations performs duties such as supervising safety regarding the conformity of supply and consumption equipment and the preparation of the Article 14 document (Article 20). Appointment and dismissal require notification to the registering administrative agency. Confirm the type of certificate and the requirement of practical experience with the latest guidance of the prefecture and regional bureau with jurisdiction and of the High Pressure Gas Safety Institute of Japan.

**Q. Must I perform safety services in-house?**
A. A registered operator bears the duty to perform safety services (Article 27 of the LP Gas Act), but may entrust them to an accredited safety organisation (Articles 28 and 29). A change of entrustee must be notified. Because the standards for each type of safety service and the accreditation requirements may be amended, confirm with the latest guidance of the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry and the prefecture with jurisdiction immediately before you act.
