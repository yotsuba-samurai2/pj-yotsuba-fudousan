**In short:** the start-up visa is a scheme based on METI's "Foreign Entrepreneur Activity Promotion Project," under which a foreign entrepreneur who cannot immediately meet the requirements of the "Business Manager" status of residence can, with the confirmation of a municipality (an implementing body), stay in Japan under the "Designated Activities" status of residence and carry out start-up preparation such as company establishment. The entrepreneur submits a "start-up preparation activity plan" to the municipality, receives confirmation and the issuance of a confirmation certificate, and files it with the regional immigration bureau to obtain the "Designated Activities" status. The period of stay, including renewals, is up to about two years, but the unit of renewal (such as every six months) and the eligible municipalities differ by municipality and plan, and the system is often revised, so be sure to confirm the latest pages of the Immigration Services Agency and METI. To continue the business after the period expires, a change to the "Business Manager" status of residence is needed. The criteria for "Business Manager" were raised by the reform effective 16 October 2025 (Reiwa 7), and the prospective criteria and personal requirements of the start-up visa were also partly revised by the ordinance reform of the same date. The confirmation application for the start-up activity plan and the filing for the status of residence are handled by the administrative scrivener of the Yotsuba group (a filing administrative scrivener, Article 1-3 of the Administrative Scriveners Act) as an independent business; company-establishment registration by a judicial scrivener; social insurance and labor by a labor and social security attorney; tax by a tax accountant; and the premises for the office by real estate, a separate business — you contract with each separately. ## How does the start-up visa differ from the Business Manager visa?

The "Business Manager" status of residence is a full status for engaging in the management of a business in Japan. Its criteria were raised by the reform of the landing-criteria ordinance effective 16 October 2025 (Reiwa 7), and now require capital/total investment of 30 million yen or more and the employment of one or more full-time employees (the full picture of the reform is organized in [the key points of the October 2025 reform of the Business Manager visa](https://luck428.com/legal/column/keiei-kanri-zairyu-2025-kaisei-shihonkin-joukin-shokuin-chuka-taiwan), and the flow of a new establishment in [how a Chinese or Taiwanese entrepreneur obtains the "Business Manager" status of residence](https://luck428.com/legal/column/keieikanri-zairyu-chuka-kigyousha-kaisha-setsuritsu)).

The start-up visa (the Foreign Entrepreneur Activity Promotion Project) is, so to speak, a preparatory-stage scheme for an entrepreneur who cannot yet meet these "Business Manager" requirements at the point of coming to Japan. A municipality (an implementing body) certified by METI confirms the start-up preparation activity plan, and an entrepreneur who has received the confirmation certificate stays in Japan under the "Designated Activities" status while advancing start-up preparation such as company establishment, securing an office, and preparing capital. The greatest difference from the full visa is that one can begin activities without assembling the formal requirements of "Business Manager" from the outset. This article focuses on this preparatory-stage scheme and the transition to the full visa.

| Item | Start-up visa (Designated Activities) | Business Manager visa |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Positioning | Residence at the start-up preparation stage | Full management residence |
| Status of residence | Designated Activities | Business Manager |
| Capital / full-time employees at entry | Need not be met immediately (via plan confirmation) | In principle must meet the criteria |
| Body of review / confirmation | Municipality (implementing body) confirms the plan; immigration grants Designated Activities | Regional immigration bureau |
| Typical period of stay | Up to about two years including renewals | 1, 3, 5 years, etc. |

## In which municipality, and with what kind of start-up activity plan, can you receive confirmation?

The start-up visa can be used in a municipality (an implementing body) certified under METI's Foreign Entrepreneur Activity Promotion Project. Formerly the national strategic special zone scheme and METI's scheme ran side by side, but in January 2025 they were unified into METI's scheme and organized into a nationwide form. There are several eligible municipalities, which increase or decrease as the system is revised. Confirm the municipalities and windows you can use with METI's system page and each municipality's guidance.

To receive confirmation, you submit a "start-up preparation activity plan" to each municipality. The municipality examines the concreteness and feasibility of the business and its contribution to the region, and if it finds it appropriate, issues a confirmation certificate (a start-up preparation activity plan confirmation certificate). Some municipalities hear the opinions of a third party with insight into start-ups and management, such as a small-business consultant or an investor. The content of the plan and how far a prospect of arranging business property during the stay must be shown were partly revised in the ordinance reform effective 16 October 2025 (Reiwa 7) regarding the prospective criteria and personal requirements. Because the concrete criteria are set in each municipality's implementation guideline, confirm the latest guideline before submission.

| Confirmation flow | Content |
| --- | --- |
| ① Submission of the start-up preparation activity plan | Submit the business plan to the certified municipality (implementing body) |
| ② Municipality's examination and confirmation | Examine concreteness, feasibility, etc. (some hear a third party's opinion) |
| ③ Issuance of the confirmation certificate | Issue the start-up preparation activity plan confirmation certificate |
| ④ Application for the status of residence | File for Designated Activities with the regional immigration bureau, attaching the certificate |

## What should you prepare during the stay, and when do you move to the full visa?

Once the "Designated Activities" status of residence is granted, during that period you advance preparation toward a transition to the full "Business Manager" status. Concretely, this is the establishment of the company, securing an independent office, preparing capital/investment, and refining the business plan. The period of stay, including renewals, is said to be up to about two years, but some municipalities require renewal every six months, and the unit and upper limit of renewal differ by municipality and plan.

To continue managing the business in Japan after the period expires, an application is needed to change the status of residence from "Designated Activities" to "Business Manager." At this point of change, it is examined whether you meet the criteria for "Business Manager" (after the 16 October 2025 reform, in addition to the formal requirements of capital/total investment of 30 million yen or more and one or more full-time employees, the qualitative requirements of Japanese ability and expert confirmation of the business plan). The thinking on the continuity and substance of the business asked at the renewal or change stage is treated in detail in [proving the "continuity and substance of the business" at the renewal of a Business Manager visa](https://luck428.com/legal/column/keieikanri-koshin-jigyo-keizokusei-rissho-chuka-taiwan). Because you must return home if the change is not permitted, it is important to allow ample time in the preparation schedule.

## FAQ

**Q. Can I come to Japan and prepare a start-up even without capital under the start-up visa?**
A. The start-up visa (the Foreign Entrepreneur Activity Promotion Project) is a system in which even an entrepreneur who cannot meet the capital/full-time-employee requirements of "Business Manager" at the point of entry can, if they receive a municipality's confirmation of a start-up preparation activity plan, stay in Japan under the "Designated Activities" status and carry out preparation. However, the confirmation examines the concreteness and feasibility of the plan, and prospective criteria are set. The permission itself is by the examination of the regional immigration bureau.

**Q. Can it be used in any municipality?**
A. It can be used only in a municipality (an implementing body) certified under METI's Foreign Entrepreneur Activity Promotion Project. In January 2025 the national strategic special zone scheme and METI's scheme were unified into a nationwide form, but the eligible municipalities increase or decrease as the system is revised. Confirm the municipalities and windows you can use with METI's system page and each municipality's guidance.

**Q. How long is the period of stay, and can it be extended?**
A. The period of the "Designated Activities" status is said to be up to about two years including renewals, but some municipalities require renewal every six months, and the unit and upper limit of renewal differ by municipality and plan. Because the operation of the period may be revised, confirm the latest guidance. To continue the business after the period expires, a change to the "Business Manager" status is needed.

**Q. Can I always change to a Business Manager visa once the period ends?**
A. It does not change automatically. To continue managing the business in Japan after the period expires, you must apply to change from "Designated Activities" to "Business Manager" and undergo examination. After the 16 October 2025 reform, "Business Manager" requires criteria such as capital/total investment of 30 million yen or more and one or more full-time employees. Whether the change is permitted is by the immigration examination that takes into account the fulfillment of the criteria and the substance of the business.
