**In short:** to open an office for independence training (life-skills training / functional training), you must obtain designation as a designated welfare-service provider from the prefectural governor (for designated cities and core cities, the mayor) under the Comprehensive Support for Persons with Disabilities Act Article 36(1). Independence training is a service that provides, as a training-type benefit, the training and other benefits needed so that a person with disabilities can lead an independent daily or social life, improving physical function or living ability (Article 5(12)). To be designated, the applicant must be a juridical person and must meet the personnel, equipment, and operation standards (set by prefectural ordinance; the standards to be followed are the ministerial ordinance on personnel, equipment, and operation of designated welfare services = 2006 MHLW Ordinance No. 171) (Article 36(3), Article 43(1)). For personnel, functional training requires nursing staff, physical or occupational therapists (including speech therapists), and life-support staff at a full-time-equivalent of at least the number of users divided by six, plus a service-management supervisor and a manager (Ordinance Articles 156, 157). Life-skills training requires life-support staff and a service-management supervisor and a manager, adding a community-transition support worker for the residential type (Ordinance Articles 166, 167). This article is general information; whether a particular office is designated rests with the competent local government's review. Preparing the designation application is handled by Yotsuba Administrative Scrivener Office (administrative scrivener); personnel full-time-equivalent calculation, work rules, and social insurance by a social insurance labour consultant; the survey of zoning and fire-service conformity of the premises by a licensed real estate broker; incorporation registration by a judicial scrivener; and tax by a tax accountant, each as an independent business under a separate contract. ## Where do you start with the designation application for independence training?

First, confirm whether you are opening "functional training" or "life-skills training," and which training-type benefit it is. Independence training provides the training needed to improve physical function or living ability and sits within the training-type benefits of Article 5(12). Functional training mainly aims at maintaining and restoring physical function, and life-skills training at maintaining and improving living ability; the personnel standards differ. Which one applies changes the required job types, so this is fixed first.

Next, grasp the overall picture of designation. The designating authority is the prefectural governor (for designated and core cities, the mayor), and without designation you cannot receive the welfare-service benefit (Article 36(1)). The broad conditions are being a juridical person, meeting the personnel, equipment, and operation standards, and not falling under a ground for disqualification (Article 36(3)).

| Order | What to confirm | Basis / referral |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Type | Functional or life-skills (residential or not) | Act Article 5(12) |
| 2 Authority | Prefectural governor / mayor of designated or core city | Act Article 36(1) |
| 3 Juridical person | A juridical person whose articles state the welfare-service purpose | Article 36(3); registration by judicial scrivener |
| 4 Premises | Zoning and fire-service survey | Licensed real estate broker (separate contract) |
| 5 Personnel / documents | FTE, work rules, social insurance | Social insurance labour consultant (separate contract); application by scrivener |

The survey of zoning, the need for a change of use, and fire-service conformity is a licensed real estate broker's domain, also set out in [Designation and premises requirements for daily-life care](https://luck428.com/en/legal/column/seikatsu-kaigo-shitei-bukken-yoken). Each works as an independent business under a separate contract.

## What are the personnel standards for the manager, service-management supervisor, and support staff?

The personnel standards are set by the ministerial ordinance (2006 MHLW Ordinance No. 171), separately for functional and life-skills training. Functional training requires nursing staff, physical or occupational therapists (and speech therapists as needed), and life-support staff at a full-time-equivalent of at least the number of users divided by six; the nursing staff, life-support staff, and service-management supervisor must each include at least one full-time person (Ordinance Article 156). The manager is in principle full-time (Article 157).

Life-skills training requires life-support staff and a service-management supervisor, adding one or more community-transition support workers where residential independence training is provided (Ordinance Article 166); nursing staff are placed where users need health management. The service-management supervisor is one or more where users number 60 or fewer. How full-time-equivalent is counted, the full-time/part-time distinction, and the social-insurance enrolment line are labour design itself, the domain of a social insurance labour consultant.

| Service | Main job types | Guideline | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Functional training | Nursing staff / PT or OT / life-support staff | FTE ≥ users ÷ 6 | Ordinance Art. 156(1)(i) |
| Both | Service-management supervisor | One or more at ≤60 users (≥1 full-time) | Ordinance Arts. 156, 166 |
| Life-skills (residential) | Community-transition support worker | One or more | Ordinance Art. 166(1)(ii) |
| Both | Manager | In principle full-time | Ordinance Arts. 157, 167 |

FTE calculation, work rules, and social-insurance design are handled by a social insurance labour consultant as an independent business under a separate contract. The after-school day-service and child-development support designation is a different type, covered in [The flow of designation for after-school day service and child-development support](https://luck428.com/en/legal/column/houkago-day-jido-hattatsu-shitei-shinsei-nagare).

## How should the juridical personality and the articles' business purpose be arranged?

In principle, only a juridical person can be designated (Article 36(3)). The form—joint-stock company, LLC, general incorporated association, NPO, social welfare corporation—does not matter, but the articles of incorporation must state that the entity carries on independence training (a welfare-service business) under the Act. Applying without this statement will prompt a demand for a purpose-change registration and delay the application.

So, if you are forming a new juridical person, put the welfare-service wording into the founding articles. If you start with an existing juridical person, check the current articles and registered particulars, and if the purpose is missing, first complete the purpose-change procedure (a resolution of the general meeting and the change registration). Supporting the resolution is an administrative scrivener's work; the change registration itself is a judicial scrivener's.

| Item | Content | Referral |
|---|---|---|
| Juridical personality | In principle a juridical person | Act Article 36(3) |
| Business purpose | Statement of independence training (welfare-service business) | Drafting by scrivener |
| Purpose change | Resolution and change registration | Resolution support by scrivener; registration by judicial scrivener |
| Officer disqualification | No ground for disqualification | Article 36(3) items |

Incorporation and purpose-change registration are handled by a judicial scrivener, each as an independent business under a separate contract. Our office receives no referral fee.

## In what order do the equipment and operation standards and the local pre-consultation proceed?

The equipment standards are set, for functional training, by Ordinance Article 158 (applying Article 81), and for life-skills training by Ordinance Article 168; both require a training/work room, a consultation room, a washroom, a toilet, and a multipurpose room. Residential independence training adds living rooms and a bath (Ordinance Article 168(3)). Their dimensions and fire-equipment requirements are confirmed against the Building Standards Act, the Fire Service Act, and the local government's guidance. The survey of zoning, the need for a change of use, and fire-service conformity of the premises is a licensed real estate broker's domain, to be settled before the lease.

Many local governments require a "pre-consultation" before the application. You consult the window with drawings and a personnel plan, confirm the prospect of conformity, then proceed to the main application. The order is: fix the type and premises, pre-consult, secure personnel and prepare documents, apply, and be designated. Skipping the pre-consultation risks being told of shortfalls at the main application and missing the designation date.

| Step | Content | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Equipment check | Training/work room, consultation room, washroom, toilet, multipurpose room | Ordinance Arts. 158, 168 |
| Fire / use survey | Zoning, change of use, fire conformity | Licensed real estate broker (separate contract) |
| Pre-consultation | Confirm conformity via drawings and personnel plan | Local-government window |
| Main application | Prepare and submit the application | Application by scrivener |

The operation standards (individual support plans, management of support by the service-management supervisor, keeping of records) must be maintained during operation too. ## How do you build the schedule working back from the standard processing period and the designation date?

The designation date is set, in many local governments, as the first of each month. So decide when you want to open (which designation date), and work back to arrange the application deadline, the pre-consultation, securing personnel, and the lease. The application deadline is set by many local governments somewhere from the middle to the end of the month before the designation date, and the processing period from deadline to designation is also set by each local government. Because the specific deadline and standard processing period differ by local government, always confirm them in the competent local government's guidance (this article does not assert a specific number of days).

As a guideline for working back, entering the pre-consultation two to three months before the desired designation date, with the premises, personnel, and juridical personality in prospect, leaves no strain. Because personnel involve employment contracts and social-insurance procedures including the service-management supervisor, you must create a state of being enrolled on the designation date. This employment, social-insurance, and work-rules arrangement is a social insurance labour consultant's domain, proceeding in parallel with the document preparation (administrative scrivener).

| Point in the countdown | What to do | Who |
|---|---|---|
| ~3 months before | Fix type and premises, confirm the juridical person's purpose | Scrivener / judicial scrivener / broker (separate contracts) |
| ~2 months before | Pre-consultation, fix the personnel plan | Local government / labour consultant |
| By the previous month's deadline | Prepare and submit the application | Scrivener |
| Designation date | Staff enrolled, social insurance completed | Labour consultant |

Schedule management from the designation date decides the success of opening. ## FAQ

**Q. Can an individual, not a juridical person, be designated for independence training?**
A. In principle you must be a juridical person (Act Article 36(3)). The form is not restricted, but the articles' business purpose must state that the entity carries on independence training (a welfare-service business). A sole proprietorship cannot be designated, so first form a juridical person or arrange an existing one's purpose. Incorporation and purpose-change registration are handled by a judicial scrivener as an independent business under a separate contract.

**Q. Can the service-management supervisor also serve as a life-support worker?**
A. The service-management supervisor manages support and is in principle expected to be a person separate from the life-support staff who provide direct support (Ordinance Articles 156, 166). The supervisor must have certain practical experience and have completed the service-management supervisor training (basic and practical). The fine design of FTE and concurrent duties is a social insurance labour consultant's domain.

**Q. How do the personnel standards differ between functional and life-skills training?**
A. Functional training places nursing staff, PT or OT (and speech therapists as needed), and life-support staff at FTE ≥ users ÷ 6 (Ordinance Article 156). Life-skills training centres on life-support staff and a service-management supervisor, adding a community-transition support worker for the residential type (Ordinance Article 166). Because the required job types differ, fixing the type before applying is important.

**Q. When is the designation date, and when should I start preparing?**
A. The designation date is the first of each month in many local governments. The application deadline is set in the month before, and the standard processing period from deadline to designation is set by each local government. Because the specific days differ, confirm them in the competent local government's guidance. Entering the pre-consultation two to three months before the desired designation date, with premises, personnel, and juridical personality in prospect, leaves no strain.
