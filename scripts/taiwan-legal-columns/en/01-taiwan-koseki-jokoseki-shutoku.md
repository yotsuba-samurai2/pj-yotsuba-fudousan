Taiwan’s household registration offices issue household registration transcripts, including the **Household Registration Cancellation Transcript (除戶戶籍謄本)**. This is a record of a former household registration. It may contain a death entry, but it is not a death certificate. The standard electronic transcript and the online inheritance application reviewed by a household registration office are different services. Before applying, ask the Japanese institution which entries and document format it needs.

## Why Taiwan household registration documents are necessary

In Japanese real estate inheritance procedures, Japanese household registration alone is sometimes insufficient. This occurs when either the decedent or the heir holds Taiwan nationality.

Under Article 36 of Japan’s Act on General Rules for Application of Laws (法の適用に関する通則法), succession is generally governed by the decedent’s national law. The heir’s nationality alone is not decisive. The table below describes the general rules when Taiwan’s Civil Code applies. Ask a qualified professional to confirm the governing law and the heirs in your case.

Taiwan household registration, like Japan's system, has a structure that traces family relationships through connecting lines. This structure makes it possible to use these documents to identify heirs.

### Succession order under Taiwan Civil Law (Articles 1138 and 1144)

| Rank | Heirs | Spouse's Succession Share When Present (Article 1144) |
|---|---|---|
| First | Direct lineal descendants (children, grandchildren) | Equal share with other heirs |
| Second | Parents | One-half of the estate |
| Third | Siblings | One-half of the estate |
| Fourth | Grandparents | Two-thirds of the estate |
| No heirs in ranks 1-4 | — | Entire estate |

The spouse always qualifies as an heir regardless of rank. The key difference from Japanese law is that at the first rank, **spouses and children share equally**, unlike Japanese law where the spouse receives one-half.

## Where and how to obtain Taiwanese household registration transcripts, including cancellation transcripts

| Acquisition Method | Who Takes Action | Required Items | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard current-household electronic transcript | Applicant | Taiwan’s natural person certificate (自然人憑證) | Generally limited to the applicant’s current household or members of the same household. Not a way to obtain a deceased person’s transcript from a different household |
| Online inheritance application (subject to review) | Eligible heir (applying on their own behalf) | Taiwan’s natural person certificate and supporting documents as applicable | The household registration office reviews who and what may be covered. Confirm separately whether Japan’s receiving institution will accept it |
| Request Taiwan representative | Taiwan-based family or acquaintance | Power of attorney authenticated at the Taipei Economic and Cultural Representative Office | Someone in Taiwan can help |
| Request professional intermediary | Administrative scrivener, judicial scrivener, etc. | Power of attorney and other documents as specified by intermediary | Numerous old handwritten cancellation transcripts / unable to visit representative office on weekdays |
| Direct application in person | Applicant travels to Taiwan | Identity document | Planned trip to Taiwan |

**After approval and payment, the receiving institution can use a case number and authorization code to access the record in Taiwan’s system.** We have not confirmed that a Japanese Legal Affairs Bureau or bank can access the system or accept a printout. If you need a deceased person’s cancellation transcript, first ask the Japanese institution what entries, paper originals and Japanese translations it requires. If necessary, apply at a Taiwan household registration office. Applications through a representative require a power of attorney and may require authentication of documents prepared outside Taiwan.

**Only Taiwan household registration offices can issue these documents**. The Taipei Economic and Cultural Representative Office handles **authentication** of documents like powers of attorney, not the issuance of household registration documents themselves. Many people mistakenly visit the representative office for this purpose.

**Older cancellation transcripts may include handwritten entries.** Translation and verification take considerable time, so calculate your timeline backward from your deadline.

## How much translation and authentication is necessary?

When using Taiwan household registration documents in Japan, it is standard to provide a Japanese translation. However, translation requirements and authentication necessity **vary depending on the receiving institution.**

| Receiving Institution | Documents to Provide | What to Confirm Beforehand |
|---|---|---|
| Legal Affairs Bureau (inheritance registration) | Taiwan household registration documents and Japanese translation | Translation requirements, whether original documents will be returned |
| Financial institutions | Taiwan household registration documents and Japanese translation | Authentication requirements vary by institution |
| Tax office (inheritance tax) | As above | Confirm with tax accountant |

**Confirming requirements with the receiving institution before collecting documents** is the shortest path to avoiding rework. If told after collection that "this format cannot be accepted," you will need to start the collection process over.

Note: When submitting Japanese public documents to Taiwan, the procedure is reversed. Because there is no formal diplomatic relationship between Japan and Taiwan, authentication is obtained from the **Taipei Economic and Cultural Representative Office** rather than through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs' apostille or official seal verification. Do not reverse the direction.

## When is the deadline for inheritance registration?

Even if Taiwan document collection takes time, the Japanese deadline will not wait.

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Legal basis | Real Property Registration Act Article 76-2, Paragraph 1 |
| Effective date | April 1, 2024 |
| Deadline | Within 3 years from the date the heir knows both that the succession has opened in their favor and that they have acquired ownership of the specific property by inheritance |
| Successions that opened before April 1, 2024 | If the heir knew both facts before April 1, 2024, apply by March 31, 2027. If the heir first meets both knowledge requirements on or after April 1, 2024, apply within 3 years from that date |
| Non-criminal monetary penalty | Up to ¥100,000 if neglected without just cause (Real Property Registration Act Article 164, Paragraph 1) |

It is not uncommon for Taiwan documents to take several months to obtain. Do not leave matters pending with "documents are not yet complete"—**create a timeline working backward from the deadline.**
