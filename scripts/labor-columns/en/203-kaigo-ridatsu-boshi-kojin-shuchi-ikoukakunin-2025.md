**Bottom line first**: From 1 April 2025, when an employee gives notice that they face caring for a family member, the company must individually inform that employee of care leave and work-care balance support measures and confirm their intent to use them (Child Care and Family Care Leave Act, Article 21, paragraph 4). It must also provide information at an early stage before care begins, such as at age 40 (same article, paragraph 5), and set up the employment environment with training and a consultation desk (Article 22, paragraphs 2 and 4). Building the measures into work rules and daily operation is the starting point for preventing care-driven resignations.

## What is the "individual notice and intent confirmation for care" that became mandatory from April 2025?

An employee faces caring for a parent and quits because "I did not know what support was available" — the duty of individual notice and intent confirmation was created to prevent this. Article 21, paragraph 4 of the Child Care and Family Care Leave Act provides that when an employee gives notice that "a family member subject to care has reached a state requiring that employee's care," the employer must inform the employee of the care leave system, the work-care balance support measures and other matters set by ordinance, and take measures such as an interview to confirm the employee's intent regarding an application for care leave or for the work-care balance support measures.

| Item | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| When | When the employee gives notice that a family member now needs care | Article 21, paragraph 4 |
| To whom | The employee who gave notice | Same paragraph |
| What to inform | The care leave system, work-care balance support measures (care leave days, limits on work beyond scheduled hours / overtime / late-night work, shortened hours measures), where to apply, and the care leave benefit | Same paragraph and the Act's enforcement ordinance |
| What to confirm | Intent to use care leave or the work-care balance support measures | Same paragraph |
| Adverse treatment | Dismissal or other adverse treatment on the ground of the notice or intent is prohibited | Same article, paragraph 6 |

A "family member subject to care" here includes a spouse (including a de facto marriage), parents, children and the spouse's parents. The method of informing and confirming intent is set by ordinance: an interview (online interviews are allowed), delivery of a document, or, where the employee wishes, electronic mail and the like. Note that the notice and intent confirmation must not be done in a way that discourages taking or using the measures; the aim is to convey that the system exists and to respect the intent to use it. For the overall map of the 2025 amendment, see [the 2025 amendment to the Child Care and Family Care Leave Act](/en/labor/column/ikuji-kaigo-kyugyo-ho-2025-kaisei).

## When, to whom and what information must be provided?

Beyond waiting for a notice, information must also be provided at an early stage before care begins. Article 21, paragraph 5 provides that when an employee reaches the start of a period set by ordinance — such as the year in which they turn 40, being "appropriate and effective for deepening workers' understanding and interest" in care leave and the support measures — the employer must, within that period, inform the employee of the care leave system, the support measures and other matters set by ordinance.

| Type | Timing | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Individual notice and intent confirmation | When notice is given that care is now faced (Article 21, paragraph 4) | The employee who gave notice |
| Early-stage information provision | A period set by ordinance such as turning 40 (Article 21, paragraph 5); in practice, the fiscal year in which the employee turns 40, or one year from the day after they turn 40 | Employees around age 40 |

The timing of information provision is either from the first to the last day of the fiscal year in which the employee turns 40, or one year from the employee's 40th birthday (confirmed in Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare materials on 12 November 2026). It may be done for eligible employees together at the start of the fiscal year. Care often begins suddenly, so telling employees around age 40 that "the company has these systems" lays the ground for them to consult rather than resign when the time comes.

## How do you set up care leave, care leave days, limits on work beyond scheduled hours and other support measures?

Before notice and information provision, the measures themselves must be set out in the work rules. The main measures the Act provides as work-care balance support are as follows.

| Measure | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Care leave | Up to a total of 93 days per family member, split into up to 3 times | Article 11 etc. |
| Care leave days | Days off for care of a family member; taken by the day or by the hour | Articles 16-5 and 16-6 |
| Limit on work beyond scheduled hours | Limits work beyond scheduled hours (overtime) for care | Article 16-9 |
| Limit on overtime | Limits overtime beyond 24 hours a month / 150 hours a year | Article 18 |
| Limit on late-night work | Limits work between 10 p.m. and 5 a.m. | Article 20 |
| Shortened scheduled hours measures | Short-time work and other measures over a period of at least three consecutive years | Article 23, paragraph 3 |

From April 2025, for care leave days, the requirement of "continuously employed for less than six months" was removed from the workers who may be excluded by a labor-management agreement, leaving only "scheduled to work no more than two days a week" (confirmed in Ministry materials on 12 November 2026). Because employees with less than six months of service can now take care leave days, the work rules and the child-care/family-care leave provisions must be reviewed. In addition, allowing teleworking during a care period was positioned as a duty of effort. On benefits, a care leave benefit may be paid from employment insurance (Employment Insurance Act, Article 61-4). It covers care leave of up to a total of 93 days over up to 3 times per family member; payment requires, among other things, at least 12 months in aggregate of deemed insured periods in the two years before the leave, and Hello Work (the Public Employment Security Office) examines eligibility and the rate.

## How far must you go with training and a consultation desk to have met the duty?

Setting up the employment environment to make the measures "usable" is also a duty. Article 22, paragraph 2 covers measures to let care leave applications proceed smoothly, and paragraph 4 covers measures for work-care balance support applications; for each, the employer must take any one of the following.

| Measure | Content |
|---|---|
| Hold training | Training on care leave and the work-care balance support measures |
| Set up a consultation system | Such as establishing a consultation desk |
| Other measures set by ordinance | Making known the policy on the measures and their use, collecting and providing examples of use, etc. |

Because the article says "any one of the measures," the duty is met in law by taking at least one of the four. However, training does not count merely by posting or distributing materials — the attendance must be managed — and a consultation desk needs an actual capacity to respond, not just a form (confirmed in Ministry materials on 12 November 2026). Because care situations differ greatly by person, combining several measures raises effectiveness. If you set up a consultation desk, writing the person in charge and contact in the work rules helps it reach employees. For how to prepare the work rules themselves, see [the duty to prepare work rules and what they contain](/en/labor/column/shugyokisoku-10nin-gimu-nani-ga-hitsuyo).

## How do you concretely build work rules and in-house notice to prevent care-driven resignations?

Beyond meeting the duty, to actually prevent resignations it helps to make the measures, notice and operation into one flow.

| What to prepare | Point |
|---|---|
| Child-care/family-care leave provisions | Set out care leave, care leave days, each limit and short-time work; reflect the removal of the six-month exclusion for care leave days |
| Notice and intent-confirmation forms | Prepare a document to hand over when notice is given (list of measures, where to apply, benefit guidance) and an intent-confirmation sheet |
| Information provision at age 40 | A mechanism to extract eligible employees and give a fiscal-year-based batch notice |
| Consultation desk | Make the person in charge and contact clear, and operate while keeping consultations confidential |
| Training for managers | Share the role of receiving a notice and linking it to the measures |

Work rules and forms are not "done" once made — what matters is that they run smoothly when a notice comes in. When an employee faces care, they can first consult a supervisor or desk, the measures are explained and intent confirmed, and if needed they are linked to a public-service consultation point — deciding this flow in-house helps prevent resignation. Preparing these provisions and forms and designing the notice is the labor attorney's field. Fees are in the [fee schedule](/en/labor/ryokin), and frequently asked questions in the [FAQ](/en/labor/faq).

## Who should employees consult about using public care services or applying for benefits?

What the company can do goes as far as notice, intent confirmation and designing work that balances with care. The employee's own care, and the procedures for benefits and public services, are a separate field.

| What you want to do | Who to consult | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Using long-term care insurance services / a care plan | Community General Support Center, care manager | Coordinating care-insurance services is the field of the municipality and the support center |
| Applying for and examining the care leave benefit | Hello Work (Public Employment Security Office) | Examining employment-insurance benefits is Hello Work's role |
| Tax treatment related to care | Tax accountant | Tax is the tax accountant's field |
| An employee's individual legal trouble over care | Lawyer | Individual legal disputes |

How to use long-term care insurance services and how to build a care plan is for the Community General Support Center and care managers. Hello Work examines the application for and grant of the care leave benefit. These are all independent bodies separate from the labor attorney's office, engaged separately. The company's role is to add information so the employee can reach these consultation points. There is no referral fee.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q. If an employee gives notice of care, must we always hold an interview?**
A. An interview is one method, not mandatory. The individual notice and intent confirmation in Article 21, paragraph 4 is done by methods set by ordinance: besides an interview (including online), delivery of a document, or, where the employee wishes, electronic mail and the like. What matters is to reliably convey the care leave system, the work-care balance support measures, where to apply and the care leave benefit, confirm the intent to use them, and keep a record. Notice done in a way that discourages taking or using the measures is not allowed.

**Q. Must we do anything for staff around age 40 who do not yet face care?**
A. Yes. Under Article 21, paragraph 5, there is a duty to provide information on the care leave system and the support measures during a period set by ordinance, such as the year the employee turns 40. In practice it is done either in the fiscal year the employee turns 40, or within one year from the day after they turn 40, and eligible employees may be informed together at the start of the fiscal year. Because care often begins suddenly, telling them early helps prevent resignation.

**Q. We have no room to set up a consultation desk. Can training alone meet the duty?**
A. In law, the employment-environment measures in Article 22, paragraphs 2 and 4 are met by taking "any one" of the listed measures, so holding training alone can satisfy the duty. But training is not enough by merely handing out materials — the attendance must be managed. To raise effectiveness, it is desirable to combine several measures, such as also making the consultation point clear. Choosing a method that fits your size, together with a labor attorney, is a good approach.

**Q. Must the company explain the amount and procedure of the care leave benefit?**
A. Making known that the system exists is the company's duty, but Hello Work (the Public Employment Security Office) examines eligibility, amount and procedure. The care leave benefit, under Employment Insurance Act Article 61-4, is paid for care leave of up to a total of 93 days over up to 3 times per family member, subject to requirements such as at least 12 months in aggregate of deemed insured periods in the two years before the leave. The specific amount and individual eligibility are matters for Hello Work's examination; the company handles guidance on the system and bridging to the consultation point.
