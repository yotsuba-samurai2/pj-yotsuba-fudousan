**In short:** Under the 2025 pension reform law (the Act to Partially Amend the National Pension Act and others for strengthening the pension system in light of socio-economic change; enacted 13 June 2025, promulgated 20 June 2025), the cap on the employees' pension standard monthly remuneration is scheduled to rise in three stages from the current grade 32 of 650,000 yen to 680,000 yen in September 2027, 710,000 yen in September 2028 and 750,000 yen in September 2029. New grades 33 (680,000), 34 (710,000) and 35 (750,000) are expected to be added, and the impact is limited to high earners with monthly remuneration of roughly 665,000 yen or more; others' premiums do not change. In payroll practice, this requires swapping the premium table at each effective date and identifying affected people and changing system settings. The figures and effective dates are organised on the premise of being fixed in the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare's official materials and enforcement orders. The outlook for an individual's pension is for the pension office; tax such as reflecting the premium in profit and loss is for a tax accountant.

"We pay high executive remuneration — how much will the company and the person pay once the cap rises?" and "When should we swap the grade table in the payroll software?" — these are questions from payroll and labour staff at companies with high earners that will grow from here. This page organises the content of the standard monthly remuneration cap rise under the 2025 pension reform and the payroll preparation. Confirm the final figures, effective dates and provisions in the Ministry's official materials and the official gazette and orders.

## When and to how much does the standard monthly remuneration cap rise?

The employees' pension standard monthly remuneration is currently divided into 32 grades, from grade 1 (88,000 yen) to grade 32 (650,000 yen) (Employees' Pension Insurance Act, Article 20). The 2025 pension reform law is scheduled to raise this cap (grade 32, 650,000) in three stages and add three grades on top.

| Timing | New cap | New grade (expected) |
|---|---|---|
| Current | 650,000 yen | Grade 32 (650,000) is the top |
| September 2027 | 680,000 yen | New grade 33 (680,000) |
| September 2028 | 710,000 yen | New grade 34 (710,000) |
| September 2029 | 750,000 yen | New grade 35 (750,000) |

The health insurance standard monthly remuneration cap (grade 50, 1,390,000 yen) is not subject to this reform; **what rises is the employees' pension cap.** The reform law is also expected to introduce a rule allowing the cap to be revised according to the proportion of insured persons in the top grade, so the cap may move further by this mechanism in future. Confirm the grade table and effective dates in the premium table and enforcement orders the Ministry presents (as of November 2026, promulgated and with staged enforcement planned).

## When a new grade is added, whose premiums rise?

Premiums rise only for **people with high remuneration who are pinned at the current cap (grade 32, 650,000).** When the cap rises, the standard monthly remuneration of those previously capped at 650,000 rises to 680,000, 710,000 and 750,000 according to their actual remuneration, and their premiums (shared half-and-half by employer and employee) rise by that much.

| Category | Impact |
|---|---|
| Monthly remuneration of roughly 665,000 yen or more | Moves to a new grade; standard monthly remuneration rises. Premiums (both employee and company) rise |
| Monthly remuneration below the 650,000 band | Stays in the current grade. Premiums do not change |
| Company's burden | As employees' pension premiums are shared half-and-half, the company's burden also rises by the same amount for affected people |

According to the Ministry's explanation, for a person whose standard monthly remuneration becomes 750,000 at the final stage (from September 2029), the employee's burden rises by roughly 9,000 yen per month (confirm the exact figure in the Ministry's materials, as it varies with rate and stage). **The impact is limited to some high earners,** so it does not affect most employees, but companies with high executive remuneration need to anticipate the rise in the company's burden. How the standard monthly remuneration itself is decided is in [how do the regular determination and the occasional revision notices differ](/en/labor/column/santei-kiso-getsu-gaku-henko-chigai).

## How does the cap rise affect future pension amounts?

Both the employees' pension premium and the future pension amount (the earnings-related portion) are calculated on the basis of the standard monthly remuneration. So **a person whose premiums rise because the cap rises also has their future earnings-related pension amount rise by that much** — it is not only a rise in burden; it is reflected in benefits too.

- **Premium:** the premium paid rises by the amount the standard monthly remuneration rises
- **Pension amount (earnings-related portion):** the period in which the standard monthly remuneration is higher is reflected in the calculation of the future earnings-related portion, working to raise the pension amount
- **The company's view:** the company's premium burden rises, while this is the flip side of the employee's or executive's future benefit

However, **how much each person ultimately receives** is decided by many factors — insured period, remuneration trajectory, reforms, etc. Estimating the outlook and awarding the pension are the domain of the pension office (Japan Pension Service); this article stays with explaining the mechanism. We do not calculate individual pension amounts.

## By when should you prepare the payroll system change?

The standard monthly remuneration cap rise requires changing payroll settings at each effective date (September 2027, September 2028, September 2029). The practical steps are as follows.

| Timing | What to do |
|---|---|
| Before enforcement | Identify affected people (high earners pinned at the cap). Estimate the rise in the company's burden and build it into the budget |
| Enforcement month (September) | Swap in the new grade/premium table. Update the grade master in the payroll software |
| After enforcement | Verify that affected people's deductions have changed correctly. Confirm the impact on executive remuneration design |

Note that **the usual revision timing of the standard monthly remuneration (the regular determination each September) overlaps with the enforcement timing of this cap rise (September).** If you use payroll software, the vendor's grade-table update data is usually distributed at each effective date, so apply the update and be sure to verify affected people's deductions. If you manage by hand or a self-made ledger, missing a new grade causes under-deduction, so swap the grade table before the enforcement month's processing. Building the payroll system and the market rate are summarised in [what is the market rate for entrusting payroll to a Shakai Hoken Roumushi](/en/labor/column/kyuyo-keisan-soba-sharoushi).

## Where is the difference from the standard bonus cap?

Social insurance premiums apply to both the monthly salary (standard monthly remuneration) and the bonus (standard bonus amount). What rises this time is **the cap on the standard monthly remuneration that applies to the monthly salary,** which is a separate mechanism from the bonus cap. As they are easily confused, here is a comparison.

| Item | Standard monthly remuneration (subject to this reform) | Standard bonus amount (not subject to this reform) |
|---|---|---|
| What it applies to | Monthly salary | Bonus (bonuses paid 3 or fewer times a year) |
| Employees' pension cap | Current 650,000 → staged rise to 680,000/710,000/750,000 | 1,500,000 yen per month (per single payment; aggregated if multiple in the same month) |
| Health insurance cap | 1,390,000 (not subject this time) | Fiscal-year (April–March) cumulative 5,730,000 |

**The employees' pension standard bonus cap (1,500,000 yen per month) does not change in this reform.** Companies paying high bonuses should estimate premiums on the premise that separate caps apply to the monthly and the bonus. The standard bonus cap and the payment-report practice are organised in [how do you handle bonus social insurance premiums and the standard bonus cap](/en/labor/column/shoyo-shakaihokenryo-hyojun-shoyogaku-jogen-shiharaitodoke). For how to include company housing and in-kind payment in the standard remuneration, see [how do you include in-kind wages in the standard remuneration](/en/labor/column/shataku-genbutsu-kyuyo-hyojun-hoshu).

## FAQ

**Q. When does the standard monthly remuneration cap become 750,000 yen?**
A. Under the 2025 pension reform law, it is scheduled to rise in three stages from the current 650,000 to 680,000 in September 2027, 710,000 in September 2028 and 750,000 in September 2029. New grades 33, 34 and 35 are expected to be added respectively. Confirm the fixed figures and effective dates in the Ministry's revised premium table and enforcement orders.

**Q. When the cap rises, do everyone's premiums rise?**
A. No. Only some high earners whose remuneration is high and pinned at the current cap (grade 32, 650,000) see a rise — as a guide, those with monthly remuneration of roughly 665,000 yen or more; others' premiums do not change. The company's burden also rises, half-and-half, only for affected people.

**Q. If premiums rise, does the future pension also rise?**
A. Both the employees' pension premium and the earnings-related pension amount are calculated on the basis of the standard monthly remuneration. The period in which the standard monthly remuneration is higher because the cap rose is reflected in the future earnings-related calculation, working to raise the pension amount. However, the final pension amount depends on the insured period and more, and estimating the outlook is the pension office's domain.

**Q. Does the cap on social insurance premiums for bonuses also rise?**
A. No. What rises this time is the cap on the standard monthly remuneration that applies to the monthly salary; the cap on the standard bonus amount that applies to bonuses (employees' pension 1,500,000 yen per month; health insurance fiscal-year cumulative 5,730,000) is not subject to this reform. Estimate premiums on the premise that separate caps apply to the monthly and the bonus.
