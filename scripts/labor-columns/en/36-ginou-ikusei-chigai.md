**In short:** the biggest change for labour administration is that **transfers at the worker's own initiative are recognised**. The system becomes one people can leave, so the axis shifts from "bringing people in" to **"keeping them"**. Because Japanese-language ability and skill stages become elements of the plan (there is a declaration form on target Japanese ability), **wage and development design** itself becomes labour administration's work.

This page is an **overview** for owners and back-office staff who have hosted technical intern trainees or are weighing the new system. System explainers abound, and sector-specific guidelines keep updating; this article stays on **what labour administration changes**, and hands the specifics to four companion articles.

## What changes most?

**People become able to move.** The certification application set includes **seven forms on changes of implementer (transfers)** (reference forms 1-16 to 1-22; OTIT, checked 14 August 2026). As the count shows, transfer is not an exception path but the centre of the design.

Labour arrangements built on the premise that "once received, people stay to the end" — dormitory charges, lent property, recouping training costs, the wage curve — get rebuilt on the premise that **people may move mid-way**. The reason treatment must be documented so many times ([what goes into the treatment sections](/en/labor/column/ikusei-shuro-keikaku-taigu-kakikata)) is that this is a system where the conditions shown at the entrance are later checked against reality.

## What does labour administration do to keep people?

Not slogans — the structure of the forms tells you.

| Measure | The corresponding element in the system |
|---|---|
| **Treatment transparency** — contract, statements and pay slips kept identical | The statement of important matters (1-13) and the remuneration/accommodation/collections statements (1-15, 1-39) |
| **Housing-charge design** — collections and [in-kind compensation](/en/labor/column/shataku-genbutsu-kyuyo-hyojun-hoshu) in order | Accommodation and collections are documented items |
| **Being someone people can consult** — a life advisor who is more than a name | Acceptance letters for the supervisor, instructor and **life advisor** (1-11) |
| **A Japanese and skills development plan** — hours and cost burden decided | The declaration on target Japanese-language ability (1-35) |
| **A wage curve** — showing how pay rises as stages rise | The stage structure of skills and language built into the plan itself |

Each is the work of showing, in documents and in fact, **a reason to stay**. A treatment gap that once ended in grumbling now ends in a transfer. Put the other way: transparent treatment and a real development plan are themselves the retention design.

## How does the transition from technical intern training work?

The calendar, with primary sources, is in [What to do now](/en/labor/column/ikusei-shuro-ima-yatte-oku-koto). In outline: licence pre-applications are **being accepted**, plan-certification pre-applications open **September 1, 2026**, and on the technical intern training side new supervising-organisation licences run to **September 30, 2026** and No. 1 plan applications to **February 2027** (OTIT, checked 14 August 2026).

For those currently in technical intern training, the form set includes **a certification application form for former technical intern trainees (annexed form 3(2))** — the transition path exists at form level. **Whether and how an individual case can transition is the territory of ministerial orders, the operating guidelines and the immigration authorities.** This office does not judge it; the application side belongs to 四葉行政書士事務所 (a separate business).

## Where do you start?

As a single sequence:

1. **Draw the calendar** — put [the deadlines](/en/labor/column/ikusei-shuro-ima-yatte-oku-koto) into your own schedule
2. **Design treatment and housing charges** — [the treatment sections](/en/labor/column/ikusei-shuro-keikaku-taigu-kakikata)
3. **Fix the rules** — [the work rules](/en/labor/column/ikusei-shuro-shugyokisoku-minaoshi)
4. **Prepare the transfer procedures** — [insurance and notifications on transfer](/en/labor/column/ikusei-shuro-tenseki-shakaihoken-koyohoken)

Step 1 has deadlines; steps 2–4 are substance. Do 1 first, and build 2–4 toward enforcement.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q. Are technical intern training and the new system different laws?**
A. No. The new system is created by amending the Act on Proper Technical Intern Training and Protection of Technical Intern Trainees (Act No. 89 of 2016) through Act No. 60 of 2024 (the title also changes, but the current text on e-Gov still carries the old title — confirmed 14 August 2026).

**Q. If people can transfer away, how do we recoup training investment?**
A. That worry is exactly why wage and development design becomes labour administration's work. Set the cost rules in the entrance documents (form 1-39) and show a wage curve that gives stages a payoff. Note that casually drafted repayment-on-departure clauses can collide with Article 16 of the Labor Standards Act — see [the work-rules article](/en/labor/column/ikusei-shuro-shugyokisoku-minaoshi).

**Q. Do we have to raise wages?**
A. This article gives no blanket answer. Remuneration levels touch plan certification, and sector-specific provisions exist. What is certain on the labour side: keep the remuneration and collections statements identical to actual payment and deductions, and show the path by which pay rises with stages.

**Q. Can we consult before anything is decided?**
A. Yes — consultation is free. The least wasteful entry is the calendar check plus a stocktake of your current state: contract templates, rules, housing charges, insurance procedures.
