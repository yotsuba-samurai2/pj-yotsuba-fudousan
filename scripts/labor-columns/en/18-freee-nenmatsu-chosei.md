**In short:** freee HR (freee人事労務) has a year-end adjustment feature, and **no licence is needed to run it for your own company**. But the year-end adjustment (nenmatsu chōsei) is a **tax procedure** that settles the over- and under-withholding of income tax, and doing it for another party for a fee is work for **a tax accountant**. Even if a shakai hoken roumushi runs your payroll, **the year-end adjustment is not included**.

"freee has a year-end adjustment button, but our retained shakai hoken roumushi says they can't take it on" — every year as December approaches, companies run into this apparent contradiction. This page is for owners and back-office staff planning to run the year-end adjustment in freee HR, and explains why something "in the software" cannot be handed to the labour consultant. Yotsuba does not handle it either; we point clients to a tax accountant.

## Is the year-end adjustment part of payroll?

No — and this is where the confusion starts.

In monthly payroll, income tax is **withheld according to the withholding tax tables**. That is part of payroll, and within what you can ask a shakai hoken roumushi to do. The year-end adjustment, by contrast, **settles the difference between the total withheld during the year and the tax actually due for that year** (Income Tax Act, Article 190). The monthly withholding is a "provisional collection"; the year-end adjustment is the "once-a-year settlement". Same tax, different character of procedure.

And the settlement side is **a tax procedure**. That is why a payroll retainer does not automatically include the year-end adjustment.

## The feature is in the software — why can't we hand it over?

Article 2, paragraph 1 of the Certified Public Tax Accountant Act defines **tax agency, preparation of tax documents, and tax consultation** as the work of a tax accountant. Calculating the year-end adjustment, and the documents it produces — the withholding certificates, the statutory report summary, the salary payment reports — belong to this territory. Doing it **for another party, for a fee**, is the tax accountant's province.

If the structure looks familiar, you are right. As with the Certified Social Insurance and Labor Consultant Act discussed in [We've brought in freee HR. So what does a retained shakai hoken roumushi actually do?](/en/labor/column/freee-jinji-roumu-sharoushi-doko-made), the definition of "tax documents" in item 2 of the Tax Accountant Act also expressly **includes electronic or magnetic records**. **Paper or cloud data, the line is drawn the same way.** The software's feature exists so you can do your own company's work yourself; the licensing line for having someone do it for you runs outside the screen.

freee's official help (updated 21 July 2026, viewed 14 August 2026) also divides what each plan can do.

| Plan | What the year-end adjustment feature covers |
|---|---|
| Year-End Adjustment Paperless plan (年末調整ペーパーレスプラン) | **Collecting employee information and exporting CSV only.** It cannot calculate refunds and additional collections; the collected data is meant to be imported into payroll software or **shared with a professional** for the remaining work |
| Minimum plan (ミニマムプラン) | The administrator enters the data; **calculation and reflection of refunds/additional collections** are covered. No electronic filing — paper submission |
| Starter plan (スタータープラン, formerly Basic) and above | Employee self-entry, calculation, and **electronic filing** (advance setup in e-Tax and eLTAX required) |

freee's features and plan structure change. The table reflects materials published as of August 2026; check the official help pages for the current scope.

## Heading into December, who decides what?

Start the year-end adjustment in December and you will be late. There are three decisions.

| Decision | Options |
|---|---|
| **Who calculates and finalises** | Complete it in-house in freee / engage a tax accountant |
| **How the documents are submitted** | Electronic filing (plan and e-Tax/eLTAX setup permitting) / on paper |
| **The employee-facing schedule** | When to request input (November is the benchmark), My Number administrator settings, how to collect insurance premium deduction certificates |

If you have a retained tax accountant, agree **by November** on how far you enter data in freee yourselves and where you hand over. If you have none and in-house completion looks difficult, deciding to engage a tax accountant comes first.

## How far can a shakai hoken roumushi take it?

Around the year-end adjustment sit tasks that can be handed to a shakai hoken roumushi. Drawing the line:

| Task | Whom to engage |
|---|---|
| Monthly payroll and bonus calculation (including withholding) | A shakai hoken roumushi can take this |
| Social and labour insurance procedures, the annual calculation report and annual renewal | A shakai hoken roumushi can take this |
| The year-end adjustment's **tax calculation and finalisation** | **A tax accountant** |
| Preparing and filing the withholding certificates, statutory report summary and salary payment reports **on your behalf** | **A tax accountant** |

In other words, "payroll to the shakai hoken roumushi, year-end adjustment to the tax accountant" — **separate contracts** — is the shape that follows the licensing lines. Since a year of accurate payroll data is the precondition for the year-end adjustment, keeping monthly payroll in order makes December lighter.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q. Can the shakai hoken roumushi who runs our payroll also do our year-end adjustment?**
A. No. The year-end adjustment is a tax procedure, and performing it for another party for a fee is the work of a tax accountant (Certified Public Tax Accountant Act, Article 2, paragraph 1). This office does not handle it either; we point you to a tax accountant, whom you engage directly. No referral fees change hands.

**Q. Do freee's plans differ in what the year-end adjustment feature can do?**
A. Yes. According to the official help (viewed 14 August 2026), the Year-End Adjustment Paperless plan covers information collection and CSV export only; the Minimum plan calculates but cannot file electronically; the Starter plan and above can file electronically. Features change — check the official help for the latest.

**Q. Do we need any licence to complete the year-end adjustment in-house?**
A. No. Doing your own company's procedures yourselves sits outside the licensing lines. To file electronically you need a qualifying plan and advance setup in e-Tax and eLTAX.

**Q. Doesn't splitting "payroll to the consultant, year-end adjustment to the tax accountant" double the work?**
A. The contracts split; the work is continuous. If monthly payroll data accumulates accurately, the year-end side becomes lighter. Deciding by November who holds what is the practical way to avoid duplication.
