**Key point:** An overseas resident’s location alone does not determine Japanese labor or social insurance coverage. Check the business affiliation, supervision and employment relationship separately for each system, together with local requirements in China or Taiwan.

This article helps Japanese employers and HR staff organize the facts and consultation routes before directly hiring someone who will work remotely abroad without coming to Japan. **Information reviewed and content updated: October 10, 2026.** Residence abroad alone does not make the arrangement a local hire or an overseas posting.

## What determines how an overseas remote arrangement is classified?

Identify the contracting employer, the business to which the worker belongs, who bears the wages, day-to-day supervision, and the place and duration of work. A direct contract with a Japanese company does not by itself settle every insurance question.

| Arrangement | Facts to check | Insurance issue |
|---|---|---|
| Overseas business trip | Whether affiliation with and direction by the domestic business continue | Distinguish a trip from a posting on the facts |
| Overseas posting or secondment | Whether a domestic employer sends the worker to an overseas business | Check special enrollment and social security agreement conditions |
| Direct remote employment of an overseas resident | Business affiliation, supervision, employment relationship and any local establishment | Do not label all cases covered or excluded |

The MHLW guide distinguishes business trips and postings using business affiliation and direction, and excludes locally hired workers from special enrollment for overseas postings. It does not establish that every direct overseas remote hire is a local hire. See [workers’ accident insurance for trips and postings](/en/labor/column/kaigai-shucho-haken-rosai-chigai).

## How should Japanese and local labor law be checked?

The territorial scope of the Labour Standards Act (労働基準法) and the contract’s governing law or mandatory rules of another country are separate questions. Neither choosing Japanese law nor working abroad removes the need to check the relevant rules. This article has **not verified** the specific administrative interpretation for a person directly working remotely abroad while affiliated with a domestic Japanese business.

Before contracting, document the country of work, business affiliation, supervision, working conditions and any local establishment. Ask the competent Japanese offices about Japanese systems. Retain an attorney for legal questions about governing law and disputes, and local professionals for Chinese or Taiwanese law.

## What should be checked for each insurance system?

| System | Facts to check | Consultation route |
|---|---|---|
| Health insurance and employees’ pension | Employment relationship with a Japanese covered workplace, work and remuneration, and the separate rules for employees and dependants | Pension office, Japan Pension Service and health insurer |
| Employment insurance | Relationship with the domestic covered business, affiliation, supervision and overseas-work rules | Hello Work and Labour Bureau |
| Workers’ accident compensation insurance | Domestic or overseas business affiliation, facts of a trip or posting, and special-enrollment conditions | Labour Standards Inspection Office and Labour Bureau |

For this direct remote arrangement, health/pension eligibility and the relevant employment-insurance manual provisions remain **unverified**. General special-enrollment guidance does not guarantee domestic accident coverage or a benefit in an individual case. Any private insurance should be assessed separately from public-system eligibility.

Social security agreements generally use coverage in the country of work, with exceptions such as temporary postings to prevent double coverage. Some agreements also totalize coverage periods. The Japan–China agreement took effect on September 1, 2019, covers pensions and does not totalize periods. There is no agreement with Taiwan. Whether a certificate can be used for direct remote work depends on the agreement and the work arrangement. [The China agreement](/en/labor/column/chugoku-chuzai-shakaihosho-kyotei-nijukanyu) and [employment or secondment from Taiwan](/en/labor/column/taiwan-koyo-shukko-shakaihoken-kyotei-nashi) discuss other arrangements.

## Who should organize contracts, working hours and tax?

- Set out working hours, time differences, availability, breaks, days off and overtime approval in contracts and internal rules. [Domestic telework hours and costs](/en/labor/column/telework-zaitaku-roudou-jikan-hiyou-shugyokisoku) is a reference for operations, not a guarantee that Japanese rules apply abroad.
- Ask a tax accountant about currency, payment dates, withholding, non-resident taxation and permanent establishments (PE).
- Ask an attorney and local professionals about governing law, mandatory local rules and disputes.
- Work wholly abroad normally does not raise Japanese entry or residence procedures. If a foreign national later comes to Japan to work, ask a Gyoseishoshi or other appropriate professional about nationality, existing residence status and planned activities.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q. Does directly hiring someone in China or Taiwan exclude Japanese labor law?**
A. Residence alone does not settle this. Check business affiliation, supervision, the contract and actual work. The administrative interpretation for this direct remote arrangement is unverified here; attorneys and local professionals should assess governing law and mandatory rules.

**Q. Are all overseas remote hires excluded from Japanese employment and accident insurance?**
A. Coverage or exclusion cannot be stated uniformly. Organize affiliation with the domestic or overseas business, supervision and work facts, then inquire with Hello Work, the Labour Bureau and the Labour Standards Inspection Office. Do not apply the general special-enrollment exclusion for local hires to every remote arrangement.

**Q. Can the person join Japanese health insurance and employees’ pension?**
A. Ask the pension office and insurer about the employment relationship with the Japanese covered workplace, work and remuneration. Eligibility for this arrangement has not been verified in this article. Employee eligibility and dependant conditions must also be distinguished.

**Q. Is Japanese residence status unnecessary if the person never comes to Japan?**
A. Work wholly abroad normally does not raise Japanese entry or residence procedures. If a foreign national later works in Japan, nationality, existing status and planned activities affect the answer. Consult a Gyoseishoshi or another appropriate professional.
