**In short:** freee HR (freee人事労務) prepares the documents and can file them electronically through Mynaportal or e-Gov. **As long as your company files its own procedures for itself, no licence is required.** A shakai hoken roumushi (Certified Social Insurance and Labor Consultant) becomes necessary when someone **prepares or files them on another person's behalf, for a fee, at that person's request** (Certified Social Insurance and Labor Consultant Act, Article 27).

This page is for owners and back-office staff of small and medium-sized companies that have adopted freee HR, or are thinking about it. The software and the consultant are not substitutes for each other; the line between them simply runs in a different place. We trace that line along the statute.

## How far can freee HR alone take you?

According to freee's official help pages (updated 10 April 2026, viewed 14 August 2026), electronic filing from freee HR runs through **two routes — Mynaportal and e-Gov** — and requires a **gBizID Prime account** (corporate accounts only; the free tier is not covered).

| Route | Documents that can be filed electronically (examples) |
|---|---|
| Mynaportal | The monthly standard remuneration revision notification (月額変更届), the annual standard remuneration calculation report (算定基礎届), the bonus payment notification (賞与支払届), acquisition and loss of insured status notifications for health insurance and employees' pension (資格取得届・喪失届), employment insurance acquisition and loss notifications (with or without the separation certificate), and application forms for childcare-leave benefits and continued-employment benefits for older workers, among others |
| e-Gov | The annual renewal of labour insurance (年度更新), the periodic health checkup results report, and the stress-check results report |

In other words, filing your own company's procedures for yourself — the social and employment insurance filings that come with hiring and departures, the annual calculation report, the annual renewal — **is something the software can do**.

Two cautions. First, freee's features and the list of supported documents change. The table above reflects **published materials as of August 2026**; check the official help pages for the current scope. Second, the same help page states plainly: **"agent filing by a shakai hoken roumushi is not currently supported."** Electronic filing in freee HR is designed as **a tool for an employer to file its own procedures itself**.

## If the software exists, why would we need a shakai hoken roumushi?

The Certified Social Insurance and Labor Consultant Act defines three types of work concerning labour and social insurance procedures (Article 2, paragraph 1).

| Item | Work | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Item 1 | **Preparing** applications and other documents | Drawing up the applications, notifications and reports submitted to public agencies |
| Item 1-2 | **Filing on another's behalf** | Carrying out the submission procedures for those documents in another's place |
| Item 1-3 | **Representation in procedures** | Acting as agent in applications, and in statements and assertions made to the agency in investigations and dispositions |

There is a phrase here that is easy to miss. The statutory definition of "applications and other documents" in item 1 expressly **includes electronic or magnetic records (電磁的記録)** created in place of paper documents. That is: not only the paper notification, but **the data you build in cloud software and file electronically falls squarely within the statute**.

Article 27 then draws the line.

> No person other than a shakai hoken roumushi or a shakai hoken roumushi corporation may, **at the request of another person and for a fee**, carry out **as a business** the affairs listed in Article 2, paragraph 1, items 1 through 2. (Certified Social Insurance and Labor Consultant Act, Article 27)

Read in reverse: **a company handling its own procedures for itself is outside this provision.** You need no licence to prepare and file your own annual calculation report in freee. The licence is needed when someone does it **for another company, for a fee**. What the software removes is the labour of filing your own paperwork — not the legal character of having someone do it for you.

Article 27 has a proviso: work performed incidentally to business designated by cabinet order is exempt. The order (Order for Enforcement of the Certified Social Insurance and Labor Consultant Act, Article 2) designates two cases — **work performed by a certified public accountant as part of CPA practice, and work performed by a tax accountant or tax accountant corporation incidentally to tax practice**. Where exactly "incidental" ends is a case-by-case judgement, so this article goes no further than presenting the provision.

## Accounting and labour: who do you ask for which?

In a company running freee, accounting and HR sit side by side on the same screen, so the question "can't we just ask one person to handle everything?" arises naturally. But the licensing lines are drawn somewhere other than the software's menus.

| Software / situation | Doing it yourself | Whom to engage, for a fee, to do it for you |
|---|---|---|
| freee Accounting (bookkeeping, closing, tax returns) | No licence needed | **A tax accountant** |
| freee HR (social and labour insurance procedures) | No licence needed | **A shakai hoken roumushi** |
| freee HR's year-end adjustment feature | No licence needed | **A tax accountant** (the year-end adjustment is tax work) |
| Licences and permits after freee Company Formation | No licence needed | **A gyoseishoshi** (Certified Administrative Procedures Legal Specialist) |

In Yotsuba's case, labour and social insurance procedures are handled by 四葉社会保険労務士事務所, and permits, residence status and subsidies by 四葉行政書士事務所. **They are independent businesses, with separate contracts and separate invoices.** For tax we point you to a tax accountant, whom you engage directly; **no referral fees change hands.**

## What exactly is the retainer paying for, then?

Once the software takes over the typing and the filing, the consultant's work shifts away from "writing documents" to **what comes before and after them**.

- Whether the procedure is **needed at all, and by when** (a judgement arises with every hire, departure and leave)
- Whether the **requirements for a benefit are met**, and which benefits are available
- Handling **investigations and inquiries** from the agencies (the representation in item 1-3 is the work for this situation)
- The **labour management underneath the procedures** — working hours, wages, work rules

For a company that has adopted the software, the retained consultant looks less like an outsourced typist and more like the place you take judgement calls. At Yotsuba we have carried this thinking into the fees themselves: **support for building a setup to process payroll and other monthly work in-house on freee is included in the retainer fee** ([what the in-house option involves](/en/labor/column/kyuyo-keisan-freee-naisei)). ## Frequently asked questions

**Q. If freee can file electronically, is there any point in engaging a shakai hoken roumushi?**
A. The work of filing your own procedures can be done by the software. What you engage a shakai hoken roumushi for is the **judgement** — whether a procedure is needed, by when, and which benefits apply — and the **preparation, agent filing and procedural representation** that only a licence-holder may perform for a fee (Certified Social Insurance and Labor Consultant Act, Article 2, paragraph 1, items 1 through 1-3). The work and the judgement are different things. At Yotsuba, support for building the setup to process the work in-house on freee is itself included in the retainer fee.

**Q. What does electronic filing from freee require?**
A. According to the official help pages (viewed 14 August 2026), a gBizID Prime account, and corporate accounts only. The routes divide into Mynaportal and e-Gov. Features and supported documents change, so check freee's official help for the current scope.

**Q. Will a retained shakai hoken roumushi file as our agent from inside freee?**
A. freee's official help states that "agent filing by a shakai hoken roumushi is not currently supported" (updated 10 April 2026). Agent filings are made through the consultant's own systems. You can stay on freee HR while engaging us — no need to switch software.

**Q. Can we also ask a shakai hoken roumushi to do the year-end adjustment?**
A. freee HR has a year-end adjustment feature, but performing it for another person for a fee is tax work — the province of **a tax accountant**. This office does not handle it; we point you to a tax accountant, whom you engage directly. No referral fees change hands.
