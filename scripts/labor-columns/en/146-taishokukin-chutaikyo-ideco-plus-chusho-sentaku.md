**In short:** Compare contributions, eligibility, and the treatment of leavers before choosing Chutaikyo, iDeCo+, or corporate DC. A Shakai Hoken Roumushi handles labor matters; a tax accountant handles tax; financial institutions handle investment products under separate engagements.

This guide is for SME owners and HR staff setting up or reviewing retirement benefits. **System check and content update: October 10, 2026.** Comparing schemes does not determine which investment product suits an individual.

## How do the three schemes differ?

All three accumulate funds outside the company, but their eligibility and payment arrangements differ.

| Scheme and statute | Contributions | Investment and payment |
|---|---|---|
| Chutaikyo: 中小企業退職金共済法 (SME Retirement Allowance Mutual Aid Act), Article 10 and others | Entirely employer-funded | The Organization for Workers' Retirement Allowance Mutual Aid manages the funds and pays the leaver directly |
| iDeCo+: 確定拠出年金法 (Defined Contribution Pension Act), Article 68-2 | Employer adds to the member's own iDeCo contribution | Member manages investments; benefits generally begin at age 60 or later |
| Corporate DC: the same Act, Article 3 | Employer contributes; member contributions may be allowed under the plan and applicable conditions | Member manages investments; benefits generally begin at age 60 or later |

Chutaikyo is for eligible SMEs. Either the regular-employee threshold or the capital threshold must be met: general industries, at most 300 employees or JPY 300 million; wholesale, 100 or JPY 100 million; services, 100 or JPY 50 million; retail, 50 or JPY 50 million.

iDeCo+ is for employers with **no corporate DC, defined benefit corporate pension, or employees' pension fund, and no more than 300 Category-1 employees' pension insured persons across all establishments combined**. This is not a simple total-employee count. The member must contribute to iDeCo and consent to the employer contribution; employers cannot force employees to join. The threshold expanded from 100 to 300 in October 2020.

For corporate DC, the employer prepares a plan, obtains consent from the majority labor union or, if none, the majority representative, and seeks approval from the Minister of Health, Labour and Welfare. Check the agreement, plan, and administrator's arrangements before implementation.

## How do contributions and treatment of early leavers differ?

| Item | Chutaikyo | iDeCo+ | Corporate DC |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly contribution | JPY 5,000–30,000 in 16 bands; special JPY 2,000, 3,000, or 4,000 bands for eligible part-time workers | Member and employer combined: JPY 5,000–23,000 as of the check date | Confirm the limit under the plan and any other pension arrangements |
| Government subsidy | New enrollment and contribution increases may qualify | None | None |
| On leaving | No payment below 12 contribution months; below total contributions at 12–23 months | Transfer pension assets through the required procedure | Generally transferred; check employer-contribution refund rules for service under three years |

Chutaikyo pays **nothing below 12 contribution months; less than total contributions at 12–23 months; the contribution equivalent at 24–42 months; and more than that equivalent from 43 months**. The basic allowance reflects an investment-interest equivalent. A separate additional allowance depends on investment income and other factors and is not guaranteed every year.

For eligible new enrollment, the subsidy starts in the fourth month and covers half of the monthly contribution for one year, capped at JPY 5,000 per employee. An eligible increase from a contribution of JPY 18,000 or less may receive one-third of the increase for one year from the increase month. Check exclusions and special part-time arrangements with MHLW and the mutual aid organization.

Defined contribution pensions generally cannot be withdrawn before age 60, and leaving or changing jobs requires transfer procedures. However, a corporate DC plan may provide for **all or part of the assets attributable to employer contributions to be returned to the employer when service is under three years**. Do not confuse this with the member's own contributions. Confirm the plan and individual circumstances to determine any refund.

The iDeCo+ range of JPY 5,000–23,000 reflects official guidance at the check date. Reforms to iDeCo and related systems are scheduled for December 1, 2026. This article has not verified the resulting iDeCo+ limits or filing practice; check updated official guidance when implementing a scheme.

## What belongs in the work rules and retirement benefit rules?

労働基準法 (Labor Standards Act), Article 89, item 3-2 requires rules covering the employees eligible for a retirement allowance, how it is determined, calculated and paid, and when it is paid. Establishments regularly employing ten or more workers must prepare and file work rules. Ensure the external plan and the company's retirement rules agree.

- Define eligible employees, service and employment-status conditions, and contributions.
- Check payment, transfer and refund conditions against the plan.
- Arrange worker consent, internal communication and filing in order.

See [work rules for establishments with ten or more workers](/en/labor/column/shugyokisoku-10nin-gimu-nani-ga-hitsuyo). Disciplinary reductions or nonpayment raise plan restrictions and dispute issues; do not decide their validity from this article alone.

## Where do applications go, and which professional handles what?

Chutaikyo applications go through financial institutions and other authorized channels to the mutual aid organization; iDeCo+ filings go to the National Pension Fund Association; corporate DC requires ministerial plan approval. Confirm union or other required worker consent and the individual member's consent for iDeCo+, and majority union or representative consent for corporate DC.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q. Must a company create a retirement benefit scheme?**
A. Creating one is not itself a statutory duty. If one is provided, the rules must state eligibility, determination, calculation and payment methods, and payment timing. Establishments with ten or more regular workers must prepare and file work rules.

**Q. Does Chutaikyo pay someone who leaves within a year?**
A. It pays nothing below 12 contribution months; less than contributions at 12–23 months; the contribution equivalent at 24–42 months; and more from 43 months. An additional allowance is not universally guaranteed. Confirm an individual's amount with the organization.

**Q. Does iDeCo+'s 300-person limit mean all employees?**
A. It means Category-1 employees' pension insured persons across all establishments combined. No corporate DC, defined benefit corporate pension or employees' pension fund may be operated, and the member must contribute to iDeCo and consent. Corporate DC is a separate employer-approved plan.

**Q. Can an early leaver transfer the full corporate DC balance?**
A. Not always. For service under three years, plan rules may return all or part of employer-contribution assets to the employer. Distinguish member contributions, check the rules, and complete the transfer procedure.
