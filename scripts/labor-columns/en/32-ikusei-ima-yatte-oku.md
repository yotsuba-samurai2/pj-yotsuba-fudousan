**In short:** the system takes effect in April 2027, but **the procedures have already begun**. Pre-enforcement applications for **supervising support organisation (監理支援機関) licences are being accepted now**, and pre-enforcement applications for **training-employment plan certification open on September 1, 2026**. The technical intern training side has deadlines too: new supervising organisation licences **by September 30, 2026**, and No. 1 technical intern training plan applications **by February 2027**.

This page is for owners and back-office staff of companies hosting technical intern trainees, or preparing to receive workers under the Employment for Skill Development (育成就労) system. Not a system explainer — **only "by when, and what"**, from the OTIT's published pages (checked 14 August 2026).

## It starts next April — why move now?

**Because pre-enforcement application machinery is already running.** Wait for enforcement and you will not be ready on day one. And on the technical intern training side, **the windows for new applications are closing on a published schedule**. Companies thinking "we'll stay on technical intern training until the new system starts" are exactly the ones who need both calendars side by side.

## By when, and what?

All items below are stated on the OTIT's pages (checked 14 August 2026). **The pages are updated frequently — reconfirm on the latest page before acting.**

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **Now accepting** | Pre-enforcement applications for **supervising support organisation licences** (full document set and forms published) |
| **From September 1, 2026** | Pre-enforcement applications for **training-employment plan certification** ("send so as to arrive on or after September 1") |
| **By September 30, 2026** | Applications for new **supervising organisation** licences under the **technical intern training** system |
| **By February 2027** | Applications for certification of **No. 1 technical intern training plans** under the **technical intern training** system |
| **April 1, 2027** | The Employment for Skill Development system takes effect (planned) |

Two cautions. First, **"September 30" is the deadline for new supervising organisation licences under technical intern training** — not a deadline for the new system's applications. Much circulating material confuses the two. Second, **the mailing destinations differ**: the licence application goes to the head-office examination division **annex** (a different address from the head office), while plan certification goes to the **regional offices and branches** (not the head office). The OTIT flags both itself. A dedicated call centre for pre-enforcement applications is open (0570-011-300, weekdays 9:00–17:00).

## What does the receiving company prepare?

The list of application forms tells you, in reverse, what will be asked.

| Form | What to prepare |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | **The employment contract and conditions document** — rebuild the contract templates |
| 1-13 | The **statement of important matters on treatment** during the period |
| 1-15 / 1-39 | Statements of **remuneration, accommodation and amounts collected** (1-39 adds relocation costs) — [what goes into the treatment sections](/en/labor/column/ikusei-shuro-keikaku-taigu-kakikata) |
| 1-11 | Acceptance letters for the **training-employment supervisor, instructor and life advisor** — three roles to fill |
| 1-35 | The declaration on **target Japanese-language ability** — decide the learning arrangements |
| 1-16 to 1-22 | **The seven transfer-related forms** — [insurance and notifications on transfer](/en/labor/column/ikusei-shuro-tenseki-shakaihoken-koyohoken) |
| 1-36 | The excellent-standards declaration — decide whether to pursue it |

Seven forms for transfers alone — **the number of forms itself shows that transfer sits at the centre of the system**. The overall picture is in [What changes for labour administration between technical intern training and the new system?](/en/labor/column/ginou-jisshu-ikusei-shuro-roumu-chigai); the work-rules side in [How do you revise the work rules before receiving workers?](/en/labor/column/ikusei-shuro-shugyokisoku-minaoshi).

## Frequently asked questions

**Q. Why are applications open before the system takes effect?**
A. Because a pre-enforcement application mechanism exists. Licence applications are being accepted now, and plan-certification applications open on September 1, 2026 (OTIT, checked 14 August 2026). To use the system from day one (April 1, 2027, planned), the permissions must be progressed beforehand.

**Q. We heard "apply by September 30" — is that the new system's deadline?**
A. No. September 30, 2026 is the date the OTIT gives for new supervising organisation licence applications under the technical intern training system. It is not a recommended deadline for the new system's applications. The two are widely confused — check the OTIT's own pages.

**Q. We are still hosting technical intern trainees. Does this concern us?**
A. Yes. No. 1 technical intern training plan applications run until February 2027; after that, new intake moves to the new system. Companies currently hosting trainees are precisely the ones that need the transition calendar early. See [the comparison article](/en/labor/column/ginou-jisshu-ikusei-shuro-roumu-chigai).

**Q. By when must the external auditor be decided?**
A. The document lists include the auditor's acceptance letter (reference form 2-5 for the licence), so the auditor must be in place when the application is filed. "Find one after the licence is granted" is not an available order of events. For choosing whom to appoint, see [the external auditor page](/en/legal/services/ikuseishuro-gaibu-kansa) (四葉行政書士事務所, a separate business).
